# The Witness Clause

## Hook

In the court of Ostrevel, a truth-scribe's sworn record becomes law, and every oath she swears
costs her a word of her mother tongue. Issa Varro has been assigned to record the treason trial
of the border-lord accused of burning his own villages. By ancient clause, the scribe and the
accused are bound within a hundred paces of each other until the verdict. He swears he's
innocent. Her records say otherwise, and the handwriting on them is hers.

## Leads

- **Issa Varro, 31.** Senior truth-scribe of the Tribunal. *Wants* the post of High Recorder,
  the one office no noble can overrule. *Needs* to be known as more than an instrument.
  *False belief:* "The record is truer than any person, me included." *Habit that contradicts
  it:* she keeps a private diary in the dying mother tongue she is paying away, oath by oath.
- **Lord Corvin Hale, 36.** Warden of the Ashmarch border, accused of burning three villages to
  starve a rebellion. *Wants* to live long enough to expose who really gave the order. *Needs* to
  stop believing he must carry every cost alone. *False belief:* "Anyone I let close becomes a
  target." He has a goal of his own: get evidence to his surviving villagers before the
  Tribunal's agents find them.

## World rule: Oath-Law

1. **Can:** a sworn statement written by a truth-scribe becomes binding fact in law.
2. **Never:** a sworn record can't be retracted, only answered by a new oath.
3. **Cost:** each oath erases one word of the scribe's mother tongue from her mind forever.
4. **Who benefits:** the Crown, which needs undeniable records.
5. **Who is excluded:** the borderlands, whose people speak the language scribes pay away.
6. **Lovers:** any declaration a scribe *swears* becomes a contract. She can never say "I love
   you" casually.
7. **Dangerous intimate act:** promising.
8. **Recurring dilemma:** record the truth that convicts someone she cares about, or lie and
   lose her voice as a scribe.
9. **Loophole:** oaths sworn in the dead border tongue aren't recorded by the Crown.
10. **Cost of the loophole:** she must use the very words she's losing, so every hidden oath
    spends her mother tongue twice as fast.

## Book-by-book

- **Book 1, *The Witness Clause*.** Will Issa record the truth about the village fires, knowing
  it will hang the man she has started to trust, or the man who framed him? The midpoint reveals
  that the forged record is in Issa's hand: she swore it years ago, and someone erased her memory
  of doing so. Ending: she swears the oath that frees Corvin, at the cost of the last word she
  had for "mother". Hinge: the erased memories were taken by the Crown's own Archive, which keeps
  them.
- **Book 2, *The Unwritten Archive*.** Can they recover the stolen memories without breaking the
  Tribunal that keeps the realm from war? Corvin is now a free man with a price on his name, and
  Issa is a scribe who can't trust her own records.
- **Book 3, *The Last Word*.** Will Issa abolish Oath-Law, knowing it will also unmake every oath
  that bound them together? Theme answered: a love that doesn't need to be sworn.

Structure: one couple across a trilogy.

## Why readers will want it

- **Being seen accurately:** he is the first person to notice what each oath costs her.
- **Choosing one's fate:** she gives up the post she wanted.
- **Moral intensity:** truth against mercy, with neither side a strawman.
- **Dramatic irony:** the reader learns before Issa that the forgery is in her hand.
- **Tropes:** enemies to lovers (rooted in values), forced proximity (the hundred-pace binding),
  forbidden (scribe and accused).

## Originality scorecard

| Test | Pass | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Swap the magic | Yes | Without Oath-Law there's no binding, no cost to promising and no forgery |
| 2. Theme as a two-sided question | Yes | Is truth worth more than mercy? |
| 3. Love interest's own goal | Yes | Getting evidence to his surviving villagers |
| 4. Not solved by killing a villain | Yes | The problem is the institution of the record itself |
| 5. Heroine changes a belief | Yes | From "the record is truer than people" to trusting testimony over the record |
| 6. One convention inverted, one honoured | Yes | Inverted: the heroine holds the power over the accused man. Honoured: slow burn to HEA |
| 7. Describable without comps | Yes | "A court scribe whose oaths become law falls for the lord on trial" |
| 8. Intimate act made political | Yes | Promising is a legal act |

## Risks

- Courtroom scenes can slow the pace. Every hearing needs a reversal.
- Readers may want Corvin to be more dangerous. Show his competence in the border-raid scenes.
