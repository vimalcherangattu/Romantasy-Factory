# Vow of Teeth

## First look

- **Title:** Vow of Teeth
- **Alternatives:** The Night Bride; A Bride with Claws
- **Series:** The Odiyan Contracts (3 books)
- **Hook line:** A shapeshifting assassin must kill the lord she was married off to, but he's the one man who has seen her night-shape, and whoever names it can trap her in it forever.
- **Tropes:** arranged marriage, enemies to lovers, secret mission, morally grey love interest, slow burn
- **Comps (shelf only):** The Bridge Kingdom; The Serpent and the Wings of Night
- **Cover concept:** A woman's hand in a gold wedding bangle lies flat on a man's bare chest, the nails turning to leopard claws, against black and turmeric-gold.

## Leads

**Neela, 27, odiyan assassin and contract bride.**
- Want: to finish the contract on Devan and earn her name back from the guild ledger.
- Need: to be known by someone and not owned by them.
- False belief: "Anyone who truly sees me will put a leash on me."

**Devan, 32, warden of Kettamala hill fort and the queen's border commander.**
- Want: to find which councillor is selling the border passes to the coastal raiders.
- Need: to let someone guard *his* back; he trusts no one since his sister was killed under contract.
- False belief: "Power is only safe in my hands if I never use it."

## World rule

1. **Can:** odiyans take one animal shape at night with the guild's bark resin.
2. **Never:** choose the shape, or change by day.
3. **Cost:** if a witness speaks the shape aloud before dawn, the odiyan is locked in it for life.
4. **Who benefits:** the guild masters, who hold every member's witnessed name in a ledger.
5. **Who's excluded:** anyone without a guild master, as rogue odiyans are hunted.
6. **Lovers:** sharing a bed means being seen; every lover is a potential cage.
7. **Dangerous intimate act:** sleeping beside someone, letting them watch the change.
8. **Recurring dilemma:** reveal herself and hand him the key, or hide and keep lying.
9. **Loophole:** an odiyan who speaks her own night-name to a chosen person turns the witness bond mutual; no third party can cage her.
10. **Loophole cost:** the pair feel each other's wounds, and the guild's ledger entry burns, marking her as rogue.

## Folklore and respect notes

- **Drawn from:** Malabar odiyan lore (night shapeshifting assassins), set in invented hill country with kalari-trained fighters and marma strikes.
- **Kept out:** the real lore's preparation from a human foetus is replaced by an invented bark resin.
- **Kept out:** the historical association of odiyan lore with specific oppressed communities. The guild is an invented order that recruits orphans of any birth, and caste is not coded onto it.
- **Kept out:** no deities. The kingdom (Varanad) and the fort are invented.
- **Non-English terms:** about eight (odiyan, kalari, marma, plus household words).

## Book-by-book

1. **Vow of Teeth.**
   - Question: will Neela kill the husband she was sold to?
   - Midpoint: Devan sees her change and stays silent, then offers a bargain: his silence for the name of his buyer.
   - Climax: she breaks the contract and her guild-sister dies covering her escape, a loss that stays lost.
   - Ending: HFN, married in truth.
   - Hinge: the contract was paid for with the queen's seal.
2. **Oath of Claws.**
   - Question: can they steal the guild's name-ledger without becoming its new masters?
   - Ending: HFN.
   - Hinge: the ledger holds names of odiyans in the queen's own guard.
3. **Crown of Night.**
   - Question: free every odiyan, and leave the kingdom without its night-guard as raiders land?
   - Ending: HEA, the guild remade as a sworn company that owns itself.

## Why readers will click

Assassin bride is a proven shelf. The leopard-claw cover and "trap her forever" give danger in one glance. A hero whose power is his silence offers "touch her and die" with restraint built in, and every bedroom scene is a trust test with a literal cost.

## Risks

- Overlap with the assassin-bride shelf: keep the witness rule on every page so it can't read as a reskin.
- His holding power over her must stay disciplined. He offers the bargain once, then gives it up by book 1's midpoint, and the story treats the threat as harm he acknowledges.
- The title "Night Bride" may collide; the market check should verify all three titles.
