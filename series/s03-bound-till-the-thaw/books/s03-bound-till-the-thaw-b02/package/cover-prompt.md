## Art prompt

Portrait book cover art, 1600 × 2560 pixels, no text, no letters, no figures. A single brass surveyor's compass hangs in tight, sharp focus from a length of madder-red hand-spun wool rope, slightly below the centre of the frame. Beneath it lies a map etched in frost on pale ice, with fine glittering contour lines and branching paths, a few of them faint and broken. The ground is a cold, wide mountain snowfield in snow white with warm-grey shadows. A jagged blue-white ridge line runs across the lower third. The upper third is a pale, clear winter sky, left empty for the title. The only warm colour in the image is the red rope. Cold, intimate and high-stakes, like breath held in frozen air. Photographic-realistic illustration with a shallow depth of field and soft glacier light. No faces, no hands, no people, no religious symbols, no prayer flags, no real shrines, no trademarked imagery.

## Motif

A brass surveyor's compass hanging from the red wool rope over a map etched in frost. This is the centre object for Book 2 of the series, and the same red rope ties it to the other three covers.

## Palette

- Snow white, with a warm-grey shadow
- Glacier blue
- Deep slate ink
- Wool red (madder), used only on the rope and the title's thin accent line

## Type spec

- Series line: "The Winter Passes · Book 2", clean geometric sans-serif, small size, white, above the title.
- Title: "THE WINTER OATH" in tall, condensed serif capitals with sharp chiselled wedge serifs, widely letter-spaced, snow white with a faint frost-crack texture. It sits in the top third over the pale sky, large enough to read at thumbnail size. Set on two lines if needed: THE WINTER / OATH.
- Accent: a thin madder-red horizontal rule directly under the title.
- Pen name: "MIRA SANGLA" in clean geometric sans-serif small capitals, very widely tracked, white, at the bottom edge over the dark slate foreground.
- Keep the vertical spacing identical to the other books in the series.
