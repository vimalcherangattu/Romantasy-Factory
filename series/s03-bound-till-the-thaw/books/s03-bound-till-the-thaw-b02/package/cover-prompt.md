## Art prompt
Portrait book cover art, 1600 x 2560 pixels, no text, no lettering, no figures, no faces. A single brass surveyor's compass in tight sharp focus, hanging from a thick hand-spun madder-red wool rope, slightly below the vertical centre of the frame. Beneath it lies an old map etched in white frost on dark slate-ink ground, fine contour lines and tiny peaks picked out in crystalline rime. The setting is a cold, wide mountain ground in glacier blue and snow white with warm-grey shadows. A jagged blue-white ridge line runs across the lower third. The top third is a pale, empty winter sky with room for the title. The red rope is the only warm colour in the image. Cold, intimate, high-stakes mood, like breath held in frozen air. Crisp macro detail on the brass and rope fibres, soft falling snow dust, shallow depth of field on the background ridge. No real people, no religious symbols, no prayer flags, no monasteries, no trademarked imagery.

## Motif
A brass surveyor's compass hanging from the red wool rope over a map etched in frost. This is the Book 2 object in a four-book set, and all four covers share the same red rope, the same ridge line in the lower third and the same vertical spacing.

## Palette
- Snow white, with a warm-grey shadow
- Glacier blue
- Deep slate ink
- Wool red (madder), used only on the rope and the title's accent line
