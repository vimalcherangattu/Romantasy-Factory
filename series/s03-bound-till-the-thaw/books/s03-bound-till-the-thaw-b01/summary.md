# Bound till the Thaw — summary

## Plot
**Act 1 (ch 1–3).** Meera (29), smuggler-guide of Kothi Shilak, packs twelve packets of crown-grade redbark for her brother Kiran's fever village, Ranu. The crate stamps were scraped off and she sews a brass drop-token into her ledger spine. Warden captain Rohan (35) arrives with a writ and hires old Jassi as guide. Meera insists on coming as his second. Rohan has secretly pocketed a grain of redbark and quietly paid the widow Puja's fine. His lieutenant Varun picks the gully route. In ch 3 a double crack (iron on rock) triggers an avalanche. Jassi dies holding the kid bell of his goat Badami. Rohan cracks two ribs shielding Meera. At the Shilak Cairn the mountain's jogini bind them by red rope for eight arm-spans until meltwater runs at the cairn. Lies cost body heat, silence is free, and promises bind.

**Act 2a (ch 4–15).** They agree terms as adversaries. Rohan carries six packets, which makes him a harbourer. Meera takes his lie-cold through skin contact (ch 6). They find a carving of two hands on a rope (ch 5) and shelter in the Rope-Hut (ch 8). Asha's crew and the regent's hunters pursue them. Rohan collapses with fever (ch 10). Meera finds the queen's lapis goat seal in his coat, spends half a pinch of redbark on him and retells him the cord in his double hitch. She writes "One captain. Price: none." (ch 11). They trade griefs: Dilan's cut rope, Rohan's two hangings including the boy Ketan, and his grandfather. They nearly kiss (ch 14). On the Goat's Teeth (ch 15) a hunter's purse holds the same crown redbark.

**Midpoint (ch 16).** In a storm, with seven sticks left, they confess: "I am the Salt Fox" and "My writ orders me to deliver the Salt Fox at the thaw." Together they deduce that Varun set the avalanche, sold the fallback route, and is Meera's middleman. Her silver has funded Pritam's coup. They kiss. Rohan promises "I will not let you fall on this mountain."

**Act 2b (ch 17–24).** In Ranu there are 31 graves. Meera and Nimmi triage eighteen courses for forty-one sick. Sunni, 7, dies (ch 18). The regent's patrol ash-marks sick houses "for cleansing" (ch 19). Meera and Rohan become lovers (ch 20), and Kiran's fever breaks. Kiran confesses to Meera that he was on Dilan's rope and that Dilan cut it himself to save him (ch 22). Meera keeps it from Rohan. A masked buyer in the walnut grove gives Meera a sack of the regent's redbark and warns her that Varun is coming (ch 24). Rohan gives her the seal to hide.

**Act 3 (ch 25–32).** Varun arrives with a warrant, chains Rohan and Meera, and names Kiran publicly (ch 25). Rohan asks if Kiran was on Dilan's rope. Meera says "No" and loses her left ring and little fingers (ch 26). The regent's men plan to burn Ranu. A grey-bearded officer orders the torches lowered (ch 27). Asha frees them for pages of Meera's ledger and the button evidence (ch 28). Kiran tells Rohan the truth. Rohan publicly declines to arrest him (ch 29). At the Snow Fair (ch 30) Rohan presses the seal in wax mixed with the queen's blood, revoking Pritam's claim. Meera enters her ledger and her confession into the crown record under her own name. Asha testifies, Varun is arrested, and Rohan admits he signed the blockade. Both declare love, with "Watch my hands." In ch 31 Nimmi amputates Meera's two fingers. They draft the Winter Crossing Law, and Meera accepts the Closing Roll for life. In ch 32 the bond ends, Rohan declines to deliver her, and they tie an ordinary rope as betrothal. The stones then speak the hinge.

## Romance
**Meera and Rohan** are betrothed by choice, with an undyed two-span rope tied at the cairn (ch 32). She now calls him Rohan, not Captain. Key moments are the lie-cold taken in ch 6, the seal refusal in ch 11, the first kiss and promise in ch 16, the refused kiss in ch 14, and lovers in ch 20. They exchange "I love you" with warm hands in ch 30. In ch 31 she asks "Stay," the first thing she has asked for herself.

**Disclosed:** each knows the other's two enemy-making facts (the Salt Fox and the delivery writ). She has told him about her ledger and her mother's death. He has told her about Ketan, Dilan and the blockade. Kiran's secret is out and repaired in ch 29. Rohan admitted he chose not to reach for her hand in ch 26.

**Promises:** Rohan's "I will not let you fall" bound him until the thaw. He avoids new promises and says "I intend" instead. He intends to petition the queen to strike the Salt Fox clause. The writ still reads "deliver the Salt Fox at the thaw." He says he is "walking to Taravan beside her, with the law in her hand."

## Character states
- **Meera:** Left ring and little fingers amputated; she wears a felted three-finger glove. She is publicly on record as the Salt Fox, on the Closing Roll for life and subject to winter service. Her belief has shifted to "a law is a rope; it depends who ties it." She no longer counts compulsively with Rohan. Her loss of Jassi and Sunni stays loss.
- **Rohan:** Ribs healing. Captain of the Shilak post, with the writ still in force. He has publicly admitted harbouring and signing the blockade. He keeps Dilan's cut rope and badge. His certainty in the law is broken. His sister is Ila.
- **Kiran:** Recovered, ear scarred. He was on Dilan's rope and is unpunished. He is quiet and was left at Garh Neshar with Ila.
- **Asha** and her crew (Lalit and others) turned against Varun and wait on the assembly's mercy. They are not pardoned. Asha knows the three-loop knot.
- **Varun** is in chains, bound for Taravan court. **Pritam** is imprisoned. **Queen Sarala** is ill and unseen. **Prince Kunal** was sympathetic. **Gopal** is the senior warden loyal to Rohan.
- **Nimmi** (healer), **Pinku**, **Bindu** and **Hakam** are alive. **Ila** stayed in Neshar to finish the pass map.

## World changes
- The Closing Law is replaced by an eleven-line Winter Crossing Law, drafted but awaiting the queen's hand in spring. It licenses bound guides under two seals, guarantees medicine always passes, and bars hanging for a death on a bound crossing.
- The regent's claim is revoked and his grain stores are under council signature.
- Ranu's blockade dead (32) are known and acknowledged in the assembly.
- The bond ended: the jogini spoke aloud to everyone at the cairn.

## Open threads
- **Old Bargain (hinge):** made at the cairn and never paid. The pass closes forever after the fourth winter unless someone pays.
- **C06:** The carved hands are unequal (a worker's and a careful one) and the rope ends cut or unpaid. The chronicle ("brothers in arms") and the crews' version (warden and smuggler) still conflict.
- **C12:** Asha's three-nested-loop knot matches the cairn carving. She silenced Meera: "Not here, not before him."
- **C14:** Rohan's grandfather died on the pass sixty winters ago, the spring before the law. His gloves were found laid neatly on a flat stone, which may mean a deliberate payment.
- **C17:** The masked buyer paid in Neshar silver with a clipped corner and gave nineteen coins. He uses regent-store redbark, has a courtly manner, knows the hall and was gentle with Pinku. His name is unknown and he has not reappeared.
- **C18:** The grey-bearded officer lowered the torches at Ranu. He laid his sword belt down at the Fair. His name is unknown and Nimmi memorised his face.
- **C19:** Queen Sarala's blood is in the seal wax. What this means about her and the Bargain is open.
- **C24:** The regent's general is officially exiled, but Ranu says otherwise. This probably links to C18.
- Rohan's writ still orders delivery of the Salt Fox, and the queen's ruling is pending.
- Ila's map: the queen "will want it very much."
- Meera fears Kiran is tied to the Bargain, since he was silent through the drafting.
