# Bound till the Thaw — summary

## Plot

**Act 1 (ch 1–8).** Meera (29), the Salt Fox who runs a Kothi Shilak smuggling crew, packs twelve packets of crown-grade redbark for her brother Kiran's fever village, Ranu (ch 1). Warden Captain Rohan (35) requisitions a guide for the closed Long Saddle, carrying the queen's lapis goat seal to the Snow Fair (twenty days) to block Regent Pritam's investiture (ch 2). His lieutenant Varun picks the gully route. An avalanche kills guide Jassi and the goats. Rohan, ribs cracked, shields Meera. Both are bound at the Shilak Cairn by a red rope: eight arm-spans' limit, lies cost body heat, promises bind, silence is free (ch 3–4). Rohan carries half the redbark, knowingly harbouring. Ch 5–8: the waystone carving, a lie that costs him, Meera taking his cold by touch (ch 6), Asha's crew trailing them, a snow-bridge fall (ch 7), and the Rope-Hut. Regent's hunters climb below (ch 8).

**Act 2a (ch 9–15).** Firewood rule: each refused question burns a stick. Rohan keeps Dilan's rope end, cut clean (ch 9). Asha offers a lordship and pardon for his coat, and her tracker Lalit says "three wardens" (ch 10). Rohan collapses with fever. Meera finds the seal, spends redbark on him and ties the cord back in his hitch (ch 11). She learns of the boy Ketan, hanged by Rohan (ch 12). Asha's crew guides the hunters up Rohan's secret fallback route (ch 13). Rohan's grandfather died on the pass. Meera refuses a kiss: "I don't kiss my noose" (ch 14). They fight hunters on the Goat's Teeth, and Meera triggers a slide (ch 15).

**Midpoint (ch 16).** In the storm, with seven sticks left, each confesses the fact that makes them enemies, and both stay warm. They deduce that Varun set the avalanche and is her middleman. Her silver funded Pritam's coup. First kiss. Rohan promises "I will not let you fall on this mountain."

**Act 2b (ch 17–24).** Descent to Ranu. Rohan counts 31 graves and tells Nimmi "Yes" to "you sign it?" (ch 17). Nimmi has eighteen courses for the forty-one sick. Meera chooses who gets one. Sunni (7) dies (ch 18–19). The regent's patrol ash-marks sick houses "for cleansing" and says a general will ride up after the Fair. Rohan admits his certainty is cracking. Rohan confesses he signed the blockade. They become lovers, and Kiran's fever breaks (ch 20). Rohan learns his sister Ila is trapped at Garh Neshar. Kiran flees when Rohan mentions Dilan's rope (ch 21). Kiran tells Meera that Dilan bound himself to him and cut his own rope. She keeps it secret (ch 22). Rohan decides to petition the queen to strike the Salt Fox clause (ch 23). The masked buyer pays in cut-corner silver and gives regent's-store redbark. He warns that Varun is coming. Rohan gives Meera the seal to hide (ch 24).

**Act 3 (ch 25–30).** Varun arrives with a warrant and exposes Kiran as Dilan's rope-mate. Rohan surrenders and is chained to Meera as "hired guide" (ch 25). In the lockhouse Rohan asks if Kiran was on the rope. Meera says "No" and loses two left fingers to lie-cold. Rohan chooses not to touch her hand (ch 26). Soldiers plan to burn Ranu. A grey-bearded officer orders the torches lowered (ch 27). Asha frees them for a torn page of the count and the brass button. The seal was hidden in Nimmi's tea pot. Kiran begins to confess (ch 28). Rohan hears it, knows Meera lied, declines to arrest Kiran, and they repair (ch 29). At the Snow Fair, Rohan presses the seal (wax mixed with the queen's blood). Meera writes her name as the Salt Fox into the crown's record. Asha and the button expose Varun. Rohan confesses the blockade. Pritam and Varun are taken. Rohan says "I love you. Watch my hands" (ch 30).

**Act 4 (ch 31–32).** Nimmi amputates Meera's two fingers. The Winter Crossing Law is drafted in eleven lines (licensed bound guides, medicine always passes, no one hanged for a death on a bound crossing). Meera takes the Closing Roll for life. At the thaw Rohan refuses to arrest her, and they retie with an ordinary rope. The cairn speaks: the Old Bargain is unpaid, and the pass closes forever after the fourth winter unless someone pays (ch 32).

## Romance

Meera and Rohan, betrothed by choice at the cairn. Moments: Rohan's lie and Meera taking his cold (ch 6), refused kiss (ch 14), first kiss and promise (ch 16), lovers (ch 20), "No" and his chosen not-reaching (ch 26), repair (ch 29), mutual "I love you", hands warm (ch 30), "Stay" (ch 31), name over "Captain" (ch 32).

Disclosed: Rohan knows she is the Salt Fox, knows of the lie and why, and knows Kiran's role. Meera knows of the seal, his mission, the blockade signatures, Ketan, and Dilan's rope. Rohan will not promise what he can't keep. His standing promise is "I will not let you fall on this mountain" (ch 16), and he has promised to carry every bucket (ch 19). Both are bound to the thaw writ. Meera is on the Closing Roll, which does not give her a pardon.

## Character states

- **Meera:** 29. Left ring and little fingers amputated, wears a felted glove. On the Closing Roll for life. Her name is in the crown record. Keeps the count. Fears Kiran will try to pay the Bargain.
- **Rohan:** 35. Captain of the Shilak post, ribs healing. His writ still orders him to deliver the Salt Fox. He gave up the post's veto over licences. Holds Dilan's rope end and badge.
- **Kiran:** Survived the fever, with a torn ear. Guilt-ridden. Heard the Bargain's warning.
- **Ila:** Rohan's sister, surveyor, stays at Garh Neshar finishing the map.
- **Asha:** Redeemed. Her name is out of Varun's books.
- **Nimmi:** Ranu's healer. Saw the grey-bearded officer's face.
- **Varun:** Jailed, bound for Taravan. **Pritam:** imprisoned. **Prince Kunal:** at the assembly. **Queen Sarala:** ill, alive.
- **Dead:** Jassi, Sunni, Dilan, and Ranu's 32 graves.

## World changes

- The Winter Crossing Law is drafted: licensed bound guides, medicine always passes, no hanging for a death on a bound crossing. The council's seal is second, and the post can't refuse a licence the council has sealed. Ila fixed the rope length to the guide's arm.
- The hill council controls Pritam's grain stores. Pritam's claim is revoked.
- The jogini now speak through the stones' cold.

## Open threads

- **C06:** The carved hands are unequal (broad, long and narrow), not "brothers in arms". Likely a warden and a smuggler.
- **C12:** Asha's three-loop knot matches the cairn carving. It was on the bound sentries, and Asha silenced Meera's question.
- **C14:** Rohan's grandfather died when the Closing Law was new. His gloves were folded on a flat stone, which suggests sacrifice.
- **C17:** The masked buyer pays in cut-corner Neshar silver from the regent's stores, and is unnamed. He gave Pinku a walnut tied in red thread.
- **C18:** The grey-bearded officer lowered the torches at Ranu and laid down his sword belt at the Fair. His identity is open.
- **C19:** The seal wax holds the queen's blood. What the queen knows of blood-magic and the Bargain is open.
- **C24:** The regent's general is officially exiled, but Ranu whispers otherwise. Probably the grey-bearded officer.
- **Hinge:** The Old Bargain is unpaid, and the pass closes forever when the fourth winter ends unless someone pays. Who pays?
- The queen's final judgement of Meera and the Salt Fox clause. Why the queen wants the Salt Fox alive and wants Ila's map.
- The three-and-tail knotted cord on Nimmi's latch (ch 23).
