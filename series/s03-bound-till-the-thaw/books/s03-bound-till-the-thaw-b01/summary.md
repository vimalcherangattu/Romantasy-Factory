# Bound till the Thaw — summary

## Plot

**Act 1 (ch 1–4).** Meera (29), the secret Salt Fox, packs twelve packets of crown-grade redbark for her brother Kiran's fever village, Ranu. Warden Captain Rohan (35) arrives at Kothi Shilak and hires the crew to cross the closed Long Saddle. He carries Queen Sarala's lapis goat seal, to be pressed at the Snow Fair (20 days) to revoke Regent Pritam's investiture. His writ also orders him to deliver the Salt Fox at the thaw. His lieutenant Varun picks the gully route (ch 2). In the gully an avalanche kills Jassi (ch 3). Meera takes Badami's kid bell. Rohan cracks two ribs shielding her. At the Shilak Cairn they tie the red rope: "Meera. Ranu." "Rohan. Garh Neshar." Rules of the bond: eight arm-spans of distance; lies cost body heat; promises bind; silence and truth are free. She can take his cold by touch (ch 6). Rohan carries half the redbark (ch 4).

**Act 2a (ch 5–15).** Waystone carving; Rohan's ribs lie (5–6). Asha's crew hunts them (7). The Rope-Hut: shared fire, with the juniper stick rule of one question a night (8–9). Rohan's rope end from Dilan is cut clean (9). Asha offers a pardon for "the coat" (10). Meera finds the seal during his fever, refuses to sell it and reties the cord in his hitch (11). Rohan learns she charges the rich double (12). The hunters use Varun's secret fallback route (13). Near-kiss refused: "I don't kiss my noose" (14). Fight on the Goat's Teeth; hunter's purse holds crown redbark (15).

**Midpoint (ch 16).** Storm night. Each confesses the thing that makes them enemies. They deduce that Varun set the avalanche and is her middleman. First kiss. Rohan promises "I will not let you fall on this mountain."

**Act 2b (ch 17–25).** Ranu: 31 graves (32 after Sunni). Rohan admits he signs the blockade (17). Triage: eighteen courses for forty-one sick. Sunni dies (18–19). Patrol marks houses with ash "for cleansing after the Fair" (19). They become lovers; Kiran's fever breaks (20). Kiran confesses that Dilan cut his own rope to save him (22). Rohan's sister Ila is trapped at Garh Neshar (21). A masked buyer gives redbark and warns of Varun (24). Rohan gives Meera the seal to hide. Varun arrests Rohan with a warrant and outs Kiran (25).

**Act 3 (ch 26–32).** Rohan asks "Was Kiran on Dilan's rope?" Meera says "No," and her left ring and little fingers die (26). Torches are lowered by a grey-bearded officer (27). Asha frees them for a page of the count and the brass button (28). Kiran confesses; Rohan declines to arrest him (29). At the Fair, Rohan presses the seal and the claim is revoked. Meera enters her name as the Salt Fox. Asha and the button expose Varun. Rohan admits he signed the blockade. They declare love (30). Law drafted; Nimmi amputates (31). At the thaw Rohan refuses to arrest her. Betrothal by a new rope. The stones speak the Old Bargain (32).

## Romance

- **Meera and Rohan are betrothed.** They tied an undyed rope of Bindu's spinning by choice. She calls him Rohan, not Captain.
- **Key moments:** her first free truth (3); he takes the cold with her hand (6); the near-kiss and "noose" (14); the first kiss and his promise (16); lovers (20); the "No" lie and his choice not to reach (26); the repair, "That's on my law, not your hand" (29); "Watch my hands" and mutual "I love you" (30); "Stay" (31).
- **Disclosed:** she is the Salt Fox, her 206 crossings, the double price, her mother's death and Kiran's role. He has disclosed the seal, the writ's order, the blockade, the two hangings (one the boy Ketan), Dilan and his doubt about the law.
- **Binding promises:** his bond not to let her fall (still unbroken). He refuses further promises, only "I intend." He has told her he will petition the queen to strike the Salt Fox clause. He said "I am looking" for a way to make the paper say no.
- **Strain:** she lied to him once and paid in fingers. Both accepted the harm and named it.

## Character states

- **Meera:** 29; left ring and little fingers amputated, wears a felted three-stall glove. Named in the crown's record as the Salt Fox, "held pending the queen's judgement." She has chosen the Closing Roll for life, though the roll's status is uncertain. Heroine belief changed: "It's a rope. It depends who ties it."
- **Rohan:** 35; ribs mending. He gave up the post's veto in the new law. He holds the unresolved writ ordering delivery of the Salt Fox, and publicly confessed the blockade. He has Dilan's rope end and the badge. He is not arresting Meera and intends to walk beside her to Taravan.
- **Kiran:** survived, weak, torn left ear. His part in Dilan's death is known to Meera, Rohan, Asha's crew and Nimmi. Rohan declined to arrest him. The new law's last clause protects deaths on bound crossings.
- **Asha:** 28, crew-leader. Turned on Varun and testified. She silenced Meera's question about the knot.
- **Varun:** in irons at Garh Neshar, to go to Taravan. Pritam is imprisoned. Ila is free and finishing her survey map. Nimmi has returned to Ranu. Bindu, Hakam, Roop, Chand and Gopal (senior warden) are at the cairn.
- **Dead:** Jassi, Sunni, Dilan.

## World changes

- Pritam's claim to Neshar is revoked, with his grain hoarding exposed. Prince Kunal and the hill council gained authority over the grain stores.
- The Winter Crossing Law draft (11 lines): licensed bound guides with post and council seals, council seal binding on the post; medicine always passes; no hanging or blame for a death on a bound crossing; the guide's arm is the unit of measure. It still needs the queen's hand.
- The Closing Law's blockade is publicly condemned.
- The stones now speak through palms: the pass will close forever after the fourth winter unless someone pays.
- The bond ended when meltwater ran at the cairn.

## Open threads

- **Hinge:** the Old Bargain is owed; the pass closes forever after the fourth winter unless someone pays. Who pays, and when?
- **C06:** carving of two hands on one rope. The hands are unequal (one broad, one long), so "brothers in arms" is false. Warden/smuggler origin unproven.
- **C12:** Asha's three-nested-loop knot matches the cairn carving. Asha said "Not here." Her link to the old crossing is withheld.
- **C14:** Rohan's grandfather died on the pass sixty winters ago. His gloves were folded neatly on a stone, a possible payment toward the Bargain.
- **C17:** the masked buyer, with Neshar silver stamped with a cut corner and the regent's own redbark, is never named or explained.
- **C18:** the grey-bearded officer lowered the torches and laid down his sword at the Fair. His name is unknown.
- **C19:** the seal wax is mixed with the queen's own blood. This suggests blood-binding tied to the crown and Bargain.
- **C24:** the regent's general is officially "exiled," but Ranu says the officer on the grey horse is he.
- Meera's status is "held pending the queen's judgement." Rohan's writ to deliver her is unresolved.
- Why the queen wanted the Salt Fox delivered rather than hanged is unanswered.
- Varun said he crossed in a jogini binding. How the spirits' bonds work in the lowlands is unexplored.
- The queen wants Ila's map, and the reason is unknown.
- Kiran may go looking for the debt. Whether anyone must pay with a rope end is unexplained.
