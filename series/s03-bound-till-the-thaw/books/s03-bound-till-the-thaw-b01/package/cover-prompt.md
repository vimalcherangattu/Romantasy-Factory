## Art prompt
Portrait book cover art, 1600 x 2560, cold and intimate, high stakes, like breath held in frozen air. One central object in tight focus: two gloved hands bound wrist to wrist by a hand-spun madder-red wool rope, dusted with fine snow, with the weave of the wool and the stitching of the dark gloves clearly visible. The hands sit slightly below the vertical centre of the frame, on a wide, cold mountain ground of wind-scoured snow with a warm-grey shadow. A jagged blue-white ridge line runs across the lower third. The upper third is a pale, empty winter sky left clear for the title. Soft glacier-blue light, shallow depth of field, crisp frost texture. The red rope is the only warm colour in the image. No faces, no figures beyond the hands, no text, no letters, no symbols, no real people, no religious imagery, no trademarked imagery.

## Motif
Two gloved hands bound wrist to wrist by the red wool rope, dusted with snow. The rope is the series thread: every book in The Winter Passes centres one object touched by the same red rope.

## Palette
- Snow white, with a warm-grey shadow
- Glacier blue
- Deep slate ink
- Wool red (madder red), used only on the rope and the title's single accent line

## Type spec
- Series line: "The Winter Passes · Book 1", clean geometric sans-serif, small, tracked wide, snow white, at the top above the title.
- Title: "BOUND TILL THE THAW", tall condensed serif capitals with sharp chiselled wedge serifs, widely letter-spaced, snow white with a faint frost-crack texture. Stack on two or three lines in the top third over the pale sky, large enough to read at thumbnail size, with strong contrast against the sky (deep slate ink shadow or outline if needed).
- Accent: a thin madder-red horizontal rule directly under the title.
- Pen name: "MIRA SANGLA", geometric sans-serif small capitals, very widely tracked, snow white, centred at the bottom edge over the dark slate foreground. Keep the same vertical spacing as the other books in the series.
