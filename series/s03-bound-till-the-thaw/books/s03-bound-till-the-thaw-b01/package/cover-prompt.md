## Art prompt
A tight-focus close-up of two gloved hands bound wrist to wrist by a single hand-spun madder-red wool rope, dusted with fine snow, held slightly below the vertical centre of a portrait frame. The hands are the only figures: no faces, no bodies, no likeness of any real person. The ground is a cold, wide mountain landscape. A jagged blue-white ridge line runs across the lower third, and the top third is a pale, empty winter sky with soft warm-grey shadows, leaving clean space for the title. Palette limited to snow white with warm-grey shadow, glacier blue, deep slate ink, and wool red used only on the rope. Mood: cold, intimate, high-stakes, like breath held in frozen air, with all warmth coming from the red rope. Crisp macro detail on wool fibres and frost on the gloves, shallow depth of field on the distant ridge. No text, no lettering, no symbols, no religious imagery, no prayer flags, no monasteries, no trademarked imagery. Portrait 1600 x 2560.

## Motif
Two gloved hands bound wrist to wrist by the red wool rope, dusted with snow. This is Book 1 of the series look: one central object, tight focus, always touched by the same red rope.

## Palette
- Snow white, with a warm-grey shadow
- Glacier blue
- Deep slate ink
- Wool red (madder), used only on the rope and the thin accent rule under the title

## Type spec
Type is added after the art, not in the image.
- Series line: "THE WINTER PASSES · BOOK 1", clean geometric sans-serif, small, widely tracked, white, above the title in the top third.
- Title: "BOUND TILL THE THAW", tall condensed serif capitals with sharp chiselled wedge serifs, widely letter-spaced, snow white with a faint frost-crack texture. Set on two or three stacked lines in the top third over the pale sky, large enough to read at thumbnail size, with strong contrast against the sky.
- Accent: a thin wool-red horizontal rule directly under the title.
- Pen name: "MIRA SANGLA", geometric sans-serif small capitals, very widely tracked, white, centred at the bottom edge over deep slate ink, sized to stay legible at thumbnail.
- Keep the vertical spacing identical to the other books in the series.
