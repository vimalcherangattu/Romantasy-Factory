## Art prompt
Portrait book cover illustration, 1600 x 2560 pixels, no text, lettering or logos anywhere in the image. One central object in tight focus: two gloved hands, in thick dark slate-grey wool gloves, bound wrist to wrist by a single hand-spun madder-red wool rope tied in a knot, dusted with fresh snow and a few frost crystals. The hands are the only human element: no faces, no bodies, no recognisable person. They sit slightly below the vertical centre, resting on a wide, cold mountain ground of snow white with warm-grey shadows. A jagged glacier-blue and white ridge line runs across the lower third. The upper third is a pale, empty winter sky with soft gradients, left clear for the title. Palette limited to snow white with warm-grey shadow, glacier blue, deep slate ink, and wool red used only on the rope. Mood: cold, intimate and high-stakes, like breath held in frozen air. The only warmth in the picture comes from the red rope. Clean, painterly digital illustration with crisp detail on the knot and wool fibres, high contrast so it reads at thumbnail size. No religious symbols, prayer flags, monasteries or shrines, and no trademarked imagery.

## Motif
Two gloved hands bound wrist to wrist by the red wool rope, dusted with snow, centred slightly below the middle of the frame. This is the first object in a four-book set where the same red rope touches the central object on every cover.

## Palette
- Snow white, with a warm-grey shadow
- Glacier blue
- Deep slate ink
- Wool red (madder red), used only on the rope and the thin accent line under the title
