# Continuity report: Bound till the Thaw (Book 1, chapters 1–32)

## What was checked

- **Names and forms of address:** Guide/Captain/Rohan, Didi, Captain-ji, and the crew, goat and village names.
- **Ages:** checked against the bible.
- **Day count:** checked against F290, including the sixth-day door rule (F292), the 20-day deadline and day 20 at the Fair.
- **Firewood ledger:** 41 → 36 → 31/26/24 → fever (taken from the night's five) → 19 → 13 → 12 → 11 → 7 → 2. It is consistent.
- **Redbark stock:** 12 packets, 6 carried by Rohan, 3 ruined, 18 courses.
- **Graves:** 31, then 32 with Sunni.
- **Rope-Bond rules:** distance, lie-cold sizes, the loophole, promise hums, and the thaw ending.
- **Injuries:** Rohan's left ribs 7–8 and his left arm, his split right knuckles, Meera's split hands, and her left ring and little fingers (dead in ch 26, amputated in ch 31, with "eight fingers" in ch 30).
- **Who knows what:** the Salt Fox identity, Varun, the seal, Kiran on Dilan's rope, the masked buyer and the grey-bearded officer.
- **Objects:** the seal and its double hitch, Badami's two bells, the brass buttons, the badge, the rope end and the writ case.
- **Places:** Rope-Hut, Ranu, woodpile and lockhouse.
- **Relationship beats:** the first use of each name and the betrothal.

## Errors found (8)

- **E01 (ch 5):** the waystone carving is called "a thousand winters" old, against the 300-winter founding.
- **E02 (ch 8):** Meera takes back "the packet" from Rohan, who carried six.
- **E03 (ch 11, major):** Meera's memory of the ch 9 rope-end night contradicts that chapter. It has the wrong night, the wrong end of the rope, an invented blanket gesture, and a "silence" about her mother when she actually answered.
- **E04 (ch 13):** the night the cut was named is called "the second night"; it was the parley night, the third in the hut.
- **E05 (ch 14):** Meera accuses Asha of breaking a deadline that had already expired.
- **E06 (ch 16):** the scraped crates are moved from the top room to the yard.
- **E07 (ch 20):** a chapter number ("in ch 19") leaks into the narration.
- **E08 (ch 23):** "Kamla's smaller boy" implies two boys, but her children are a girl and a boy.
