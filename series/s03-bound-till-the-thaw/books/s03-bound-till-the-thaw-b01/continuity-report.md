# Continuity report: Bound till the Thaw (Book 1, chapters 1–32)

## What I checked

- **Names and forms of address.** I checked the cast, the Guide/Captain/Rohan progression, "Captain-ji" and "Didi". I also checked the minor names: Puja, Hakam, Leela, Badami, Sunni, Pinku, Lalit, Gopal, Pasang, Chetu and Bhim.
- **Timeline.** I checked against the S7 day count (F290–F293): sixteen days left at the ch 6 dawn, eleven at ch 13, eight at ch 19, five at ch 21, four at ch 22, and the Fair at dawn of day 20. Varun is buried seven days at ch 13. The door rule is the sixth day. Kiran's recovery, the snow delay and the travel times are consistent.
- **Magic.** I checked rope length, separation cold, lie sizes, the loophole, promise hums, cairn and waystone hums, and the thaw release at the Shilak Cairn.
- **Injuries.** Rohan has left ribs 7–8 cracked and cannot raise his left arm. His right knuckles split at the stone fold. Meera's hands split on the Teeth. Her left ring and little fingers die and come off two days after the Fair. The glove has a thumb and two fingers.
- **Who knows what.** I checked the Salt Fox identity, the seal, Varun, Kiran on Dilan's rope, the badge, the buyer and the grey-bearded officer.
- **Objects and places.** I checked the redbark packet and course arithmetic, the firewood ledger, the brass buttons, the seal cord knots, the bells, the badge, the rope end and the patterns of hut, ledge, Teeth and Ranu.
- **Relationship beats.** All match the outline as revised by the continuity facts.

## Errors found (5, all minor)

- **E01 (ch 22, ch 25):** Dilan's report is recalled as a "curl of frozen cartilage". Ch 2 and F383 give it as a scrap of skin from an ear rim.
- **E02 (ch 13):** The firewood is four sticks off. The count goes 24 → 20 after the fever pot, then a five-stick night should leave 15, not 19. Fixed by having the ch 12 night run on deadfall plus one dry stick.
- **E03 (ch 28):** Rohan counted to 160 at the grove wall, not "at the shed".
- **E04 (ch 32):** Meera produces Badami's big bell from her coat, though in ch 31 she left it on its peg for Bindu to bring.
- **E05 (ch 32):** Meera calls herself a "held name" after choosing the Closing Roll over held-name custody in ch 31.

## Noted, not raised

- In ch 28 Asha says Varun told her a pedlar sold him the button. This differs from his quartermaster's-tin story in ch 2 and ch 16. I read it as Varun lying to her and left it.
- In ch 31 Roop and Chand are sent downhill to Leela's orchard, yet they are at the cairn at the thaw. The orchard's distance is never given, so this is plausible.
- Varun's bound salt-road partner is never shown near him at Ranu or the Fair. This is not contradicted on the page, but it is worth watching in later books.
- The glove text in ch 31 ("thumb and two fingers") is correct. F262 and F363 say "three fingers"; the corrected wording is logged in new-facts.json.
