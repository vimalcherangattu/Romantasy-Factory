# Continuity report: Bound till the Thaw (Book 1, chapters 1–32)

## What I checked

- **Names and forms of address:** Captain, Guide, Captain-ji, Didi, and the first uses of "Meera" and "Rohan".
- **Ages and arithmetic:** cast ages against the Closing Law's sixty winters.
- **Day count:** checked against F290 to F293, covering days remaining, Varun's crossing and the door rule.
- **Firewood ledger:** traced from 41 sticks down to the 7 saved.
- **Redbark stock and courses:** 12 packets down to 18 courses.
- **Ranu figures:** grave and sick counts (31 graves, then 32; 41 sick).
- **Rope-bond rules:** distance, lie sizes, the loophole, and every bound promise and whether it was kept.
- **Injuries:** ribs, knuckles, Kiran's ear, and Meera's two fingers.
- **Objects:** seal and cord knot, brass buttons, Dilan's rope end and badge, both of Badami's bells, the pattus and the coat.
- **Who knows what:** the Salt Fox, Varun, Kiran on Dilan's rope, the seal, and the masked buyer.

## Errors found

All 13 are minor.

- **E01 (ch 6):** the days-remaining count is off by one against F290.
- **E02 (ch 3):** Rohan goes to find "his men", but only Varun is with him.
- **E03 (ch 14):** Meera says Asha didn't wait an hour past her deadline; it was a day.
- **E04 (ch 17):** "sixty winters before he was born" doesn't fit a 35-year-old.
- **E05 (ch 31):** twelve winters holding the seal conflicts with ten years as captain.
- **E06 (ch 19):** Rohan says he vowed to keep the law "in the Teeth"; he said it in the Rope-Hut.
- **E07 (ch 21):** the buckets read as Nimmi's, against Rohan's bound "I carry every bucket".
- **E08 (ch 22):** Dilan follows Kiran alone up the pass, unaffected by the lone-crosser rule.
- **E09 (ch 23):** Rohan's letter implies Ila knows about the seal; ch 16 says only Varun and the queen know.
- **E10 (ch 31, 32):** the glove is cut for three fingers instead of two, and ch 32 gives it an empty stall.
- **E11 (ch 29):** Rohan opens his coat while Meera is wearing it.
- **E12 (ch 29):** "Asha and her four" makes five people.
- **E13 (ch 32):** the goat pen gate is placed within reach of the cairn.

No major errors. The midpoint, the point of ritual death, the losses that stay lost, and the clue payoffs all hold.
