# Bound till the Thaw

**Series:** The Winter Passes, Book 1. **Pen name:** Mira Sangla.

**Logline:** A smuggler-guide racing medicine to her brother's fever village is rope-bound by the mountain spirits to the warden captain sworn to hang her crew. On the rope, every lie costs body heat, so every true word between them is both an intimacy and evidence.

**Central question:** Will Meera get the medicine through to Ranu, and will Rohan arrest her?

**Answer:** Yes, the redbark reaches Ranu. No, Rohan does not arrest her. He bends his law in public, and together they write the Winter Crossing Law.

**Heroine belief:**
- Opening: "The law is only ever a noose for people like us."
- Changed: "A law is a rope; it depends who ties it."
- Proof: chapter 30. Meera enters the count into the crown's record under her own name, as the Salt Fox.

**Hero change:** Rohan tells the assembly that the blockade he signed killed Ranu's people. He declines to arrest Kiran (chapter 29), and he refuses his writ's order at the thaw (chapter 32).

**Love interest's own goal:** Rohan must carry the queen's seal to the Snow Fair within twenty days and stop Regent Pritam's coup.

**Heat plan:**
- Chapter 20 (explicit): follows Rohan's truth that he signed the blockade. They become lovers knowing he is the noose.
- Chapter 30 (explicit): follows "I love you" spoken with bare, warm hands. It is the first night Meera is held without counting.
- Chapter 31 (closed door): Meera asks for something for herself.
- Both leads are adults, Meera 29 and Rohan 35. State their ages on the page in chapters 1 to 3 and again before chapter 20.

**Losses that stay lost:** Jassi (chapter 3), Sunni and the thirty-one dead of Ranu (chapters 17 and 18), Meera's left ring and little fingers (chapter 26, amputated in chapter 31), and Dilan.

## Chapter 1: The Count

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Pack the redbark and get her crew out of Kothi Shilak before the wardens arrive.
- **Value shift:** from hope to threat.
- **Beats:**
  - Before dawn in the salt house, Meera counts twelve packets of redbark into oilcloth aloud. She notices the crown stamp scraped off the crate lids, shrugs and writes the packets into the count (C01).
  - In the count she records the lowland middleman's last delivery. Its token for the next drop is a single brass button, which she sews into the ledger's spine (C21).
  - Kiran's note, brought down by the last crew pair to cross back: grey cough in Ranu, Nimmi's tally of the sick, and "Didi, come before the children go." Meera remembers his black-rimmed left ear. He jokes about it and has never once named the crossing that took it (C02).
  - Jassi (40) names every goat while grumbling about the run, then volunteers. Bindu sings knot-counts over the red rope, eight arm-spans of wool from Lata's house in Taravan. Meera's age, 29, comes up when Jassi teases her about ten winters of this.
  - A Taravan salt merchant pays double. Meera tells him it buys the widow Puja's son a free crossing next run. She does not say please.
  - At the village gate stand the Closing Law notice and the hanging tree, with two fresh pegs from last winter. The law is shown at its cost.
  - Warden horns sound. Meera hides the redbark in Bindu's wool bales.
  - Rohan walks into the salt house. He stops a warden from kicking a goat, asks for "the guide Jassi" by name, and calls Meera "Guide" when she answers for him.
- **Reader question out:** Why does the queen's warden captain want a guide over a closed pass in winter?
- **Techniques:** 1, 5, 11

## Chapter 2: The Writ

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Secure a guide over Shilak and find the Salt Fox while he is here.
- **Value shift:** from control to compromise.
- **Beats:**
  - Rohan (35) searches the salt house to the plank. He finds pass ropes but no contraband, and notices Meera counting his steps under her breath.
  - He shows the writ of passage in Queen Sarala's own hand. He tells the village only "crown business at the Snow Fair, twenty days." In his interiority the reader learns about the lapis goat seal, Pritam's coup and the order to bring in the Salt Fox at the thaw.
  - He asks the village who froze an ear on the pass two winters ago. The warden record of Dilan's death notes blood and torn frozen skin on the ice. Meera answers that half this village has lost something to that pass (C03).
  - Jassi refuses to guide without "his second." Meera insists on coming. Rohan pairs himself with Jassi and Meera with his lieutenant, Varun.
  - Varun, fastidious, polishes a brand-new brass button on his coat and calls Rohan "Captain-ji." He says he has chosen the gully approach as the safer route (C04, C22).
  - At night Rohan pays the widow Puja's seizure fine from his own purse and tells her not to name him (C23). He sees Meera at the salt-house door watching him leave, and says nothing.
  - Before dawn he finds a grain of red powder on Bindu's wool. He rubs it between his fingers and pockets it without a word.
- **Reader question out:** He has found the redbark trace. Why hasn't he searched the bales?
- **Techniques:** 13, 15, 16

## Chapter 3: The Second Slide

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Get Jassi, the goats and the hidden redbark up the gully alive.
- **Value shift:** from a living crew to being alone with her enemy.
- **Beats:**
  - In the gully Meera reads wind-slab and dislikes Varun's route. Jassi tells her about his sister's apricot orchard and the goat Badami, who will retire with him.
  - Varun drops back to fix a pack strap. Meera hears a double crack, iron striking first and then the slope letting go (C05).
  - The avalanche hits. Jassi is gone. Rohan throws Meera into the lee of a boulder and takes the second slide across his own back.
  - They dig until dark and find Jassi dead, with Badami's bell in his fist. Meera takes the bell (C25). Varun's tracks are buried. The redbark is safe in Meera's own pack, where she moved it at dawn.
  - Rohan, ribs cracked, says, "Your mountain. Give the orders." He obeys each one.
  - The approach is buried behind them, night is falling and no one crosses alone. At the Shilak Cairn they tie the red rope on each other's wrists. "Meera. Ranu." "Rohan. Garh Neshar." Blue cairn-light lifts out of the stones.
  - He asks why Ranu. She gives her first free truth to her enemy: "My brother is dying there."
- **Reader question out:** What will the bond cost two people who are each other's hanging?
- **Techniques:** 5, 6, 19

## Chapter 4: Watch My Hands

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Learn the bond's rules and reassert the law.
- **Value shift:** from resented dependence to agreed terms.
- **Beats:**
  - Meera teaches the rules precisely: the distance limit, lie-cold, and what is free. She demonstrates with a small lie, "Jassi hated those goats." Her fingertips whiten, and the grief under it shows.
  - Rohan takes a share of the load and finds the twelve packets. She says truthfully that they are medicine for Ranu, and that she will walk off the mountain before he takes them.
  - He states the law: seizure and the rope at the thaw. She answers: "Then arrest me at the thaw, Captain. Today you carry half."
  - He asks, "Are you the Salt Fox?" She stays silent, which is free. He says, "Silence is an answer, Guide. Watch my hands, and I will watch yours." It is a threat: he will see every lie she tells (C20).
  - He carries six packets and tells himself it is evidence custody. The barrier is now explicit: aiding her is harbouring.
  - Meera bets him he can't stay silent for an hour. He does it with ease, and she is furious to lose.
  - At the waystone below the Long Saddle she raises a small cairn for Jassi. Rohan takes off his glove to place a stone.
- **Reader question out:** Can two truthful adversaries survive a law that makes every truth evidence?
- **Techniques:** 15, 16, 22, 23

## Chapter 5: Two Brothers in Arms

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Shelter at the waystone and keep a hurt captain moving.
- **Value shift:** from adversaries to curious.
- **Beats:**
  - On the waystone a weathered carving shows two hands bound by one rope. Rohan quotes the warden chronicle, "two brothers in arms." Meera says the crews tell it as a warden and a smuggler. Neither can prove their version (C06).
  - Rohan steps past the rope's length to check a pitch. The cold hits them both at once. Meera hauls him back hand over hand and tells him his body is hers to keep alive until the thaw.
  - They make a dung fire. He says aloud that sharing it is harbouring, then sits down at it anyway.
  - She notices he breathes shallowly on his left side. He deflects.
  - First trade of questions. She asks what is at Garh Neshar, and he refuses. She asks why he hunts the crew, and he answers that a warden died on this pass two winters ago.
  - She asks again, flatly: "Are your ribs hurt?" He says, "It's nothing." The waystone hums higher, and under the cairn-light she sees his fingertips start to whiten as he pulls his glove on.
- **Reader question out:** How big was that lie, and what will it cost him on the climb?
- **Techniques:** 1, 4, 10

## Chapter 6: Taking the Cold

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Hide his ribs and keep pace for the deadline.
- **Value shift:** from pride to owing her.
- **Beats:**
  - On the morning climb Meera asks outright whether his ribs are broken. He says, "They are fine." It is a middling lie. Frostnip creeps over his knuckles.
  - She stops, strips her glove and says, "Give me your hand." He gives it, not knowing what she can do. The cold flows into her, and she knows the lie was middling.
  - He watches her hands go white and start to shake as she explains the loophole. She pays for his lie. "Now we go slow, and you tell me true, or I take it every time."
  - He tells the truth: two ribs cracked. She binds them with Jassi's spare cord.
  - That night she shakes, and fire can't touch lie-cold. He gives her his pattu. She refuses it, then takes it.
  - He tallies her choice: she could have let him slow and stumble and fall behind. She paid instead.
  - She asks, "Promise you won't lie again?" He knows a promise binds. "No. I'll show you."
- **Reader question out:** What will a man who won't promise do instead?
- **Techniques:** 9, 14, 22

## Chapter 7: Other Ropes

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Cross the first day of the Long Saddle before the weather.
- **Value shift:** from alone to hunted.
- **Beats:**
  - Glare and squeaking snow. Meera counts steps aloud. Rohan's eyes burn, so she cuts goggle slits in a strip of leather from his writ case.
  - She spots two bound pairs on her line far below. It is Asha's crew from the lower salt road, and Asha never crosses Shilak unless someone pays.
  - Rohan admits that he carries something the regent's people would pay a lordship for. He won't say what, which is free.
  - To lose the pursuers she wants a hidden crew ledge. He asks her not to show him anything he would have to report. She shows him anyway, because time matters more.
  - Competence duet: he reads the pursuers' pace and kit like a soldier, and she reads the snow.
  - Mid-traverse on the ledge, a snow bridge gives under Rohan. The rope snaps taut, and Meera braces on her axe, saying, "Don't you dare." The chapter cuts with him hanging.
- **Reader question out:** Can she hold him, and what does saving him show Asha's crew?
- **Techniques:** 6, 7, 16

## Chapter 8: The Rope-Hut

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Reach the Rope-Hut alive.
- **Value shift:** from fear of losing him to sharing one fire.
- **Beats:**
  - Meera hauls him out with a crew pulley trick, calling the count. He climbs on her numbers with his ribs screaming. Afterwards they both laugh, badly.
  - The Rope-Hut has one chulha, one sleeping shelf and a juniper pile. Meera counts forty-one sticks.
  - He names the crime aloud: this fire is harbouring. She answers, "Then hang yourself warm."
  - Checking the redbark for damp, he rubs the powder and names it crown-grade, ground fine, with the store's sieve mark. She says truthfully that her middleman never told her (C07).
  - He cooks the sattu badly and she lets him. They negotiate the one shelf. He sleeps on the floor within the rope's length, and she lies awake listening to him breathe.
  - Far below on the Neshar side, torches climb the road. They are not Asha's. They are the regent's hunters.
- **Reader question out:** Who told the regent's hunters which pass to climb?
- **Techniques:** 9, 11, 23

## Chapter 9: Clean-Cut

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Decide whether to wait out the hunters or go down.
- **Value shift:** from certainty to doubt.
- **Beats:**
  - Meera sets the firewood rule: one question each night, and each refused question burns one juniper stick. Evasion now costs warmth.
  - Her question: who died? He answers. Dilan, twenty-three, his youngest warden, died on this pass chasing a Kothi smuggler in a storm.
  - His question: who taught her the line? Her mother, who died of fever ten winters ago in a closed winter. Meera was nineteen.
  - She sleeps. Alone at the fire, he takes out Dilan's rope end and ties and unties the fibres. They are cut clean, not frayed (C08). He swore at the warden hall to find the smuggler on that rope.
  - She half-wakes, sees him put the rope away and doesn't ask. He notices that she chose silence.
  - He catches himself filing every truth she tells him as evidence, and hates it.
- **Reader question out:** Why does a rope that was cut clean keep him awake?
- **Techniques:** 10, 13, 25

## Chapter 10: Parley

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Learn what Asha wants and keep her crew off them.
- **Value shift:** from a rival to a named threat.
- **Beats:**
  - Asha's two pairs camp above the hut. Asha (28) calls a parley from a rock rib, staying within her own rope.
  - Asha names her price. The regent will pay a lordship's purse and a crown pardon for "the warden's coat and whatever's sewn in it" (C10).
  - Asha's tracker Lalit boasts of the gully approach: "three wardens and two goat-folk." Only the approach plan held those numbers. Meera marks it (C09).
  - Meera offers salt-road favours. Asha laughs, prices her and swears an invented oath.
  - Inside, Meera asks directly what is in his coat. He refuses twice and burns two sticks, then gives part of the answer: "The queen's will."
  - His breath rattles. In the middle of telling her the hunters' road, he collapses. She catches him, and his skin is burning.
- **Reader question out:** Can she keep him alive, and what will she find on him?
- **Techniques:** 4, 7, 16

## Chapter 11: The Lapis Goat

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Break his lung fever.
- **Value shift:** from holding her enemy's life to giving power back.
- **Beats:**
  - She strips his coat to poultice his ribs with juniper steam and spends a half-pinch of redbark on him. She records it in the count: Ranu is one dose poorer.
  - In the coat seam she finds the queen's seal, lapis cut with a mountain goat, on a cord tied in a warden's double hitch.
  - In fever he talks of Pritam, the Snow Fair and twenty days. She pieces together the coup, and why Asha wants the coat.
  - She holds the seal a long time. It could buy a pardon for her crew from Asha and medicine for Kiran.
  - She sews it back in and reties the cord in his double hitch, not her knot (C11).
  - He surfaces enough to see her finish the knot. Neither speaks.
  - In the count, under the half-pinch, she writes: "One captain. Price: none."
- **Reader question out:** Did he see enough to understand what she just refused?
- **Techniques:** 14, 16, 18

## Chapter 12: Grandmother's Knot

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Gather wood and watch Asha's crew.
- **Value shift:** from a profiteer in his eyes to someone he can't fit into his picture.
- **Beats:**
  - Rohan is too weak to walk, and she can't go further than eight arm-spans. She drags him in his blankets to the door so she can reach the deadfall. The comic logistics leave them both swearing.
  - Asha's pairs move off downslope and leave a tied-off food cache. Its knot is in an old pattern that matches the carved hands on the cairns (C12). Meera copies it into the count.
  - His question, lucid: why does she charge the rich double? So the poor cross free. He cannot make it fit any warden record.
  - Her question: has he hanged anyone? Two men, he says, and one was a boy carrying salt to his mother. He says it flatly, and his hands stay warm.
  - She doesn't forgive him. She counts it.
  - He says, "The double price is in no record." She answers, "Nobody writes down what we do for free."
- **Reader question out:** If the warden records are wrong about her, what else are they wrong about?
- **Techniques:** 10, 11, 23

## Chapter 13: Hunters' Fires

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Get himself mobile and read the hunters.
- **Value shift:** from patience to being cornered.
- **Beats:**
  - Rohan walks again, ashamed of having been dragged. Meera drills him on rope work until he stops apologising.
  - The hunters' fires are climbing Rohan's own fallback route, which only he and Varun drew. He notices it and refuses the thought (C13).
  - Nineteen sticks of juniper and nine days to the Snow Fair. They must move within two days.
  - He asks about the Salt Fox again. She burns a stick rather than answer, and he sees she will pay for evasion.
  - She asks why he calls her "Guide." The crew name is a hanging name, he says, and he won't say it to her face. It is true.
  - His hand rests on the rope beside her wrist, and she doesn't move hers.
  - Lalit's voice rises from the slope below, calling directions. Asha's crew is guiding the regent's hunters.
- **Reader question out:** Who sold the route, and to whom?
- **Techniques:** 1, 6, 9

## Chapter 14: Died on the Pass

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Plan a way down past the hunters.
- **Value shift:** from guarded to known.
- **Beats:**
  - Plan: the crew route called the Goat's Teeth avoids the hunters' road but costs a day, and the storm sky is building.
  - Her question: why keep the law? He answers that his grandfather "died on the pass" when the Closing Law was new. He believes it was an accident, and he keeps the law so no one dies that way (C14).
  - His question: what would she do if the law were gone? "Sleep."
  - She asks whether he pays poor families' seizure fines, as the widow Puja said. He stays silent and burns a stick, and she knows he does (C23 payoff).
  - They nearly kiss. She pulls back with a new reason: "At the thaw you deliver me. I don't kiss my noose."
  - The storm wall rises over the Long Saddle.
- **Reader question out:** Can they get down the Goat's Teeth before the storm and the hunters close?
- **Techniques:** 10, 15, 25

## Chapter 15: The Goat's Teeth

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Break past the hunters down the Goat's Teeth.
- **Value shift:** from escape to being trapped by the storm.
- **Beats:**
  - Two of the regent's hunters, bound as a pair, come up the Teeth. Rohan fights them in a disciplined way, disarming one and breaking the other's wrist rather than killing.
  - Meera reads a loaded slope and cuts a small slide that blocks the second pair. It is snow-craft, not magic.
  - In one hunter's purse Rohan finds a twist of crown-grade redbark with the same sieve mark as hers (C15).
  - The storm hits and forces them back up to the hut.
  - Meera's hands are raw with ordinary cold. He warms them against his skin, and this time fire and touch both work.
  - Eleven sticks left.
  - At the fire she starts, "The redbark in my pack and that purse came from the same—" A gust tears the door open and scatters the woodpile into the dark. They must save it or freeze.
- **Reader question out:** What was she about to name, and can they last the night?
- **Techniques:** 6, 7, 11

## Chapter 16: What the Storm Kept

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Survive the storm night on the last of the wood.
- **Value shift:** from enemies hiding things to complicit lovers.
- **Beats:**
  - They save seven sticks for a full night of storm. Meera says the arithmetic aloud: no lies tonight, because they can't afford the heat.
  - "I am the Salt Fox." Her hands stay warm. He answers, "My writ orders me to deliver the Salt Fox to Taravan at the thaw." His hands stay warm too.
  - They work it out together from the evidence: Varun's gully route, the double crack, Varun lagging behind, Lalit's numbers, the hunters on Varun's fallback, and crown redbark in a hunter's purse and in crates with scraped stamps. Varun set the avalanche, and he is her middleman (C01, C04, C05, C07, C09, C13, C15).
  - The mirror: her lifeline was his enemy's purse, and she has been funding Pritam's coup. He trusted the man who killed Jassi.
  - Both are complicit, and every earlier chapter rereads.
  - The first kiss, costed: she is kissing the man who must deliver her. He promises, "I will not let you fall on this mountain." The rope hums.
  - She realises what that means. Until the thaw, his life is bound to her footing, and the thaw is when he must hand her over.
- **Reader question out:** What does a promise to keep her safe do to a man ordered to hand her to the rope?
- **Techniques:** 3, 12, 17, 22

## Chapter 17: Below the Snowline

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Descend to Ranu past the hunters.
- **Value shift:** from a promise made to the weight of it.
- **Beats:**
  - The storm clears. Rohan, newly bound by his promise, tries to make every step safe. She pushes back: "Your promise doesn't make you my guide." He learns that keeping it means obeying her.
  - He tells her truthfully that a warden cannot pardon. He can't unwrite her name; only the queen can.
  - He grieves Varun, ten years his friend, and lets the anger show.
  - Birch gives way to walnut slope, and they smell boiled walnut leaf and hear coughing behind timber walls.
  - On the slope he counts new graves in the frozen ground, many of them small. There are thirty-one.
  - Nimmi, with a shovel: "Blockade dead. Thirty-one this winter. You're the captain who signs it?" He says, "Yes."
- **Reader question out:** What will Meera find in Nimmi's house, and what will Ranu do with a warden?
- **Techniques:** 14, 15, 19

## Chapter 18: Twelve Packets

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Get redbark into Kiran and the worst of the children.
- **Value shift:** from the hope of arrival to the grief of triage.
- **Beats:**
  - Kiran lies in Nimmi's house, grey and still joking: "Didi, you brought a warden?"
  - Nimmi lists symptoms like grievances. Twelve packets less a half-pinch makes eighteen courses for forty sick.
  - Meera and Nimmi choose who gets a course, and Meera writes each name in the count. She won't let Rohan watch the choosing, and he holds the lamp anyway.
  - Rohan carries water house to house but can't go further than eight arm-spans from her, so he follows her everywhere.
  - Under Kiran's pillow Meera finds a tarnished warden's badge. He won't say whose it is (C16).
  - Sunni, seven, gets her course too late and dies before dawn.
- **Reader question out:** Whose badge is Kiran hiding, and can Meera forgive the count she made?
- **Techniques:** 5, 13, 19

## Chapter 19: Nimmi's Count

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Bury Sunni, stretch the medicine and keep Rohan hidden.
- **Value shift:** from grief to anger at the law and the man.
- **Beats:**
  - At the graveside Meera faces Rohan with the law. He argues that without the Closing Law the regent's grain wagons and the salt lords would own the passes. She answers that it kills children. Both are right.
  - The regent's patrol rides through Ranu and marks sick houses with ash "for cleansing after the Fair" (C26).
  - Rohan must hide in the goat loft, and she must stay within the rope's length, so they lie pressed together in the hay.
  - In the loft he whispers the truth: he doesn't know if he can keep this law after Ranu.
  - The patrol captain says the general will ride up after the Snow Fair.
  - Back down, Meera finds she has stopped wanting him to be the villain.
- **Reader question out:** What does "cleansing" mean, and how long does Ranu have?
- **Techniques:** 15, 16

## Chapter 20: Across the Rope

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Get through the night after Sunni.
- **Value shift:** from enemies at peace to lovers by choice.
- **Beats:**
  - In Nimmi's storehouse Meera writes Sunni's name in the count. He asks to see it, and she lets him.
  - The truth across the rope: "I signed the Shilak blockade order three winters running. Sunni is in my hand too." His hands stay warm. It is acknowledgement, not absolution.
  - She writes his name in the count beside the blockade line. It is the first time she has let him into her ledger.
  - The open-door scene follows, with both their ages (29 and 35) on the page and explicit consent: she asks, and he asks back what she wants. His ribs and her careful hands shape it.
  - What changes: she chooses the man who carries the noose with her eyes open, and he commits harbouring knowingly.
  - Afterwards he avoids anything that sounds like a promise, and she notices. She asks, "Is this only the cold?" He says, "No." His hands stay warm.
  - Kiran's cough through the wall stops. She runs, and finds the fever has broken.
- **Reader question out:** Lovers now, so what happens when the thaw and his writ arrive?
- **Techniques:** 9, 10, 14

## Chapter 21: Six Days

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Plan the road to Garh Neshar for the Snow Fair, now six days off.
- **Value shift:** from united to split goals.
- **Beats:**
  - Kiran is recovering and needles "the warden wearing my sister's pattu." When Rohan mentions Dilan, Kiran goes still and changes the subject. The reader sees it and Rohan does not.
  - Nimmi says a crown surveyor woman has been trapped at Garh Neshar since first snow. Rohan knows at once it is his sister Ila, and that she is in danger if Pritam wins.
  - Rohan must reach Garh Neshar, so the bond means Meera must come. She won't leave Ranu while children are on their last doses and the houses are marked with ash.
  - They argue strategy, not insults. He concedes three days, and she concedes she will walk the seal to the Fair.
  - He drafts his speech to the assembly and cannot write the line about the blockade.
  - He starts to tell Kiran that the smuggler on Dilan's rope— and Kiran sees Meera at the door and bolts.
- **Reader question out:** Why did Kiran run from that sentence?
- **Techniques:** 8, 13, 15

## Chapter 22: The Badge

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Find out why Kiran ran.
- **Value shift:** from ignorance to terrible knowledge.
- **Beats:**
  - By the woodpile, within rope's length of Rohan inside, Kiran gives her the badge and won't speak the dead man's name. He was on the rope with Rohan's warden that night. The warden cut his own rope to stop Kiran being dragged off the ice, and Kiran's ear froze while he lay there (C02).
  - Kiran begs her. Rohan hunts the smuggler on that rope, and a death on an unlawful crossing hangs.
  - Meera's choice: telling Rohan forces him to report or break, while silence is free as long as he never asks.
  - She goes back inside. Rohan asks nothing, and she lies beside him awake.
  - The reader now knows seven chapters before Rohan does.
  - She realises there is exactly one question he could ask her that she can't survive.
- **Reader question out:** What will she do when he asks?
- **Techniques:** 2, 3, 13

## Chapter 23: Knots for Children

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Prepare to leave and find more redbark.
- **Value shift:** from closeness to a distance he can feel.
- **Beats:**
  - Meera has turned careful and spare with words. He sees it and doesn't push, because silence is her right. It is a choice he makes.
  - He teaches Ranu's recovering children a warden's knot game, and Meera laughs for the first time since Sunni.
  - The redbark is down to its last doses. Nimmi says a masked buyer has paid crew contacts for medicine before.
  - He tells Meera he will petition the queen to strike the Salt Fox clause. He states it plainly as an intent and not a promise, because a promise he can't guarantee would freeze him. She understands it as care.
  - Alone, he writes to Ila.
  - A knotted cord appears at Nimmi's door: a crew signal to meet in the walnut grove tonight.
- **Reader question out:** Who is the buyer, and what does he want for the medicine?
- **Techniques:** 9, 22, 23

## Chapter 24: Cut-Corner Silver

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Get more redbark from the masked buyer.
- **Value shift:** from hope to Varun's arrival.
- **Beats:**
  - In the walnut grove Meera meets the buyer while Rohan waits behind a wall at the rope's full length.
  - The masked buyer is courtly, answers questions with questions and is gentle with Nimmi's grandson. He pays in Neshar silver stamped with a cut corner (C17) and hands over a sack of redbark from the regent's own stores.
  - He warns her that Varun crossed bound to a salt-road man. Varun is in Neshar with Asha and the regent's warrant for "the seal-thief," and will reach Ranu at dawn.
  - She asks his name. He asks why a smuggler wants names.
  - Back with Rohan: Kiran is too weak to flee and the new doses must be given. Rohan gives her the seal: "Hide it where I can't lie about it." She ties the cord in his double hitch (C11 payoff).
  - Horns sound on the road at dawn, and Varun's voice calls "Captain-ji!"
- **Reader question out:** Will Rohan run or stand, and where has she hidden the seal?
- **Techniques:** 1, 7, 20

## Chapter 25: Captain-ji

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Protect Ranu, Meera and the seal.
- **Value shift:** from free to chained.
- **Beats:**
  - Varun arrives with the regent's men and Asha's pairs, carrying a warrant naming Rohan a seal-thief and traitor. Rohan surrenders to keep the soldiers out of the sick houses.
  - They search him and find no seal. Varun smiles, and the bounty becomes clear (C10).
  - Varun learns of the bond and keeps Meera chained near Rohan as "the hired guide." He doesn't know she is the Salt Fox.
  - Varun names Kiran in front of the village. His middleman's crew talk puts Kiran of Kothi on Dilan's rope: "The smuggler who killed your boy has been sleeping in your sickhouse."
  - Rohan looks at Meera, and she looks at nothing.
  - Asha watches the regent's men freshen the ash marks on Nimmi's house and goes quiet.
  - In the lockhouse, low, Rohan asks: "Was Kiran on Dilan's rope?"
- **Reader question out:** Will she tell the truth that hangs her brother?
- **Techniques:** 8, 13, 16

## Chapter 26: No

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Save Kiran.
- **Value shift:** from whole to maimed and failing.
- **Beats:**
  - Her calculation: silence is a yes to a man who cannot lie to the assembly. The truth hangs Kiran, and a lie costs flesh.
  - "No." It is a great lie. Her left ring and little fingers die white inside her glove.
  - She keeps the hand in her lap under her pattu.
  - Rohan sees her shoulders shake. He could take her hand and know for certain, and he chooses not to reach.
  - She can't tell whether that was mercy or belief, and neither can the reader yet.
  - Outside, the guards talk of firing Ranu tonight to "burn the cough" and its witnesses.
  - She can't feel her hand. She counts her breaths as her body cools.
- **Reader question out:** Will she live through the night, and will Ranu?
- **Techniques:** 14, 18, 19, 22

## Chapter 27: Torches

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Keep her alive and stop the fire.
- **Value shift:** from despair to a stranger's mercy.
- **Beats:**
  - Meera is sinking under shock and lie-cold. Rohan wraps her in his coat and pattu and keeps his body to her back, gloved, never touching her bare skin so he never takes the cold. He tells her truths all night to keep her awake: Dilan's laugh, Ila's measuring, his grandfather.
  - As she slips, his own fingertips begin to chill. His promise not to let her fall is pressing on him.
  - Through the slats he sees the regent's men circling the ash-marked houses with torches while the sick are inside (C26).
  - A grey-bearded officer rides up, looks at the houses and orders the torches lowered. The men obey, and Nimmi watches from her door (C18).
  - Rohan says aloud, "Live, Guide. I won't ask you anything else."
  - Before dawn Asha stands at the lockhouse door with a key and a knife: "Price first."
- **Reader question out:** What is Asha's price, and can Meera still walk?
- **Techniques:** 7, 9, 25

## Chapter 28: Asha's Price

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Escape with the seal and the evidence.
- **Value shift:** from captive to armed with proof.
- **Beats:**
  - Asha's price is not the pardon. She saw the torches, and she knows Varun's pardon is the same noose. She wants her crew alive and her name out of his books.
  - Meera tears a page from the count and trades it: the middleman's dates and crate marks, and the brass button token, which matches the new button on Varun's coat (C21, C22). Asha confirms that Varun paid her and that the middleman was his runner.
  - They escape, and Meera recovers the seal from the pot of steeping walnut leaf in Nimmi's chulha corner.
  - Nimmi looks at her hand. The fingers are dead and must come off after the Fair, or the rot will spread.
  - Rohan offers to carry her pack. For the first time she gives him her load.
  - Kiran insists on coming. Asha leads them on a salt-road path toward Garh Neshar.
  - Kiran says, "Captain. I have to tell you what happened on that rope."
- **Reader question out:** What will Rohan do with the truth Meera paid two fingers to hide?
- **Techniques:** 11, 12, 16

## Chapter 29: The Cut End

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Hear Kiran out.
- **Value shift:** from the law's certainty to mercy.
- **Beats:**
  - Kiran tells it without once saying Dilan's name. He calls him "your warden" and "him," then hands over the badge (C16).
  - Rohan takes out the rope end. It is cut clean, and he has known for two winters that Dilan cut it himself and has never let himself say so (C03, C08).
  - Rohan understands her lie. He looks at her glove and knows its size. She says, "Yes. I lied to you."
  - In front of Asha, he declines to arrest Kiran. It is the first public bend.
  - Repair: Rohan names his part. He didn't reach because he was afraid of what he would have to do if he knew. She lied to protect her brother from him. He says, "That's on my law, not your hand."
  - They plan the Fair: he presses the seal, she gives evidence, and he speaks about the blockade.
  - Garh Neshar's brass bell hums in their teeth. It is the dawn of day twenty.
- **Reader question out:** Can a warden and a smuggler win a regent's assembly?
- **Techniques:** 12, 18, 20

## Chapter 30: Watch My Hands

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Get the seal pressed and Varun exposed.
- **Value shift:** from outlaw to witness in the record, and from lovers to declared.
- **Beats:**
  - The Snow Fair ground: drums, roasting barley and a white sky. Pritam's investiture is under way, with Prince Kunal on the dais, Varun at Pritam's side and Ila in the crowd.
  - Rohan presses the seal. Its wax is mixed with the queen's own blood, proof she sealed it herself (C19). Pritam's claim is revoked.
  - Varun accuses Rohan. Meera steps up and enters the count into the crown's record under her own name, "Meera of Kothi Shilak, called the Salt Fox," as evidence of Varun's stolen-redbark trade. Asha testifies, and the button matches. It is the act a smuggler never commits.
  - Rohan tells the assembly that the blockade killed Ranu's thirty-one and that he signed it.
  - Pritam is taken and Varun arrested. Meera's confession stays on the record.
  - Alone afterwards, Rohan says "I love you" and holds up his bare hands: "Watch my hands." They stay warm (C20). She says it back, and her eight fingers stay warm.
  - Open-door scene in a borrowed room at Garh Neshar. What changes: love verified by the body, and she is held without counting anything.
  - His writ still orders him to deliver the Salt Fox at the thaw, and she has just confessed to being her.
- **Reader question out:** At the thaw, will he deliver her?
- **Techniques:** 18, 20, 24

## Chapter 31: The Winter Crossing Law

- **POV:** Rohan
- **Goal:** Write a law that keeps the protection and ends the hanging.
- **Value shift:** from a broken law to a drafted one.
- **Beats:**
  - Nimmi takes the two fingers. Rohan holds Meera's right hand, and she counts aloud while he counts with her.
  - They draft over the following weeks with Nimmi, Ila (who corrects every distance) and the hill council. The clauses: licensed bound guides, medicine always passes, and no one hangs for a death on a bound crossing. Rohan writes the last clause with Kiran in the room.
  - News: Varun goes to Taravan in chains when the passes open, Pritam is imprisoned, and "the regent's general" has been exiled, though Ranu whispers otherwise (C24).
  - A warden can't pardon, so Rohan offers to name Meera on his writ as his guide for the return crossing. That enters her in the Closing Roll for life. She chooses it: "I'll owe them winters. I'd give them anyway."
  - Closed-door night. She asks him for something for herself for the first time: "Stay."
  - Meltwater weather comes, and they cross back over Shilak as a lawful pair to be at the cairn for the thaw.
- **Reader question out:** When the bond ends, what will they choose?
- **Techniques:** 9, 14, 24

## Chapter 32: First Thaw

- **POV:** Meera
- **Goal:** Be at the Shilak Cairn when the bond ends.
- **Value shift:** from bound by the mountain to bound by choice, and from a book's peace to a series threat.
- **Beats:**
  - At the cairn with Bindu, the Kothi villagers and Rohan's wardens, Meera hangs Jassi's goat bell on the stones (C25). She counts the drips from the snow cap.
  - Rohan's senior warden reminds him that the writ says deliver the Salt Fox at the thaw. Before the meltwater runs, Rohan says, "I am not arresting her. I am walking to Taravan beside her, with the law in her hand."
  - Meltwater runs at the cairn and the bond ends. She walks past eight arm-spans, feels no cold, and walks back.
  - They retie an ordinary rope of Bindu's new spinning by choice, and that is their betrothal. She calls him Rohan.
  - The stones ring. For the first time in living memory the jogini speak in the cold itself, and every hand at the cairn feels the words: the Old Bargain is unpaid, and the pass will close forever after the fourth winter unless someone pays.
  - Meera looks at the carved hands on the inner face (C06). Rohan says Ila stayed in Neshar to finish her map, and that the queen will want it.
- **Reader question out:** What is the Old Bargain, and who will be made to pay?
- **Techniques:** 3, 20, 26
