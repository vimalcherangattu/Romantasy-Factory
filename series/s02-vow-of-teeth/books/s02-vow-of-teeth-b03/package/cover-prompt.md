## Art prompt
Close-cropped, intimate, dangerous still life, portrait 1600 × 2560. A circlet made of gold leopard claws rests on folded black cloth, a thin laterite-red cord threaded through the claws like a vow-binding, its loose end trailing across the fabric. The circlet sits just below the centre of the frame on a deep black field, lit from one side as if by a single oil lamp, so the claw tips catch turmeric-gold highlights and the rest falls into shadow. A faint leopard-rosette pattern shows in gloss black on matte black in the background. Hushed, sensual, the moment before a word is spoken. No people, no faces, no text, no lettering, no logos, no trademarked imagery. Leave the top third and the bottom edge clear and dark for type added later.

## Motif
A crown of gold leopard claws on black cloth, bound by a thin red vow-cord. Same placement, lighting and background pattern as books 1 and 2 so the three covers read as a set.

## Palette
Deep black, turmeric gold, laterite red, ivory (used sparingly in the cloth highlights).

## Type spec
- Series line: "THE ODIYAN CONTRACTS" in small, widely spaced laterite-red caps, centred above the title.
- Title: "CROWN OF NIGHT" in a tall, high-contrast serif, all caps, foil-gold on black, set large across the top third. The terminal of one letter (the crossbar of the T in NIGHT) is drawn out into a fine claw hook. Two lines (CROWN OF / NIGHT) so it stays readable at thumbnail size.
- Pen name: "ANYA KALLOOR" in a clean geometric sans, widely spaced small caps, ivory, centred along the bottom edge.
- Keep strong contrast so the title reads at about 150 px wide.
