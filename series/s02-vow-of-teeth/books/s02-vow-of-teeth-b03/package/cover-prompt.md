## Art prompt
Close-cropped, intimate, dangerous book cover art, portrait 1600 x 2560. On a deep matte black cloth field, just below centre, rests a circlet made of curved gold leopard claws, each claw catching warm turmeric-gold light along its edge, with a thin laterite-red cord threaded through the circlet like a vow-binding, one loose end trailing across the cloth. The scene is lit from one side as if by a single oil lamp, leaving the far side in deep shadow. A faint leopard-rosette pattern shows in gloss black on matte black across the background. Hushed, sensual, the moment before a word is spoken. Rich texture of folded cloth and hammered gold, shallow depth of field. No people, no faces, no hands, no text, no letters, no logos, no real-world symbols. Leave the top third dark and clear for a title and the bottom edge clear for a name.

## Motif
A circlet of gold leopard claws resting on black cloth, threaded with a thin red cord like a vow-binding. It is the crown the queen offered, and the bond Neela chooses instead.

## Palette
Deep black, turmeric gold, laterite red, ivory.

## Type spec
- Series line: "The Odiyan Contracts" in small, widely spaced laterite-red capitals, centred above the title.
- Title: CROWN OF NIGHT in a tall, high-contrast serif, all caps, foil-gold on black, set large across the top third. One letter's terminal (the tail of the R) is drawn out into a fine claw hook. Two lines is acceptable (CROWN OF / NIGHT) if it keeps the letters large; it must read clearly at thumbnail size.
- Pen name: ANYA KALLOOR in a clean geometric sans, small caps, widely spaced, ivory, along the bottom edge, with enough size and contrast to read at thumbnail.
- Placement matches books 1 and 2: motif just below centre, same margins, so the three covers read as a set.
