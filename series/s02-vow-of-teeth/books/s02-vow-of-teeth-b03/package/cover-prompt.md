## Art prompt
Close-cropped, intimate, dangerous book-cover illustration, portrait 1600 x 2560, no text or lettering anywhere in the image. A circlet made of gold leopard claws rests on folded black cloth, a thin red cord threaded through the claws like a vow-binding, its loose end trailing across the cloth. The motif sits just below centre on a deep black field, lit from one side as if by a single oil lamp, so the claws catch turmeric-gold highlights and the rest falls into shadow. The red cord reads as laterite red, the cloth edge picks up a hint of ivory. A faint leopard-rosette pattern shows in gloss black on matte black in the background. Empty space across the top third and along the bottom edge for type added later. Hushed, sensual and dangerous, the moment before a word is spoken. No faces, no real people, no trademarked imagery.

## Motif
A circlet of gold leopard claws on black cloth, with a thin red cord threaded through it like a vow-binding. Same placement as books 1 and 2 so the three covers read as a set.

## Palette
Deep black, turmeric gold, laterite red, ivory.

## Type spec
Series line "The Odiyan Contracts" in small, widely spaced laterite-red caps above the title. Title "CROWN OF NIGHT" in a tall, high-contrast serif, all caps, foil-gold on black, set large across the top third, with one letter's terminal drawn out into a fine claw hook; keep it readable at thumbnail size. Pen name "ANYA KALLOOR" in a clean geometric sans, widely spaced small caps, ivory, along the bottom edge.
