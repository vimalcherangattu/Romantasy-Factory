# Continuity report: Crown of Night (book 3), chapters 1 to 32

## What I checked

- **Bible and world rules:** the witness word, self-naming, the ledger and page rules, resin and the ache, and the wound-share.
- **Continuity facts:** F001 to F1014, including the book 1 and book 2 summaries.
- **The outline.**
- **The full text, chapter by chapter**, for:
  - names and roles
  - ages
  - day counts and travel times
  - injuries and which side they are on
  - who knows what
  - Company and defender counts
  - resin pinches
  - places and layouts
  - objects (the oilcloth bundle, pebbles, tins, the crown leaf case, the seal)

## Errors found

**Major (2)**

- **E01:** Ch 8 says Devan has not struck first in eight years, but he struck first at the picket in ch 5, with Kandan watching. Ch 8 should be his first killing blow.
- **E02:** In ch 24, Chathu's plain-ink leaves burn at the namings. That breaks the page rule and contradicts the ch 29 reveal that only the members' own Resin House pages burned.

**Minor (13)**

- **E03:** Resin-ink is called "years dry" in ch 15 and "will take a day to dry" in ch 16, but it stays tacky (ch 13, ch 29).
- **E04:** Chs 19, 21 and 22 place the queen's meeting with Neela in the sea room. It happened in the drying shed.
- **E05:** In ch 17, Kuttu asks Vellan whom he would name while Kuttu is in Kodiyur. Changed to Thulasi.
- **E06:** Ch 22 says Neela learned the guards' names on the sea-stair landing. She learned them in the drying shed.
- **E07:** Rema's escort stayed three days (ch 2), not four (ch 9).
- **E08:** Ch 23 makes the shelf a hundred and ten paces wide. Elsewhere the narrows are forty paces and narrower under the shelf.
- **E09:** Ch 8 credits the doubled-leaf coin to the man by the tower. Neela never saw coins on him.
- **E10:** Ch 11 says Kandan called Neela a witch "a month ago". It was months ago.
- **E11:** Koman says the Resin House fell "two moons" ago. It was three.
- **E12:** Ch 17 says "three days" of holding the shelf. It has been about a week.
- **E13:** Ch 31 counts forty-one kennel cages. There are forty-two, with forty-one animals.
- **E14:** Ch 3 says Devan named her "the other one". She told him that name.
- **E15:** Ch 10 says Devan's healing rope burns come from the well shaft. They come from the goat-path rope.

## Not flagged

Several things look like slips but are explained on the page, so I left them alone:

- Neela's sea-stair route versus Usha's description (ch 13 reconciles them).
- The crown leaf moving from the red chest to the green case (ch 27).
- The pairing of Vasu with a guard, which happens off the page.
