# Crown of Night — summary

## Plot

**Act 1 (ch 1–3).** Thurai land about sixty ships at Nellimala, Neela's birth village. The treaty is broken because Ittan is dead and Muthu seals the pass honestly. Devan, who has no office, asks the Company's leave to hold the Monkey Pass (ch 1). Usha, First Guard, brings the queen's pardon letter, which says the hill villages must bear raiders "as they always have". She leaves a door: the crown leaf is in the queen's room, reached by the sea stair at the noon gun (ch 2). The Company votes by two to send six to Kodiyur under Neela and hold the pass with the rest (ch 3).

**Act 2a (ch 4–15).** Muthu's crown order says walls only. Devan leads volunteers at the lightning boulder and takes the mirror. A captive says the Thurai mean to occupy the hills (ch 5). Neela scouts Nellimala, then learns from Ponni that she is Indu, daughter of Ammini, taken by men in grey with a bird on their cart (ch 6–7). A scout's coin shares a die with the widow's coin (ch 8). Muthu refuses to send men to Kodiyur, in an open letter (ch 9). The couple part (ch 10). Muthu sallies by written order (ch 11). Neela meets the thirty and Thankam in Kodiyur, then takes a debt from Chathu (ch 12–13). Devan saves Kesu's village and is hooked in the ribs (ch 14). Neela steals the thirty pages (ch 15).

**Midpoint (ch 16).** The queen walks into the drying shed. She has rewritten Usha's page: "You carried away paper. You can't carry away what I saw." She owns the folded bird and offers command of the guard plus Kettamala for Devan.

**Act 2b (ch 17–23).** Devan refuses the offer by letter without telling Neela, and summons Rema (ch 17). Neela returns with the twenty-two guards. The couple fight back to back at the burning tower (ch 18–19). Devan's breach is aired and repaired (ch 20). Neela decides to refuse the command openly (ch 21). The queen arrives and the two talk on the west stair (ch 22). The assault is moved up to dusk (ch 23).

**Act 3 (ch 24–32).**
- Neela refuses the command in the open (ch 24). Twenty-six of the thirty and most of the forty self-name, and Usha names herself to the queen.
- In the night battle Devan uses the killing strike once, and a spear takes him under the right collarbone. Neela is dragged down by the share, and Vellan's bear holds the cut (ch 25–26).
- Vellan gives Asha's last words and dies (ch 27).
- Neela signs the roll first (ch 28).
- Chathu's forty pages are plain ink (ch 29). The queen admits the raids made the knives. Rema arrives.
- Devan reads the payment writ aloud and the Company votes its terms (ch 30).
- The queen signs the charter and burns the leaf. Chathu is sent to the kennels (ch 31).
- Weeks later the Thurai sail under truce. A Thurai envoy asks to hire the Company, with a ledgerless rogue beside him (ch 32).

## Romance

Neela (27) and Devan (32) are married and end as equals. Her belief changes from "belonging to no one" to choosing who she is bound to (ch 24, 28).

Key moments:
- The wound-share bargain: if I bleed, don't come; if you bleed, I won't, unless the vote sends me (ch 10).
- The noon-thorn signal: three welts mean alive.
- Devan breaks their "say it before you do it" term by refusing the queen's offer unilaterally. He apologises, and she is still angry (ch 20).
- The lamp-lit scene in the warden's chamber (ch 21).
- Her public naming of him (ch 24).
- His public declaration, entered on the high court roll as "Devan, of the Company, bound to Neela" (ch 30).
- The tent scene, where she becomes his right hand and he becomes the place where her vigilance stops (ch 31).

Disclosures:
- Devan knows her birth name, her mother's name Ammini, and her temptation to take the command.
- Neela knows his wish to keep Kettamala, and that he carried the writ.

Promises that bind them:
- Say it before you do it.
- Pain is not a summons.
- "Bound by choice", written on the roll.
- Each counts the other's breath hitch.

## Character states

- **Neela/Indu:** Chartered Company member, first name on the roll. Her palms and ribs mirror Devan's wounds. She grieves Chathu and Ammu. She knows the queen is the folded-bird hand and that men in grey took the Nellimala children.
- **Devan:** Right hand permanently dead from the nerve cut. The killing strike is gone. He has no office, has refused Kettamala, writes left-handed, and teaches left-handed drills. He knows Asha's last words. He is bound to Neela.
- **Vellan:** Dead. He held Kamalakshi's badly carved bird. His smear of lamp-black is on the plank.
- **Queen Nirmala:** Keeps her throne and loses her hidden knives. She shares Usha's wounds. She admitted in writing what the crown permitted.
- **Usha:** Bound to the queen, who looked at her. She signed as Ushakumari.
- **Muthu:** Holds the border seal. He has the seventh-day round with Devan.
- **Janu:** Bound to Kamalakshi. She lights the lamp at the west stair every noon.
- **Chathu:** Kennel-keeper at the groves under Pachan for life, gagged at dusk. He cheated the queen with plain ink ("Written off. Paid in full. C.").
- **Kamalakshi:** Bound to Janu.
- **Pavi:** Bound to Thilak.
- **Kuttu:** Bound to Bhaskaran.
- **Sanu:** Bound to Kandan.
- **Lata:** Bound to Usha.
- **Kandan:** Now trusts and follows Devan.
- **Koman:** Hired as watcher.
- **Ponni:** Lives at the hall.
- **Kirri:** Still caged; the boat *Kirri* is built and the cloth sent.
- **Dead:** Biju, Sankaran, Chami, Rajan, Chami's cousin, Raiu and Asha (earlier).

## World changes

- The Company is chartered. It owns its name, the Blackbark groves under Pachan's map, and its members' lives. It contracts by vote.
- The crown holds no page, and the "Sold to the crown" sums are owed back to the members.
- The charter names what the crown permitted: the controlled raids, the stolen children, and the killing of Asha.
- The hall stands in the old watch-house above the pass, with an unshuttered lamp at the door.
- The Thurai fleet has sailed under an open truce.
- Varanad's night-guard is now bound pairs. Their wounds are shared, and a mortal wound drags the partner down.
- Rema's high court roll holds Asha's finding and the writ.

## Open threads

- **C12:** Salt-and-clove-bark resin on scarred Thurai wrists (ch 6, 8). Ch 32's rogue is in no ledger. Someone across the sea is making odiyans.
- **C13:** The long, low shape in the river that no scent matches (ch 17, 20, 26). It pulled Thurai wounded from the water and did not fight. It is not mentioned in ch 32.
- **Hinge:** What do the sea lords want with free knives, and who made the ledgerless rogue?
- Who the "men in grey" are, and who held the steward's lamp. The grey household steward asked "Has she come?" (ch 13) and is never named.
- The four-clause note on "the promise to my own people" the queen would not explain (ch 16).
- Eravi's fate and Sanu's salve-pot notes.
- Kirri's recovery.
- The four unbound guards (Sajan and others) and Vellan's unburied Thurai boy.
- The first Thurai contract vote at noon. Neela votes no.
