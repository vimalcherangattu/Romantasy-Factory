# Crown of Night — summary

## Plot
**Act 1 (ch 1–8).** Rains lift; sixty Thurai sails appear off Nellimala, Neela's birth village. The treaty is dead with Ittan, so this is an occupation, not a raid (ch 5). Devan, officeless, asks the Company's leave to hold the Monkey Pass and gets it (1). Usha, First Guard, brings the queen's pardon letter and a private message: "The leaf sleeps in the queen's room… sea stair at the noon gun" (2). The Company votes by two to send six to Kodiyur under Neela while the rest hold the pass (3). Muthu, bound by a "walls only" crown order, will not yet commit his garrison (4). Devan, Corporal Kandan and volunteers hold the gorge (5). Neela scouts Nellimala and meets Ponni, who names her mother Ammini and tells of men in grey taking the children (6–7). The shelf is fortified; Biju dies; coins and scars hint at a Thurai odiyan (8).

**Act 2 (9–16).** Muthu defies a crown order in plain black wax (9). Neela and Devan consummate a bargain: pain is not a summons (10). Muthu's "refugee escort" saves the shelf (11). Neela reaches Kodiyur (12), takes Chathu's sweet and learns the stair (13). Devan sallies to save Kesu's village and is hooked (14). Neela steals thirty pages (15). **Midpoint (16):** the queen arrives, writes Usha's page afresh ("You can't carry away what I saw"), owns the folded-bird hand, and offers command of a page-free guard plus Kettamala for Devan: "Safe, unseen, and mine."

**Act 3 (17–24).** Devan refuses the offer in writing without telling Neela, breaking their term, and writes to Justice Rema (17). Neela brings twenty-two guards north (18). They break the burning tower together (19). Devan's breach is repaired (20). Night in the warden's chamber (21). The queen reaches Kettamala; she and Neela speak on the west stair (22). The war council votes Devan's plan (23). At dusk Neela publicly refuses command and names Devan as her chosen; twenty-six guards self-name and their leaves burn (24).

**Act 4 (25–32).** Devan uses the killing strike for the last time and is speared; Neela is dragged down with him (25). Thankam operates; the queen holds the lamp; Neela turns back and holds the healing point (26). Vellan gives Asha's last words and dies (27). Neela signs the roll first (28). She finds Chathu's forty pages are plain ink, so the forty were never bound; the queen admits the raids made her knives (29). Devan reads the payment writ and enters it on Rema's roll (30). The queen signs the charter, burns the leaf, and Chathu goes to the groves' kennels (31). Weeks later the Thurai have sailed under a truce; a Thurai envoy arrives with a rogue odiyan (32).

## Romance
Neela (27) and Devan (32) are married and end HEA. Key beats: his vote request (1); "I counted your hand" (3); consent to risk (8); ch 10 bargain and noon thorn signal; breach and repair (17, 20); ch 21 lamp lit, "seen" as a choice; her public choice of him (24); both signing with "Bound by choice" (31). She is his right hand; he is her place of rest. She told him her mother's name and he keeps it; he disclosed Asha's note. Binding promises: tell before acting, don't answer for each other, pain is not a summons, noon three-prick means alive, ask rather than assume. Their wounds are shared for life; a mortal wound drags the other.

## Character states
- **Neela/Indu:** human; signed "Indu, called Neela, of Nellimala and Kettamala." Believes freedom is choosing who you are bound to. Knows her mother Ammini died on the goat path, that the crown took the children, and that Chathu faked the forty pages.
- **Devan:** right hand dead (nerve cut), killing strike gone, no office, left-handed pen. Company member, not warden. Heard Asha's words; weeps and is at peace.
- **Vellan:** dead. **Queen Nirmala:** keeps throne, lost the leaf, signed an admission; shares Usha's wounds. **Usha:** bound to the queen, Lata bound to Usha. **Kamalakshi:** bound to Janu. **Chathu:** unbound and gagged at dusk, serving in the groves' kennels under Pachan. **Muthu:** warden's seal-holder, border loyal. **Janu:** bound; lamp at the west stair each noon. **Sajan:** burned his leaf and never self-named; Sanu is bound to Kandan. Pavi–Thilak, Kuttu–Bhaskaran, Thulasi–Madhavi are paired.
- **Dead:** Biju, Sankaran, Chami, Rajan, Lekha, Orma, Kani, Swathi, Sreedhar, Vellan.

## World changes
The Night Company is chartered: self-owning, with the groves under Pachan's map, contracts by vote, no crown page. "Sold to the crown" sums owed back. The crown's role in the raids and in Asha's death is written into the charter. Resin-ink pages work only through a living witness; self-naming voids all other witnesses. The Thurai fleet has left under an open truce. The hall stands in the old watch-house above the pass. Rema holds the high court roll. The Company contracts paired members only.

## Open threads
- **C12 (series):** salt-and-clove-bark resin on scarred Thurai wrists, now a rogue odiyan in no ledger, brought by the Thurai envoy (ch 32). Who makes odiyans across the sea?
- **C13 (series):** a long, low river shape no scent matches, which pulls Thurai wounded from the water without fighting and watches the road.
- **Hinge:** the Thurai hire offer and the first Company vote on it at noon.
- Sajan's freedom rests on the charter alone; the queen remains a living witness for the four unnamed guards' pages.
- The grey steward's "Has she come?" (ch 13) is unexplained, as is the fresh yellow bird on the Thurai cart (17).
- Who put the grey men and the cart at Nellimala; the Keeper's household.
- Eleven Resin House pages Neela holds are never shown returned (ch 24).
- Kirri remains caged; the boat bears her name.
