# Crown of Night — summary

## Plot
**Act 1 (ch 1–8).** Cloud lifts; sixty Thurai sails off Nellimala, Neela's birth village (1). The treaty of controlled raids has broken. Devan, with no office, asks the Company's vote to hold the Monkey Pass. Usha, First Guard, brings the queen's pardon offer, "as they always have" (2). Neela hears "the leaf sleeps in the queen's room; sea stair at the noon gun." The Company splits: Neela takes six to Kodiyur, the rest hold the pass (3). Muthu holds a crown order of walls only (4). The advance guard is stopped at the lightning boulder; a captured sergeant says this is an occupation (5). Neela scouts Nellimala and meets Ponni, who names her mother Ammini and the men in grey with a yellow bird on a cart (6–7). Devan kills first at the shelf and asks Neela's consent to his war (8).

**Act 2 (ch 9–16).** Muthu answers the crown in open black wax, keeping his men at the border (9). Devan and Neela part with the noon-thorn signal (10). Muthu's thirty relieve the shelf (11). In Kodiyur Neela meets the guards and the surgeon Thankam (12). Chathu gives her the way in; she takes his sweet and owes him (13). Devan sallies for Kesu's village (14). Neela steals the leaf from the queen's chest (15). **Midpoint (16):** the queen, Nirmala, shows she can rewrite any page she witnessed. She owns the folded-bird private hand and offers Neela command of a page-free night-guard, with Kettamala for Devan.

**Act 3 (ch 17–24).** Devan refuses the offer in writing without telling Neela and summons Justice Rema (17). Neela's twenty-two riders arrive as the tower burns; they fight unbound (18–19). The Company hears the cost of self-naming; Devan and Neela repair the breach (20–21). The queen arrives; Neela chooses to answer in the open (22–23). Naming dusk: Neela refuses command, naming Devan as her choice; twenty-six guards self-name and their crown leaves burn; Usha names herself to the queen (24).

**Act 4 (ch 25–32).** Devan kills a Thurai captain with the two-finger strike, then takes a spear under the right collarbone; Neela falls with him (25). Thankam operates; the queen holds the lamp; Neela heals by throat point at dawn (26). Vellan dies giving Asha's last words (27). Neela signs the roll first by daylight (28). Chathu's forty pages are plain ink and worthless; the queen admits the raids made her knives (29). Devan reads the payment writ; the Company votes the charter terms (30). The queen signs and burns the crown leaf (31). Thurai sail under truce. A Thurai envoy asks to hire the Company, with a scarred rogue in tow (32).

## Romance
Neela (27) and Devan (32), married, end bound by choice. Key moments: his public ask for leave (1); consent asked at the shelf (8); the noon-thorn term and "pain is not a summons" (10); his unilateral refusal breaching "say it before you do it," then repair (17, 20); her confession of wanting command (21); public declaration both ways (24, 30); the roll line "Bound by choice" (31). Disclosed: Neela has told him her birth name, Indu, and her mother Ammini; he has told her Asha's hidden note and his wish for the fort. Bargain: his right hand is dead, so she is his hand and he is her rest; they ask before acting. He trains left-handed and still takes turns being answered first. They stay at the Company's hall. Wound-share remains for life.

## Character states
- **Neela/Indu:** leopard odiyan, self-named to Devan. First on the roll; both names on the door board. Shares wounds with him. Fears nothing now about being seen.
- **Devan:** right hand permanently dead, killing strike gone; no office, member of the Company; holds the writ, now entered with Rema.
- **Nirmala:** keeps the throne, lost the hidden knives. Shares wounds with Usha.
- **Usha:** self-named to the queen; Lata is named to Usha.
- **Muthu:** warden-captain of Kettamala. **Janu:** bound to Kamalakshi. **Pavi–Thilak, Kuttu–Bhaskaran, Sanu–Kandan, Sajan–Sanu's brother** pairs. **Kamalakshi:** names Janu.
- **Chathu:** kennel-keeper under Pachan, gagged at night. **Kirri:** still caged; boat Kirri built.
- **Dead:** Vellan, Biju, Sankaran, Lekha, Orma, Kani, Swathi, Sreedhar.
- **Koman:** watcher in Company pay. **Ponni:** lives near the hall.

## World changes
The Night Company is chartered: owns itself, groves under Pachan's map, contracts by vote. The crown holds no pages; no named member is rogue. The "Sold to the crown" sums are owed back. The crown's complicity in Nellimala and Asha's death is on record. Rema's roll is the high court's. The charter names its own crime. Controlled raids are over; the Thurai fleet sailed under an open truce. Haran left the queen's confidence.

## Open threads
- **C12 (series):** salt-and-clove-bark resin on scarred Thurai wrists; the envoy's rogue, in no ledger, has it. A second maker of odiyans exists overseas.
- **C13 (series):** the long low river shape no Company scent matches; it watches, drags Thurai wounded out, and was last seen watching the pass road.
- **Hinge:** the sea lords want to hire the Company; the Company votes at noon.
- Who pays the Thurai now and who ran the "men in grey" cart; the grey-cloaked steward who recruited Muthu and asked "Has she come?"
- Eleven of the forty and four guards held pages; the queen's remaining leaves are burned or returned, but who among the unnamed is still unbound is unsettled.
- Kirri's cage and anklet.
- Neela's mother Ammini's fate beyond "did not come down."
