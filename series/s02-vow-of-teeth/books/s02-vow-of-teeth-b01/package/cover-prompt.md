## Art prompt
Close-cropped, intimate, dangerous romantasy book cover art, portrait 1600 x 2560. On a deep matte black field, just below centre, a woman's hand wearing a plain gold wedding bangle lies flat on a man's bare chest. Her fingertips are turning into leopard claws, curved and pale, catching turmeric-gold light. Only the hand and a section of the man's chest are shown, with no faces. The scene is lit from one side as if by a single oil lamp, with warm gold on skin and bangle and deep shadow elsewhere. Laterite-red warmth bleeds faintly into the shadow edges. In the background a faint leopard-rosette pattern shows in gloss black on matte black, visible only where the light grazes it. Hushed, sensual, the moment before a word is spoken. Leave the top third and the bottom edge as clear dark space for type. No text, letters, logos or watermarks in the image. No real person's likeness and no trademarked imagery.

## Motif
A woman's hand in a plain gold wedding bangle, fingertips turning into leopard claws, resting on a man's bare chest. It is the series look: a detail of bodies, never a full face. It carries the book's promise of a marriage, an intimate touch and a hidden predator.

## Palette
Deep black (field and rosette pattern), turmeric gold (lamp light, bangle, title foil), laterite red (shadow warmth, series line), ivory (claws, pen name).

## Type spec
- Series line: "The Odiyan Contracts" in small, widely spaced laterite-red caps, centred above the title.
- Title: "VOW OF TEETH" in a tall, high-contrast serif, all caps, foil-gold on black. Set large across the top third, in two lines (VOW OF / TEETH) if needed to fill the width. Draw out the terminal of one letter (the final H of TEETH) into a fine claw hook. It must stay readable at thumbnail size, with strong contrast against the black.
- Pen name: "ANYA KALLOOR" in a clean geometric sans, widely spaced small caps, ivory, centred along the bottom edge. Large enough to read at thumbnail size.
- Keep all type clear of the hand and chest. Use the same placement as the other books in the series.
