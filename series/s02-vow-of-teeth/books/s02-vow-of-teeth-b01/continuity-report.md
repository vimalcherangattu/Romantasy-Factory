# Continuity report: Vow of Teeth (Book 1, chapters 1–32)

## What was checked

- **Names and titles:** the full cast and household (Kannan, Chinna, Raiu, Unni, Bhaskaran, Gopan, Eravi, Sudhi, Pachan, Parukutty and others).
- **Ages and spans:** Neela 27, Devan 32, Ammu 25, Raiu 19. Twenty-one years numbered, eleven years of turnings, eight years without sparring, six years since Asha.
- **Night counts:** the counts down to the rains and to the new moon, and the sealing schedule.
- **Post days:** the ninth-day post and Muthu's letters.
- **World rules:** the Turning, witnesses (Janu sees only eyes; Devan never sees Vellan turn), self-naming, page burning with the intake slip surviving, wound-share, and the ache.
- **Injuries and their sides:**
  - Left-forearm arrow graze.
  - Left-rib poison cut and its twin welt on Neela.
  - Resin-scar welts on Devan's wrists and throat.
  - Split nails.
- **Objects:** shutter bar, sword, knife (given and returned), pebbles, tooth, bangle, the forty-one kennel animals, tokens, seals.
- **Who-knows-what:** across the secret map.
- **The bible's fixed traits:** Devan never lies to Neela; Neela never says the word until she self-names.

## Errors found

- **Major (1):** Ch 32 says the queen already holds Neela's name from the closing writ, but ch 29 establishes that the sealed pass lets no rider down for four months.
- **Minor (6):**
  - Ch 3: the bar is taken from hooks after it was left leaning against the wall in ch 2.
  - Ch 16: the unlit lamp is placed at the stair head instead of the corridor.
  - Ch 17: Asha's death is dated "winter", against "the rains" in ch 12.
  - Ch 18: the shutters are called Devan's after he gave them to Neela in ch 16.
  - Ch 29: Devan expects letters and council news to travel through the sealed pass within days or weeks.
  - Ch 30: Neela misremembers the ch 3 shutter exchange, and Devan is off by one on the day.

Each error has an exact patch in errors.json.

## Noted, not flagged

- **Muthu's "monthly" letters:** they appear on two post days about twelve days apart (ch 12, ch 17). This is read as a deliberate clue to his reporting.
- **The ache:** it builds from scattered bare nights rather than a full moon without resin. This follows the bible's line that each skipped night "counts against her".
