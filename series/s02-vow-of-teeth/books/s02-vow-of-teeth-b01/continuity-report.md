# Continuity report: Vow of Teeth (Book 1, chapters 1–32)

## What I checked

I read all 32 chapters against the series bible, continuity.json (F001–F411) and the outline. The checks covered:

- **Names and roles:** sex, ages and who is called what by whom.
- **Timeline:**
  - the nights-left count, from forty down to the sealing;
  - the new-moon count from ch20 to ch25;
  - Muthu's seventh-day sparring requests;
  - the post days;
  - the Resin House ride and Muthu's arrival.
- **Magic rules:**
  - witness and caging rules, including that Janu saw only eyes and that the turning was hidden at the stair bend;
  - self-naming and its costs;
  - the wound-share, which runs left ribs, throat and wrists, and thumb;
  - the page burning while the intake slip survives;
  - the ache count.
- **Injuries:** the left-forearm graze, the left-ribs poison cut, the split nails and the torn resin scars.
- **Who knows what:** the Devan witness secret, Ittan's name, Vellan's shape, Muthu's tell and the inside informant.
- **Objects and places:** the sword, the knife, the bar and shutters, the kindi, the pebbles, the tooth, the slip, the seals and Ammu's resin pouch.
- **Relationship state:** the protections and the order they happened in, the first kiss, the first intimacy, the declaration and the vow.

## Errors found

- **E01 (major):** Chinna becomes "his" in ch14. She is a woman everywhere else.
- **E02 (major):** In ch17, "I won't answer that" is called the first time Devan said it to Neela. He refused her the same question in ch12, and used the words with her in ch3.
- **E03 (minor):** Ch16 says Neela missed the stone on the narrows night. Ch14 shows her leaving a late mark, and ch18's count agrees with ch14.
- **E04 (minor):** The knife returns without explanation after she gives it to Devan in ch17. Fixed in ch24, which also covers its use in ch25 and ch26.
- **E05 (minor):** The window shifts between three shutters and two leaves. Fixed in ch1 and ch3.
- **E06 (minor):** Raiu is first placed outside the hall, but the same chapter says he was on the chamber corridor all evening.
- **E07 (minor):** The place of Janu's "he used to have a partner" remark is misremembered in ch11.
- **E08 (minor):** In ch17 the sword lies "between them" rather than on Neela's side.
- **E09 (minor):** Deity references ("God", "Gods", temple, church) break the bible's no-deities setting.

## Checked and found consistent

- the nights-left and new-moon arithmetic;
- the order of Devan's protections;
- the ages stated before intimate scenes;
- the wound sides;
- Ammu's death staying permanent;
- Devan never lying to Neela;
- the revisions to Muthu's mother and the letters;
- the sealing-day logic with two impressions.
