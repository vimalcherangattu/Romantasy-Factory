# Continuity report: Vow of Teeth (book 1, chapters 1 to 32)

## What I checked

- **Names and titles:** servants, guards, riders, ponies.
- **Ages and dates:** ages and stated year-gaps (Neela 27, Devan 32, Ammu 25, Kirri 31, Asha dead at 22 six years ago, the ford eight years ago).
- **Night counts:** the night-count from 40 down through the new moon, the delayed sealing and the rains.
- **Magic rules:** the turning, witness, self-naming, ledger and wound-share rules against the bible.
- **Injuries:** Devan's left-forearm graze, his left-rib poison cut, Neela's shoulder cut, split nails, the torn resin scars and the shared welts.
- **Knowledge:** who knows what by chapter, especially Neela and Devan's view of Ittan, Muthu, Vellan and Ammu.
- **Objects:** bangle, sword, knife, tooth, pebbles, the beeswax impressions, Asha's token, seals, the slip and the oilcloth bundle.
- **Places:** the layout of the fort, stairs, ramparts, the grove, the cache, and the Resin House kennels.
- **Relationship:** the steps of the relationship and the protections Devan put in place.

## Errors found (9)

- **E01, ch 14, timeline:** the narrows night leaves the stone unmarked, but ch 16 and ch 18 do not count it.
- **E02, ch 14, knowledge:** Neela "remembers" Devan saying one tally was not enough. He never said it.
- **E03, ch 16, name:** the fort's shutter theories contradict ch 10 and ch 31.
- **E04, ch 17, object:** Sudhi is in "royal blue" here but "palace green" in ch 12.
- **E05, ch 22, object:** Devan's grey pebble should be Kannan's white one.
- **E06, ch 22, knowledge:** Neela knows Ittan proposed the match before anyone tells her.
- **E07, ch 28, object:** Ammu could not have cached her cloth at the tree line before dusk.
- **E08, ch 30, timeline:** the hounds are kennelled "within a few nights" here, but from the second night elsewhere.
- **E09, ch 17, knowledge:** Neela claims to have seen Muthu's grey letters three times. This is her first.

## Watched, not flagged

- **The ache:** the bible says it comes after a full moon without resin. The book shows it building from scattered skipped nights plus rationing (ch 16 "rationed"). This reads as acceptable but should stay consistent in book 2.
- **Muthu's letter rhythm:** his "once a month" letters fall on consecutive posts in ch 12 and ch 17. This sits behind his lies and is left as texture.
- **Devan's warden tenure:** he has been warden five years, but Asha was his second "at the fort" six years ago and Ittan has written every season for six years. Book 2 should state Devan's post before he became warden.
- **Ch 31 to 32 packet:** how Muthu's grey packet ends up in the guardroom is not explained.
