# Continuity report: Vow of Teeth (book 1, chapters 1–32)

## What was checked

- **Names:** names, titles and ages, checked against the bible and continuity.json.
- **Timeline:** the nights-left count, new-moon and sealing arithmetic, eight- and six-year anchors, and Neela's and Ammu's childhood ages.
- **World rules:** the turning, witness and self-naming rules, including who sees which turning, the forced resin and the wound-share.
- **Injuries:** which side and which arm for the arrow, the poison cut, the torn wrists and the split nails.
- **Who knows what:** Devan's secret witnessing, Ittan's name, Vellan's name and shape, Muthu's letters, and Ammu's name.
- **Objects:** the sword, the knife handed over and returned, the bar and shutters, the pebbles, the seals and wax, the tooth, and the Valli cloth.
- **Relationship state:** the kiss, the first night, the vow and the dawn scene.

## Errors found (5, all minor)

- **E01:** Asha "came to the fort at nineteen" (ch 5, ch 12), but she was raised at Kettamala. The fix makes nineteen the age she took her officer's post.
- **E02:** The gift seal is said to have been "cut in half" (ch 32), but in ch 6 it was lifted off whole.
- **E03:** Ammu's Valli cloth is blue in ch 18 and ch 20, then brown in ch 26 and ch 28.
- **E04:** Devan "saw the scars by lamplight on the wedding night" (ch 5), but the lamp was out and he watched by starlight.
- **E05:** Neela knows on day one that Devan drills at dawn (ch 1). Later chapters say she learned it only from Janu.

## Items reviewed and left as they are

- **Day counts:** small off-by-one drifts in the nights-left count and the ninth-day post rhythm (ch 12 to ch 24). They are too fuzzy for readers to track.
- **Devan's honesty:** "Rain" at dusk under a clear sky is a non-answer, and ch 30 acknowledges it. "I don't know" is said to Muthu, not to Neela. Neither breaks the rule that he never lies to Neela.
- **Bible beat:** Chathu said he would smear the resin himself, but the hunters apply it in ch 27. This reads as a change of plan.
- **The knife:** ch 24 explains why Neela carries it again.
- **The cages:** 42 cages including Neela's, against 41 occupied cages, is consistent.
