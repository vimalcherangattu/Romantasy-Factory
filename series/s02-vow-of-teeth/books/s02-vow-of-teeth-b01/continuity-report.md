# Continuity report: Vow of Teeth (s02-vow-of-teeth-b01), chapters 1–32

## What I checked

- **Names and pronouns:** every named servant, guard and villager against the bible and the facts list, F036–F411.
- **Day count:**
  - Neela's nightly count of nights left. It runs 40 → 37 → 35 → 34 → 32 → 31 → 30 → 29 → 28 → 20 → 19 → 17 → 16 → 15, then the new-moon count 9 → 8 → 7 → 6 → 5, then the sealing.
  - Days since the wedding: 6 days in ch 7, 20 days in ch 15, five weeks in ch 26.
  - The post cycle every ninth day and the seventh-day sparring requests.
  - The ride times: Kodiyur is four days away (the writ returns on about day 8), and the Resin House is one day away.
- **Magic rules:**
  - Who witnessed which turning: Janu saw only eyes, Devan did not see Vellan turn on the stair, and Muthu never sees a turning.
  - The self-naming conditions in ch 27.
  - The ledger entry burning while the intake slip survives.
  - Wound-share limits: Devan carries her torn scars, and she carries his rib cut and his thumb prick.
  - The ache, nails splitting after the run of bare nights.
- **Injuries:**

  - The old ford seam on his ribs.
  - Neela's shoulder cut.
  - Her split nails.

- **Objects and places:** the sword hilt, the knife, the pebbles, the kindi, the beeswax impressions and Asha's token, the green wax seal, the cache under the second spur, the west stair layout and the shutters.
- **Relationship steps:** each change matches its outline chapter (kiss in 19, first intimacy in 22, declarations in 30, vows in 31).
- **Character rules:** Devan's "never lies to Neela".

## Errors found

1. **E01 (ch 5) and E02 (ch 8):** Devan tells Neela two plain lies. One is the made-up errand to see Bhaskaran, the other is "Oil." Both break his fixed rule.
2. **E03 (ch 13) and E04 (ch 14):** Chinna is called "he". She is a woman everywhere else.
3. **E05 (ch 17):** Devan says she kept the third slat silent on the wedding night. In ch 1 she made it creak on purpose.
4. **E06 (ch 20):** The lie to Ammu is called her "first lie", but she had already lied to Ammu in ch 18.
5. **E07 (ch 24–26):** The guild knife Neela gave Devan in ch 17 reappears in her hand with no return scene. The fix sits in ch 24.
6. **E08 (ch 7):** Unni's age does not fit a recruit on Asha's slate nine years ago.
7. **E09 (ch 11):** Neela misremembers where Janu mentioned the old kalari partner.
8. **E10 (ch 16):** The narrows night is counted as a bare cup, against the late mark made in ch 14 and Ammu's count in ch 18.

- Empty categories on F237–F243 and F364–F366 have been filled in.

## New facts established by this book

- **Self-naming (ch 27):** Neela self-named to Devan at dusk in the Resin House yard. Her witness entry burned, so she is rogue. The bond is mutual, and a caging now needs both voices.
- **Losses:** Ammu died (ch 27) and is buried at the edge of the Blackbark Wood (ch 28). Raiu died on the west stair (ch 25).
- **Vellan and Chathu:**
  - Devan struck Vellan's bear shoulder, his first blow in eight years. Vellan fled with a dragging foreleg.
  - Chathu fled with the ledger and the intake slip. By ch 32 he is back on the Resin House step holding the book.
- **The sealing (ch 29):**
  - Ittan was arrested on sealing day with 36 pre-waxed blanks and his tally book.
  - The pass was closed with the warden's seal alone. Neela's name stands as the household witness on the crown writ.
  - The monsoon cut Kettamala off for about four months.
- **Love and vows:** Neela and Devan declared love (ch 30) and remarried in daylight (ch 31). She turned on purpose in front of him and let him see the dawn return.
- **The queen's seal (ch 32):**
  - The payment writ for Devan's contract carries the queen's sunbird seal. The seal matches the wedding-gift wax, chip included.
  - The writ, the guild chit and the cut gift seal are hidden in the shutter frame.
- **Muthu:**
  - His grey packet is locked unopened in the hall strongbox.
  - He carried the fodder run and saw Kirri lie on the cloth.
- **Who knows about Neela's guild link:** Janu, Muthu and Eravi heard Neela speak with inside knowledge of the Resin House (ch 25). Muthu also saw the yard aftermath at dawn.
- **Devan's father** was warden before him and wore the iron seal.
