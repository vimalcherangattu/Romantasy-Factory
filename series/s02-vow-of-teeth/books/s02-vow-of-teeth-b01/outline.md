# Vow of Teeth

**Book 1 of The Odiyan Contracts. Anya Kalloor.**

**Logline:** An assassin who becomes a leopard by night is sold as a bride to the border warden she is paid to kill. On their wedding night he watches her change, and now one word from his mouth can cage her for life.

**Central question:** Will Neela kill the husband she was sold to?

**Answer (chapters 27 and 30):** No. She turns in front of him by choice, gives him the word, and hands him the leopard tooth she was meant to leave in his wound.

## Reader-complaint pass (fixed)

1. **"Why doesn't she kill him on night one?"**
   - Fix: the contract forbids the kill before the marriage writ returns sealed (chapter 1, clue C26). This matters to the buyer because the widow must legally hold the pass-seal.
   - Once the writ arrives, Devan's public habit of barring the shutters at dusk makes any leopard kill an obvious inside job (chapter 10).
2. **"Why doesn't he cage or report her?"**
   - Caged, she tells nothing, and he wants the buyer.
   - His false belief is that power is only safe if never used.
   - He states both reasons in chapter 2.
3. **"Why doesn't she flee?"** Chathu holds her page, and Ammu's page is in the same ledger. Fleeing means forced resin and a caging (chapter 7).
4. **"The misunderstanding is artificial."** Her misreading of his silence is rational: every witness she has known used the word as a leash. The midpoint tests it openly, and she refuses to trust it for a stated reason.
5. **"Why didn't Ammu tell her the loophole sooner?"**
   - Speaking of it is guild treason.
   - It makes the odiyan rogue and hunted.
   - Ammu believes hiding keeps them alive.
   - She tells it only when Neela will be caged anyway (chapter 27).
6. **"Neela should connect Asha's death to her lookout job."** Devan refuses to tell her how or where Asha died ("I won't answer that", chapter 12). The bear marks in chapter 25 are seen only by poisoned Devan. The connection stays series-level.
7. **"Repetitive romantic refusals."** Each chapter's romance change below names a new reason.
8. **"Magic bent at the climax."** The forced resin turns her regardless, so the choice is to turn facing him and self-name. Muthu stays at the gate and never sees the turning.

## Chapter 1: The Kasavu Bride

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** survive her own wedding unseen and map her target's chamber.
- **Value shift:** anonymous instrument -> committed to a kill she cannot yet make.
- **Beats:**
  - Opens mid-rite at sunset on the Kettamala rampart. Neela, in kasavu, counts four exits while Devan closes a plain gold bangle over the ridged resin scar on her left wrist.
  - Her inner tally sets the society:
    - This is her twelfth contract, as ledger number Seventeen.
    - Release means her page burned and her birth name returned.
    - The Monkey Pass closes in about forty days.
  - Janu fusses within earshot that the warden is 32 and too thin, and the bride 27 and too quiet.
  - At the feast she collects names: Kannan the goat-boy, Chinna in the kitchen, Raiu on the corridor. Devan notices she thanks Raiu by name and asks how she learned it so fast. She answers with a question.
  - Contract terms surface in Chathu's soft remembered voice:
    - The warden dies in his chamber like a leopard kill.
    - "Not before the marriage writ comes back sealed from Kodiyur, and not after the rains."
    - She carries the guild's leopard tooth to leave in the wound.
  - In the chamber Devan answers her questions with exact facts and no comfort. He lays his sword on the floor by her side of the bed, hilt toward her, and says only that he sleeps badly.
  - She counts three hundred of his breaths, crosses to the west window, opens the teak shutters (the third slat creaks), uncorks the resin and smears throat and wrists.
  - Ends as the burn takes her and her spine begins to bend.
- **Reader question out:** Was he really asleep?
- **Techniques:** 1, 5, 7, 16

## Chapter 2: What the Warden Saw

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** learn what the council has put in his bed.
- **Value shift:** suspicion -> he holds a weapon he refuses to use.
- **Beats:**
  - Devan lies awake under lowered lids, expecting a knife. Instead he watches the whole turning:
    - the minute of burn;
    - spotted pelt rising over bone;
    - a leopard flowing over the broad ledge into the dark.
  - He knows the witness rule from border training and lays it out exactly:
    - He is her witness now.
    - The word cages her only while she is in shape, before dawn, in her hearing.
    - Intent doesn't matter.
  - Memory of Asha: bear wounds inside walls no bear could climb, an odiyan under contract. Rage pulls him to wait on the ledge and say the word when she returns.
  - He weighs the case against it:
    - Caged, she tells nothing.
    - Muthu would want her dead.
    - The council chose her, so whoever sent her may be the man selling tokens.
    - Under it all is his belief that power is only safe if he never uses it.
  - He holds the third slat silent and watches the dawn return: shaking, hollow, a woman pulling her sari close on the ledge. He shuts his eyes before she looks his way.
  - At the cistern chain's dawn clank he asks Janu to leave rice and plantains in the chamber, claiming his own hunger.
- **Reader question out:** What will he do with the word?
- **Techniques:** 13, 2, 15

## Chapter 3: Closed Shutters

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** learn the fort's rhythm and find a clean route to the kill.
- **Value shift:** confident -> blocked by an obstacle she reads as habit.
- **Beats:**
  - The morning hunger shakes her hands. She assumes the waiting rice is Janu's doing, eats too fast, and hates being seen eating.
  - Janu tours her through the fort: pepper terraces, the cistern and its hourly chain, the sunken kalari, the padippura. Neela collects guard rotations and names.
  - On the terrace she asks Devan direct questions. He answers literally. To "Why did your council choose me?" he says, "I won't answer that." Their first real banter: she can't make him lie, and it unsettles her.
  - At dusk she returns to find him closing and barring the west shutters. "Rain," he says. The sky is clear. She files it as a fussy habit.
  - After he sleeps she unbars them to go out, and realises she must bar them again before dawn or he'll know.
  - Ends at first light as she slides the bar home. The third slat creaks, and she can't tell if he stirred.
- **Reader question out:** Is the barred shutter a habit or a trap?
- **Techniques:** 1, 9, 13

## Chapter 4: Kennelled

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** test the household's night defences.
- **Value shift:** obstacle -> opportunity, then unease.
- **Beats:**
  - Kannan grumbles that the warden has ordered the hounds kennelled every night "since the bride came". The yard is open at night for the first time in years.
  - Neela argues it both ways in her head. It is either a careless gift to an assassin or bait laid for her.
  - A patrol rides in with a dead raider's satchel. In the hall Devan breaks a pass-token open and states flatly, in front of Muthu and the men, that the wax is genuine council wax.
  - She watches him calm a furious sergeant without raising his voice. The competence stirs something she files as threat assessment.
  - She learns his goal: someone on the queen's council sells the passes. Asked who, he says, "If I knew, he'd be in a cell."
  - That night she crosses the dogless yard as the other one. She stops below his window, aware she wants to know who he is hunting.
- **Reader question out:** Who on the council sells the passes, and why did the warden empty the yard?
- **Techniques:** 4, 11, 13

## Chapter 5: The Man Who Doesn't Spar

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** find out who sent her without showing that he knows.
- **Value shift:** she is an instrument -> she may be a person.
- **Beats:**
  - Dawn drill alone in the kalari. Muthu, loud and cheerful, begs to spar as he does every week, and Devan refuses as he has for eight years. Muthu's joke covers how much it bothers him.
  - Devan walks Neela along the pepper terraces and tests her cover. She grades pepper like a Chirayil ward but meets every personal question with a question, and he notices the pattern.
  - He glimpses the ridged scar under the bangle and says nothing.
  - She stops to bind Kannan's goat-bitten hand and knows the boy's mother's name. Private face: Devan remembers Asha learning every recruit's name, and has to walk away.
  - At supper Janu starts a leopard story. Devan sees Neela's knuckles whiten on her cup.
  - He decides to let her try, to watch who she reports to. Quietly, he tells Muthu to pull the night sentry off the west wall below his window, giving no reason.
- **Reader question out:** Will his patience protect her or trap her?
- **Techniques:** 13, 25, 1

## Chapter 6: Green Wax

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** find the household's weak hour.
- **Value shift:** sure of her ground -> shaken by a word she tells herself is chance.
- **Beats:**
  - Neela and Janu unpack wedding gifts: a brass lamp from the Chirayil house, and a sandalwood box from the palace sealed in the queen's green wax. Devan cuts the seal and studies it longer than the silver betel set inside.
  - She asks about the gift. He says plainly that he doesn't trust gifts from the palace. It is the first distrust they share.
  - Janu complains a cat has been at the pepper store and wants the hounds loose again. Devan: "Leave the cat. It keeps the rats down." Neela's hand stops on the box lid.
  - She reasons it away: a real cat, a real store.
  - Night as the other one, she runs the hill paths. She finds the guild's meeting stone and a fresh jackal scent-mark. Ammu is close.
  - At dawn she meets the actual pepper-store cat, a scruffy tom, and laughs out loud.
  - Ends with a jackal's call from the tree line: contact tomorrow.
- **Reader question out:** Did he mean the cat?
- **Techniques:** 11, 13, 20

## Chapter 7: There's a Way, Chechi

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** report to the guild without giving away more than she must.
- **Value shift:** alone -> held by her sister, and given a door she can't open.
- **Beats:**
  - Market day below the fort. Ammu, disguised as a pepper trader's girl, is humming her fishing song, calls Neela chechi and teases her about the handsome husband.
  - Ammu passes Chathu's message:
    - The writ is on the road.
    - After it arrives, every day counts.
    - Failure means forced resin and Chathu reading Neela's page aloud at dusk.
  - Ammu has written three letters to Kirri, the released sister, and none has been answered. She jokes that Kirri is busy with her fishing boat. Neela hears the strain under it.
  - Neela: "If anyone ever saw me, I'd be finished." Ammu: "Not always. There's a way, chechi, but it costs—" She stops herself because she has spotted Muthu at the next stall, and this is guild treason.
  - Devan arrives to escort Neela home, and she suspects he followed. On the road he quietly clears a soldier's widow's salt debt. Asked why, he says only that the man died on his patrol.
- **Reader question out:** What way, and what does it cost?
- **Techniques:** 2, 7, 5

## Chapter 8: The Unlit Lamp

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** narrow the token-seller and learn why the council wanted this marriage.
- **Value shift:** watching her -> realising the marriage itself may be the weapon.
- **Beats:**
  - Devan orders the corridor lamp outside his door left unlit after dusk, telling Raiu it saves oil. In truth he wants no light under the door when she slips back at dawn.
  - He and Muthu compare token wax impressions. Only three council seals could make this mark. Muthu laughs it off as clerks' carelessness.
  - At noon on the rampart she asks why he never laughs first. He says he waits to learn whether the joke is at someone's expense. She makes one at her own, and he laughs.
  - He admits nothing about following her to market, and she doesn't ask.
  - A courier brings the marriage writ back sealed from Kodiyur. Reading it, Devan sees what it means: as his lawful wife, if he dies she holds Kettamala's pass-seal until a new warden is named.
  - Ends as he looks up and sees Neela's face go still at the sight of the writ.
- **Reader question out:** Who wants his widow holding the pass-seal?
- **Techniques:** 3, 13, 23

## Chapter 9: The Goat Path

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** study his patrol habits on the road.
- **Value shift:** contempt for his caution -> respect for his skill.
- **Beats:**
  - At supper Janu begins a story about a man-eating leopard of the pass. Devan says quietly, "Not tonight, Janu," and she stops. Neela decides he simply dislikes stories.
  - The council expects the bride shown to the pass villages, so Devan takes her down to inspect the lower waystation.
  - The langurs fall silent: strangers on the road. A rockfall blocks the cart road.
  - Neela leads them down a goat path she knows without a map, and she cannot say how she knows it. He asks, and she answers with a question.
  - At the waystation he reads the raider tracks and she reads the boot-wear. Neither could do the other's half.
  - Riding home at dusk without resin, she is human beside him under the first heavy clouds, and lets herself enjoy it for one mile.
- **Reader question out:** How does she know the pass?
- **Techniques:** 1, 11, 6

## Chapter 10: The Broken Beak

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** plan the kill now that the writ is sealed.
- **Value shift:** a window open -> a plan he has quietly made impossible.
- **Beats:**
  - Neela walks the west stair to map an approach. Her hand closes on the parrot newel's broken beak and her stomach drops. She counts the steps and cannot stop counting.
  - Janu mentions that the warden has barred his shutters every dusk since the wedding, never once before, and the whole fort knows it.
  - Neela sees the problem. A leopard kill inside a barred room points straight at the bride. Prediction bait: she lists whether he did it on purpose or by chance.
  - Night: as the other one she comes through the shutters she unbarred and stands over him. His hand lies open on the blanket, old scars on the knuckles. The kindi on her side has been filled.
  - She cannot make the kill look like a beast, and tells herself that is the only reason. She leaves.
  - Ends at dawn with a new plan: the kalari, before first light, human hands, marma.
- **Reader question out:** Can she kill him with her hands?
- **Techniques:** 4, 11, 13

## Chapter 11: Dawn Drill

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** kill him in the kalari with a marma strike.
- **Value shift:** resolved killer -> a woman who failed on purpose.
- **Beats:**
  - Before dawn she takes the path to the kalari through lantana. The smell of crushed leaves makes her gag, a hatred she has never understood.
  - She watches him drill, faster and deadlier than anyone she has seen, striking only air.
  - She steps down into the pit and asks for a form. He refuses to spar. She offers to correct his stance instead, and he allows it.
  - Her fingers rest at the base of his skull, on the killing point, clean. She counts three breaths. She lifts her hand and tells him his guard drops on the left.
  - He says, "You'd have had me." She says, "Yes." Neither explains.
  - She climbs out shaking and lies to herself in the chamber about why.
- **Reader question out:** Why did she pull it, and why did he let her stand there?
- **Techniques:** 16, 18, 13

## Chapter 12: Parrots

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** decide what a woman who pulled her strike is worth to him.
- **Value shift:** a tool to watch -> a will to bargain with.
- **Beats:**
  - Devan revises his view. She had him and didn't kill him. A woman with a will can be bargained with.
  - Janu asks him at last to clear Asha's chest. He finds her notes, each signed with a drawn parrot, and the first forged token Asha found. Its wax matches the new tokens.
  - Neela finds him there. He tells her about Asha:
    - his second at the fort;
    - fearless with soldiers;
    - afraid of the dark;
    - killed six years ago under contract.

    To "How?" he says, "I won't answer that."
  - In his head, unsaid, are the west stair and the bear wounds. The reader holds what Neela does not.
  - Neela reads one of Asha's notes and laughs at the right line, and asks what Asha would want now. He answers truly and feels, for a moment, less alone with her.
  - That evening he sees Muthu hand a plain-sealed letter to the palace rider. Muthu laughs and says it's for his mother, and Devan believes him.
  - Ends with Asha's line in her hand, "the truth protects whoever finds it". He decides to offer the bargain.
- **Reader question out:** What will he ask for, and what will he give?
- **Techniques:** 13, 10, 11, 25

## Chapter 13: The Cat on the Sill

- **POV:** Devan, with one Neela scene
- **Goal (Devan):** survive a fever without speaking the word.
- **Goal (Neela):** keep him alive though his death would suit her.
- **Value shift:** hidden guard -> guard held by a bitten tongue.
- **Beats:**
  - Devan rides in with a raider arrow-graze gone bad. By evening he has a fever, and the fort physician bleeds him and despairs.
  - Neela takes over. She knows the marma healing points and how to clean a wound, and she tells Janu what to boil. Her guild report is due tonight.
  - Through fever Devan watches her take resin at dusk and turn again. The leopard settles on the sill to keep watch.
  - Janu, at the bedside, sees eyes on the ledge and cries out about an animal. Burning, Devan feels the word on his tongue. He starts it ("the l—") and bites down until he tastes blood. "It's the cat. Leave the cat. Draw the curtain." Janu sees only eyes, not a turning, and is no witness.
  - Neela's scene, in shape: she hears him rave about a cat and thinks nothing of it. The reader knows she just lived through a caging by a hair.
  - At dawn she turns back on the ledge while he sleeps. The fever breaks. She sits hollow on the floor by the bed and does not leave.
- **Reader question out:** How long can he keep the word behind his teeth?
- **Techniques:** 13, 6, 9, 7

## Chapter 14: Varanad Coin

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** find out who is hunting the patrol.
- **Value shift:** neutral to his survival -> his protector, at the cost of her way out.
- **Beats:**
  - Devan insists on riding the dawn patrol through the lower narrows while still weak. Muthu argues, and Neela hears the route.
  - At dusk, as the other one, she scents two raider scouts camped above the narrows with bows and a signal mirror, waiting for dawn.
  - Her choice: if he dies in an ambush she is free of the job without blame. She kills both scouts. The animal's hunger rises and she refuses it.
  - Their satchel holds Varanad-minted silver and a forged token. She drags it onto the patrol path where it will be found.
  - She turns back at dawn among the rocks, freezing, and walks home. From the terrace she watches his patrol find the mauled scouts, the coin and the token. He looks up at the hills for a long time.
  - At supper he says the pepper-store cat has been busy. She can't tell if he is joking.
- **Reader question out:** Why would raiders carry Varanad coin, and does he know who saved him?
- **Techniques:** 18, 11, 6

## Chapter 15: First Storm

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** get home before first light.
- **Value shift:** hidden -> seen, and offered a leash.
- **Beats:**
  - The monsoon's first storm breaks while she tracks couriers far down the ridge as the other one. The rain wipes out scent, and she misjudges the hour.
  - She reaches the ledge as dawn takes her, half-turned, fur sloughing to skin. Devan is sitting on the bed, awake, watching the whole return.
  - He tells her he saw her on the wedding night. He has been her witness for twenty days.
  - She counts her witnesses: Chathu, Ammu, him. Each could cage her. She reaches under the mattress for the knife, and he does not move.
  - He offers the bargain, once: his silence for the name of whoever bought his death.
  - She refuses. She doesn't know the name, and if she did she wouldn't buy her life from a man who can take it with one word.
  - He says, "Then we both know where we stand," and goes to drill. She sits shaking on the sill with the rain blowing in.
- **Reader question out:** Now that he holds the word openly, will he use it?
- **Techniques:** 12, 16, 3, 15

## Chapter 16: Terms Withdrawn

- **POV:** Devan, then Neela (scene break)
- **Goal (Devan):** decide what kind of man made that offer.
- **Goal (Neela):** find the trap in his gift.
- **Value shift:** master and captive -> a gift she cannot trust.
- **Beats:**
  - Devan stands in the kalari and cannot drill. He hears his offer in a guild master's voice. He made himself her master, the thing he has sworn never to be.
  - At dusk he says it aloud: the bargain was the guild's leash in his mouth. He withdraws it with no terms. His silence is hers, freely.
  - Neela's scene: she asks why. "Because I don't want to own anyone."
  - She rereads the first half:
    - the shutters barred at dusk so no servant saw her turn;
    - the hounds kennelled so they wouldn't bay at a leopard;
    - "the cat";
    - the unlit lamp;
    - Janu's stories stopped;
    - rice after every hungry morning.
  - Her life allows one conclusion: a hunter's patience, saving the word for when she is most useful caged.
  - Still, as an experiment, she sleeps that night without resin, human, her back to him, the knife untouched.
- **Reader question out:** Is his gift mercy or patience?
- **Techniques:** 17, 12, 20, 3

## Chapter 17: The Keeper's Letter

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** learn to live beside a witness who claims no rights.
- **Value shift:** cover identities -> truthful adversaries.
- **Beats:**
  - New terms of living: in the dark they talk plainly. She asks, and he answers or says "I won't answer that."
  - Councillor Ittan's letter arrives, genial and wordy, every line "my friend". He will come himself to seal the pass for the monsoon. He asks after the warden's bride, "such a comfort, should anything befall the fort".
  - Devan reads it aloud. Neela marks the phrase. Devan lists his suspects: three council seals, and Muthu's palace rider.
  - She asks why he hasn't asked for the name again. "I said no terms."
  - A jackal's call: report tomorrow. About seventeen days remain.
  - She lies awake wanting to give him something she doesn't have.
- **Reader question out:** Is the genial Keeper a friend, or the hand on the knife?
- **Techniques:** 14, 4, 10

## Chapter 18: Kirri's Anklet

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** keep Ammu satisfied without exposing him.
- **Value shift:** loyal to her sister -> lying to her for him.
- **Beats:**
  - At the meeting stone by day, Ammu is sharper than before. Chathu is impatient.
  - Ammu remembers that Kirri swore she'd send her silver anklet as a sign once she had her boat. It never came. Ammu's fear shows through her chatter.
  - Neela does not tell Ammu that Devan is her witness. It is her first lie to her sister, told to protect him. Ammu senses it, pushes, and gets questions back.
  - Ammu states her creed: they're safe as long as nobody but them has seen. Neela can't argue, because it has kept them alive.
  - Evening: Janu teaches the fort's pebble game, seven wells. Neela cheats, Devan catches her and is delighted, and cheats back. The hall laughs and Muthu roars.
  - Ends with Neela alone, booking the lie and the laugh as the same debt.
- **Reader question out:** Where is Kirri?
- **Techniques:** 15, 23, 2, 14

## Chapter 19: Resin on the Rampart

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** hunt for Ammu's report.
- **Value shift:** wanting -> kissed, and caught.
- **Beats:**
  - Ammu demands a night report. At sunset on the east rampart, where she means to go over the wall, Neela smears resin on throat and wrists. She has about a minute.
  - Devan steps out of the cistern housing. They talk about Asha's fear of the dark and about Neela's counting. He says he'd like to know what she counts.
  - She kisses him, knowing the clock. The rule makes it dangerous three ways:
    - The turning starts in front of him if she stays.
    - Ammu watches from the tree line.
    - One careless word from him could cage her.
  - At the first burn she breaks away mid-kiss and runs for the stair. He doesn't follow, and she notices.
  - She turns in the stair's shadow. From the wall as the leopard she sees a jackal's eyes at the tree line. Ammu saw.
- **Reader question out:** What will Ammu do with what she saw?
- **Techniques:** 7, 8, 22, 9

## Chapter 20: The Name Given Back

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** keep Ammu from reporting, and make the nights safe.
- **Value shift:** the word as her burden -> the word as his responsibility.
- **Beats:**
  - Ammu confronts her: "You kissed the mark." It is fear for Neela, not reproach. Ammu will report nothing for one more week.
  - Ammu hums, then sings a verse of an old tappers' song about "the name given back at the closing of day". Neela asks what it means. Ammu says it's only a song and changes the subject.
  - Ammu warns that Chathu sends Vellan if there's no kill by the new moon, about day 35.
  - That night in the dark Neela asks Devan to promise never to say it. He won't promise what depends on sleep or fever. Instead he shows her the old watchman's trick, a smooth river pebble in the cheek, and keeps one there every resin night from now on.
  - She laughs, then stops. No one has ever made the word their problem instead of hers.
- **Reader question out:** What name, given back to whom?
- **Techniques:** 9, 2, 14

## Chapter 21: The Captain at Twenty-Four

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** answer her honestly when she finally asks.
- **Value shift:** guarded -> wanting her at his back.
- **Beats:**
  - Rain-locked day. They trade a question for a question, each costing more.
  - She asks why he won't spar. At 24 a raider captain surrendered, knelt and spat at him. Devan struck one marma point in anger and killed him. He has struck no living person since.
  - She doesn't absolve him. She names exactly what he did and that he stopped, and he feels understood rather than forgiven.
  - Her disclosure: a number, not a name. Seventeen. She learns every servant's name because no one ever learned hers.

## Chapter 22: A Night Without Resin

  - Morning at the stone: Ammu covered the missed signal by going out as the jackal herself, so the guild's watcher logged both. It was a reckless dare.

## Chapter 25: Claw Marks

## Chapter 26: The Kennels

## Chapter 27: Leopard

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** live free, and keep him alive.
- **Value shift:** about to be caged -> uncageable, and bereaved.
- **Beats:**
  - Before sunset Chathu's hunters force the resin onto her throat and wrists. The scars tear and bleed, and she will turn at dusk whatever she does.
  - Ammu unlocks the kennel and cuts her bonds, a reckless dare. She gives back the antidote vial and tells her the self-naming loophole:
    - once in a life;
    - to one person who is looking at her;
    - during the dusk turning;
    - it voids every other witness;
    - the pair share wounds;
    - she goes rogue.

    "I didn't tell you because it costs, chechi, and because hiding kept us alive."
  - Devan rides in, grey with poison, held in the saddle by Muthu. Muthu stays to fight at the gatehouse and never sees the yard. Neela pours the antidote into Devan's mouth.
  - Sunset. Chathu steps out with her page. Neela turns to face Devan, and as the burn takes her she says in her human voice, to him, "Leopard."
  - Chathu reads the page aloud, and nothing happens. The witness entry blackens and burns in his hands.
  - Vellan, as the bear, charges the downed Devan and the leopard. Devan strikes his first blow at a living creature in eight years, a marma strike to the bear's shoulder that breaks the charge.
  - Ammu turns jackal and draws Vellan and the hunters into the trees. Neela, voiceless in shape, hears the spear and the jackal's last cry.
  - She cannot turn back before dawn. She lies across Devan in the mud and does not leave.
- **Reader question out:** What is left of them at first light?
- **Techniques:** 18, 19, 24, 12, 22

## Chapter 30: The Tooth
