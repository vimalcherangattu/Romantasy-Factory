## Errors found

All five are minor.

- **E01 (ch 2):** Neela is shown with a kalari habit, but ch 5 says she has been in the pit only once.
- **E02 (ch 5):** The wound-share location doesn't match. Her welt is on the back of her knuckle; his blood is on the heel of his hand.
- **E03 (ch 8):** Neela arrives on the ledge already wearing a cloth, though she came back in leopard shape.
- **E04 (ch 9):** Neela is said to be sleeping "in the hall" when she is in the chamber.
- **E05 (ch 30/32):** Rema's refusal of the green quire is referred to in ch 32 but never shown.

No major breaks were found. Devan never lies to Neela, Neela never says the word aloud, and every intimate beat states both ages first.
