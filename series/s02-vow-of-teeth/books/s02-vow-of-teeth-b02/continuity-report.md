## Errors found (6, all minor)

- **E01 (ch 4):** "for a year" of grey letters should be six years.
- **E02 (ch 5):** Janu told Neela about the eight years in the first week, not the second.
- **E03 (ch 7):** The narration implies Neela doesn't know the tappers' song past the first verses, but she learned the last verse from Ammu.
- **E04 (ch 25):** The garrison is called "Kettamala brown"; it is grey elsewhere.
- **E05 (ch 25):** "Never seen that face by day" contradicts the ch 7 ridge sighting.
- **E06 (ch 32):** Vellan's explanation of the night-guard scars does not account for First Guard's smooth wrist seam.

No major errors found. The midpoint reveal, the ledger bargain, Vellan's caging threat and the handover readings all respect the world rules.
