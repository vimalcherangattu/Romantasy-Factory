# Continuity report: Oath of Claws (book 2, chapters 1–32)

## What I checked

I read all 32 chapters against the series bible, continuity.json, the outline and the book 1 summary. The checks covered:

- **Names and ages:** Neela 27, Devan 32, Muthu 35, Rema 58, Janu 61, Thilak 41, Pavi 24.
- **Timeline:** the smear count, the days from Rema's arrival to the execution, the raid falling on the dark of the moon, nights spent at the Resin House, and the date of the queen's writ against the closing writ's rider.
- **World rules:** witnesses, pages read by daylight, forced versus willing turning, the wound-share running both ways (calf, thigh, knuckles, palms, ribs), and the ache.
- **Who knows what:** Muthu's letters and the post-key, the lookout leaf, Eravi's informing, and Vellan's witness.
- **Objects:** the pebbles, the ledger halves, the green quire, the flasks, and the bundle behind the third slat.
- **Head-counts:** the raid party, the forty, and the kennel cages and pages (41 caged, 82 pages read).

## Errors found (11; 1 major, 10 minor)

- **E01 (minor):** Ch 4 gives the wrong age and years for when Devan met Muthu.
- **E02 (minor):** Ch 6 dates the first spent smear to a night Neela was human.
- **E03 (minor):** Ch 4 says Ittan's wife refuses bitter medicine, but she takes willow bark.
- **E04 (minor):** Ch 15 counts the raid party as eight including Bhaskaran; later chapters need nine.
- **E05 (minor):** Ch 16 says the newel was "touched once", which conflicts with ch 2 and ch 22.
- **E06 (minor):** Ch 18 counts one contract twice.
- **E07 (minor):** Ch 19 says "three nights" at the Resin House; it was two.
- **E08 (major):** Ch 19 misstates when Devan took the post-key and what Muthu knew.
- **E09 (minor):** Ch 27 shows Vellan with torn scars, which only a forced turning causes.
- **E10 (minor):** Ch 30 misplaces Kandan's "witch" remark in time and place.
- **E11 (minor):** Ch 28 and ch 32 disagree about the ledger's cut-away back half.

No content-rule problems were found. Both leads' ages are on the page before each explicit scene.
