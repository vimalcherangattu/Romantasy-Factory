# Continuity report: Oath of Claws (book 2, chapters 1–32)

## What I checked

All 32 chapters were checked against three sources: the series bible, continuity.json (F001–F672, including this book's provisional facts) and the book 1 summary. I also checked the book against itself. The areas covered were:

- names and titles
- the day-by-day timeline from nine days into the rains through Rema's stay, the raid, the assize and the ride to the shelter
- the resin count
- the wound-share rule
- injuries and which hand is bandaged
- headcounts
- who knows what
- objects (the cut seal, flasks, slips)
- places (the newel, grate, stairs and hall doors)
- relationship beats

Rule use generally holds: caging needs a witness voice at night, handovers are read by daylight, and Neela never says her own shape. Ages and adult status are stated before each intimate scene.

## Errors found (32)

**Major (2)**
- **E11:** Devan's shared welts bleed in ch 7, 15 and 16, against the book's own statement that the partner's welt weeps but does not open (ch 20, 27).
- **E12:** Neela turns deliberately in front of Devan in ch 7 and 8. This undercuts ch 13's beat of the first turning she invites him to watch.
