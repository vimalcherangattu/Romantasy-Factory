Hello, dear reader,

It's here. Book 2 of The Odiyan Contracts, Oath of Claws, is live today.

When we left Neela and Devan, the Monkey Pass had closed for the monsoon and they were married in truth. Now the rains hold the whole hill country shut, her resin is nearly gone, and the guild that made her is hunting her. Her plan is bold and a little reckless: steal the ledger that owns every odiyan, and give each page back to the person it names.

Devan wants one page from that book, for his sister. He is the husband who could cage her with a word and has sworn he never will. The danger in this one is what they find when they open the book, and whether trust can survive it.

You'll get a monsoon-night heist, a crown justice with strange guards, a marriage tested honestly, and heat that changes the bargain between them every time.

It's the book I most wanted to write, and I can't wait to hear what you think.
