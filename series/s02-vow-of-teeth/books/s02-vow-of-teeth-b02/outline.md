# Oath of Claws

**Series:** The Odiyan Contracts, Book 2. Pen name Anya Kalloor.

**Logline:** A hunted shapeshifter assassin and the warden she was sold to kill raid her guild for the ledger that owns every odiyan. The first contract she translates names her as the lookout at his sister's murder.

**Central question:** Can they steal the guild's ledger without becoming its new masters?

**Heroine belief:** She opens believing "What the guild made me do isn't mine to answer for." She ends believing "I owe repair even for what I was made to do." The proof comes in chapter 28: she reads every page aloud by daylight, Vellan's included, and makes herself no one's master. Chapter 23 (her confession to Janu) and chapter 29 (her testimony) prepare and confirm it.

**Love interest's own goal:** Devan wants to prove to the high court's Justice that Asha's murder and the pass sales were one conspiracy, and to keep his garrison loyal while he does it.

**Real loss:** The queen's sealed writ strips Devan of Kettamala (chapter 30). He never returns as warden. Kirri stays caged.

**Ending:** HFN. Neela and Devan ride into the hills as husband and wife with forty odiyans who each hold their own page. Chapter 32 opens the hinge: the queen's own guard are odiyans, and their pages sit in her crown leaf.

**New cast (all adults):**
- Justice Rema, 58, travelling justice of the high court. Honest and bound by law.
- The First Guard of her escort, age unstated but plainly a grown veteran. A series thread.
- Pavi, 24, odiyan, otter shape.
- Thilak, 41, odiyan, kite shape. Eldest of the forty.
- Corporal Kandan, garrison doubter.
- Pachan, a hunter, carried over from book 1.

**Regis's eight elements:**
- **Society defined, ch 1:** rogue status, the witness rule, the crown's right to hunt unvouched odiyans.
- **Meeting, ch 1:** their first morning as true spouses and allies, renewed on new terms.
- **Barrier, ch 2 to 5 and ch 16:** crown law inside the fort, then "Lookout: Seventeen".
- **Attraction, ch 5 and ch 12:** sparring as intimacy, then the bargain in the dark.
- **Declaration, ch 24 privately and ch 30 publicly:** "I choose you knowing."
- **Point of ritual death, ch 25 to 27.**
- **Recognition, ch 20 (the shared crown wound) and ch 28 (each refuses power).**
- **Betrothal, ch 31 to 32:** the vow of partners, and the HFN ride.

**Beat map (32 chapters):**
- **Ch 1 to 3:** society and meeting.
- **Ch 4 to 8:** Ittan's execution and the inciting threat.
- **Ch 9 to 15:** investigation, raid preparation and the raid.
- **Ch 16:** Midpoint Mirror.
- **Ch 17 to 24:** cost ledger, repair and the informant.
- **Ch 25 to 27:** ritual death.
- **Ch 28 to 30:** recognition, confrontation and loss.
- **Ch 31 to 32:** betrothal and the series hinge.

**Explicit scenes (open door, moderate, consensual, both leads adults: Neela 27, Devan 32, both restated before ch 12):**
- **Ch 12:** they make the mutual veto over the ledger.
- **Ch 24:** staying becomes a choice, not a reward for forgiveness.
- **Ch 31:** warden and bride become partners. The shutters stand open on purpose.

## Chapter 1: Nine Days of Rain

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Make a plan to take the ledger before her resin runs out.
- **Value shift:** safe for now -> on a clock

**Beats:**
- First light, nine days into the rains. Neela turns back on the warden's window ledge. The brazier-warmed cloth is waiting, and Devan has the river pebble in his palm. She counts what is left in Ammu's pouch: two smears.
- Society is shown at a cost. Her page burned. Her name went to Kodiyur on the closing writ by the last rider. The crown hunts unvouched odiyans. The welt on his wrist answers her torn scar.
- In the hall over Ittan's tally book, she can read some marks in guild shorthand. A small sunbird beside certain lines means nothing to her yet.
- They name their gap honestly. He needs the guild's contract for Asha as court evidence. She needs every page back in its owner's hands. They agree to go together and argue later.
- At Ittan's grille, Ittan asks only after his wife's medicine. Neela notes that he is afraid of something that isn't Devan.
- At dusk on the west rampart, the langurs on the closed pass road go silent. They do that only when strangers walk it, and nobody can walk it in the rains.

**Reader question out:** Who is on a road nobody can walk?

**Techniques:** 1, 5, 22

## Chapter 2: The Justice at the Gate

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** Keep Ittan alive to testify, and keep Neela out of crown sight.
- **Value shift:** master of his fort -> outranked inside it

**Beats:**
- At dawn, Bhaskaran's horn sounds. Justice Rema of the high court, 58, stands at the padippura with four escort in green sunbird sashes. They crossed by the goat path. Their oilcloth packs are dry and their boots barely worn.
- Rema presents the queen's warrant for Ittan: treason under the wartime charter. Devan says plainly that he means to put Ittan before the court as witness to Asha's murder. Rema grants an assize hearing in three days.
- The escort give numbers, not names. When the First Guard's cuff rides up, Devan sees a pale ridge on the inner wrist and reads it as a lamp-burn.
- Rema remarks that her escort fixed the ropes on the crossing by night, and that she never once saw them sleep.
- Devan tells Neela everything before she asks. She points out that hiding her by day looks like guilt, so they agree she will be seen and say only true things.
- Rema asks to meet the household witness who signed the closing writ, by name.

**Reader question out:** What will Neela say to a crown justice who can hang her?

**Techniques:** 11, 13, 16

## Chapter 3: Numbers, Not Names

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Get through Rema's questions without a lie, and learn who the escort are.
- **Value shift:** unseen -> on a crown record in person

**Beats:**
- Neela answers Rema in the hall as Kunjan Chirayil's ward. Every sentence is true, and she lets Rema infer the rest.
- She asks the escort their names, as she asks everyone. They give only numbers, and that chills her more than the warrant did.
- The hounds run the yard loose again. They whine and will not go near the escort's quarters.
- Eravi salves her split nails. The salve smells of scorched tamarind and pine sap. She tells herself many barks smell bitter.
- At night she finds Devan alone in the hall, practising his case to an empty bench, and doing it badly. She coaches him: say less, say only what you can prove.
- He asks what she would say if the court asked her what she is. She has no answer.

**Reader question out:** What does the escort's silence hide, and what will Neela say under oath?

**Techniques:** 1, 25, 11

## Chapter 4: Ask Your Captain

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** Get Ittan's sworn statement before the hearing.
- **Value shift:** a witness in hand -> a witness silenced

**Beats:**
- In the guardroom, Ittan refuses to swear to anything. He asks only that his wife's cough medicine be sent on, and Devan promises it.
- Devan offers to write to the widow's physicians himself. Ittan nearly breaks: "Ask your captain what he brought me." Then he falls silent.
- Devan asks Muthu. Muthu laughs, then goes quiet, which is his lying tell. Devan says nothing yet, because he needs Muthu at the gate through the hearing.
- In the warden's chamber, Neela suspects Muthu is the guild's man. Devan thinks Muthu is the queen's. Each argues their case.
- They decide together not to give Rema the sunbird payment writ hidden behind the third slat. The queen's justice cannot try the queen.
- In the last dark hour Unni knocks. Rema has moved the execution to this dawn under a charter clause, and the escort are already in the court.

**Reader question out:** Can Devan stop an execution without turning his garrison into mutineers?

**Techniques:** 13, 4, 15

## Chapter 5: Dawn Writ

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Help Devan delay the execution without exposing herself.
- **Value shift:** the case has a witness -> the witness is dead

**Beats:**
- In the courtyard in rain, Devan enters a formal objection under assize law before the garrison. Rema rules that the warrant outranks the hearing. Devan could stop it with sixty soldiers. He chooses not to make them traitors, and says so aloud.
- Ittan's last words are for his wife: keep the window open in rain. The escort carry out the sentence. Neela watches from the west rampart and looks at their hands, not the block.
- On the wind from the escort she smells nightbark, faint and old, and the hounds whine. She guesses they are guild hunters carrying resin to take rogues, and that they are here for her.
- At the kalari pit Devan cannot stop moving. Neela steps down and asks for a round. He spars with her by choice. She takes a point and lets him see her pleased.
- At dusk the escort walk out into the rain toward the pass. The First Guard glances at Neela's split nails and says, "It passes."
- Rema stays. She will hold assize until the pass opens, hearing the warden's case "and any other the crown has sealed". She sets a second sealed writ case on the table.

**Reader question out:** What is in the Justice's second sealed writ, and who were those guards?

**Techniques:** 4, 11, 9

## Chapter 6: The Ledger He Needs

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Agree with Devan on what the ledger is for.
- **Value shift:** shared plan -> shared plan with a fault line in it

**Beats:**
- Devan writes to Ittan's widow and sends the medicine. Neela watches him do a kindness for the man who bought his death.
- Rema tells Devan to bring the court paper the guild wrote, not his grief. The guild's contract books are in ordinary ink and could be evidence. The pages are lives.
- In the warden's chamber they fight honestly. He needs the ledger before the court. She says any court that holds it becomes a master. Both are right.
- They settle a first term: contract books to the court, pages to their owners. Neither knows yet what the contract books hold.
- She keeps the last smear of Ammu's resin rather than turn tonight. The ache is close.
- She tells him she has to rob Chathu's groves before she can rob Chathu.

**Reader question out:** Can a hunted rogue steal resin from the guild's own trees?

**Techniques:** 15, 10, 14

## Chapter 7: Blackbark in the Rain

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Steal fresh resin and find where Chathu keeps the ledger.
- **Value shift:** empty-handed -> armed with resin and a location

**Beats:**
- Between downpours they ride the wood road by day. She taps a nightbark tree, humming the tappers' song, and the knife slips. The cut on her palm rises as a welt on his. He hands her a cloth without a word.
- From a ridge they watch the Resin House. Forty members train in the yards. Hunters walk the walls. Vellan limps on his lamed leg. Kirri's kennel roof is visible below.
- At dusk Chathu crosses to the shuttered well house with the black book under his arm, and comes back without it.
- On the way out, the hunter Pachan finds them in daylight. He sees Neela's face and lowers his hooked pole: "I owe you a sweet." He lets them go.
- She rides home with a full flask. Ammu's pouch is empty for the first time, and she keeps it anyway.
- The book is in the well house, and getting it needs human hands at night, which she won't have.

**Reader question out:** How do they reach the book when she can only get there as a leopard?

**Techniques:** 6, 11, 14

## Chapter 8: Six Years of Letters

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** Plan the raid and keep the fort steady under the Justice's eye.
- **Value shift:** a trusted captain -> a captain named in front of the hall

**Beats:**
- Devan and Bhaskaran draw the Resin House from Neela's counts and exits. Devan notices she will not draw the kennels.
- She turns at dusk to scout the escort's trail toward the goat path. Devan holds Kannan's white pebble in his cheek all night, though nobody is watching him do it.

- At supper Rema courteously thanks Captain Muthu, before the whole hall, for six years of faithful letters to the queen's household.

- Muthu, white, says to Devan: "Seventh day tomorrow. Give me a round."

## Chapter 9: The Seventh Day

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** Learn the truth from Muthu.
- **Value shift:** suspicion -> a known traitor kept as captain

**Beats:**
- At the dawn kalari, Devan accepts the round. Between blows Muthu confesses to letters to the queen's household since the winter Asha died, asked of him "to keep the warden safe".
- Muthu admits the grey packet he carried into Ittan's cell held a household order: say nothing and your wife is kept. He did not know an execution would follow.
- Devan breaks the wax on the locked packet with Muthu beside him. Every report is confirmed, Neela's vow words included. There is nothing about the guild.
- Muthu swears he never wrote to any guild and never told anyone Neela's cart order. Devan says, "I won't answer whether I believe that." He takes the post-key and keeps Muthu as captain.
- That night Devan asks Neela how she would handle a man like this. It is the first time he has asked her about command. She says: give him a job he can't fake.
- Devan names Muthu to hold Kettamala during the raid, with Rema inside its walls.

**Reader question out:** If not Muthu, who in Kettamala talks to the guild?

**Techniques:** 12, 10, 16

## Chapter 10: Every Name in the Barracks

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Help hold the garrison while the fort whispers.
- **Value shift:** a strange bride -> a woman the men can name back

**Beats:**
- The barracks talk: the warden bowed to the crown, and the bride is odd. Corporal Kandan says she has bewitched him.
- Neela walks the barracks by day and learns every man's name and his mother's village, Asha's slate method. She wins Unni and Bhaskaran's lads, but not Kandan.
- In the kitchen she plays seven wells with Kannan and Chinna and cheats. Devan catches her and laughs, the first laugh since the execution.
- At the foot of the west stair, Janu says, "You walk round it like I do."
- Neela hears the eleventh step ring hollow and sees that the drain grate under it is newer iron than the stair.
- She cannot say why her feet go wide of the parrot newel, and she takes the long way up.

## Chapter 11: Sharp-Edged Silver

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Finish the raid plan and decide who carries the book out.
- **Value shift:** a plan -> a plan that puts every page in his hands for a night

**Beats:**
- Rema pays Parukutty the harvest death-gift early, in crown silver. Neela sees the sharp milled edge and the sprig, never handled, like the scouts' coin.
- Devan weighs one coin against the scouts' satchel. The crown pays widows and raiders from the same mint run. He won't guess further aloud.
- They work out the raid. She will be in shape, so he must climb the well shaft. For a night, he will hold every page.
- He admits he is afraid of himself with that book. She admits she wants her own name more than she wants to be good.
- Both adults restate the choice they have made twice: she is 27, he is 32, and they married by contract and again by vow.
- She chooses to skip resin the night before the raid so she can be human with him, and the ache counts that night against her.

**Reader question out:** What will they promise each other before one of them holds forty lives?

**Techniques:** 10, 11, 15

## Chapter 12: The Bargain in the Dark

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Make a promise that protects each of them from the other's power.
- **Value shift:** trust given -> trust with a check written into it

**Beats:**
- In the warden's chamber the shutters are barred and her nails itch with the coming ache. She asks him to stay with her through dusk as a woman.
- Explicit scene, open door. She leads, they check in with each other, and the pebble lies unused on the sill because she took no resin.
- After, under the dark rule, they make the bargain: whoever holds the ledger, if they begin to read a page at night for any reason, the other takes it from their hand, and they let go.
- He asks for one of her eleven contracts, as she offered in book 1. She tells him the first, a port enforcer's man, plainly.
- He chooses not to ask for the second: "After the book."
- She lies awake counting doors, knowing ten contracts remain to tell him and not knowing which one he will hate.

**Reader question out:** Which of her ten untold contracts will break him?

**Techniques:** 13, 10, 14

## Chapter 13: The Wood Road

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Get into position for the raid.
- **Value shift:** watched in secret -> invited to watch

**Beats:**
- They ride with eight of Bhaskaran's men in a break in the rain. Muthu holds Kettamala, and Rema is in it.
- They plan the wound-share as a battle problem. Any spear in her opens on him, so he must stay back with the lamp men and come in only for the shaft. He hates it and agrees.
- At the dusk camp she smears resin and turns in front of him on purpose. It is the first turning he has been invited to see.
- In shape she circles the camp. He talks to her quietly about Asha's parrots and Kannan's goat, because talk in the dark is safe between them now.
- His wound from Vellan's old cut aches in the damp, and she lies against his side until it eases.
- She leaves the firelight for the Resin House wall, and he cannot follow until she calls.

## Chapter 14: The Shuttered Well

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Clear the way to the book for Devan.
- **Value shift:** unseen -> found

**Beats:**

- A hunter's lamp swings toward her. She knocks him down without killing him.

## Chapter 15: Bear and Lamp

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** Get the book out, and get Neela out alive.
- **Value shift:** a raid -> a guild without a master

**Beats:**
- In the shaft by lamplight, Devan feels claw welts open across his back. Neela is fighting the bear above him.
- He prises the box open with a pry bar and climbs into the yard with the black book.
- Bhaskaran's men come in with lamps and spears as planned. The lamed bear faces light and blades and gives ground. Devan doesn't strike first. He strikes once, to the shoulder, when it rears over her.
- On the cook-house step, Chathu sees the book in Devan's hand. He runs for the dark wood with Vellan and the hunters, because he knows the members follow whoever holds it.
- The forty, still in shape, gather at the yard's edge and do not attack. Before first light they go into the tapping sheds to turn back unseen, as they were trained.
- They come out dressed and look at the book. In front of all of them, Devan hands it to Neela.

**Reader question out:** What will Neela find when she opens it?

**Techniques:** 6, 16, 22

## Chapter 16: Lookout: Seventeen

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Find Asha's contract for Devan and read it honestly, as she promised.
- **Value shift:** a wife he trusts -> the lookout at his sister's death

**Beats:**
- The forty bow when she takes the book. She stands among them unable to tell whether she is their sister or their owner.
- In Chathu's room by daylight she opens the contract quire. It is in guild cipher, which Devan can't read, so she reads aloud as promised.
- Release is proven a lie. "Kirri, released" is cross-referenced to kennel nine, and eleven more "released" members lead to cages. Vellan's entries run to several leaves, and she turns past them.
- Asha's contract reads: "Kettamala, the warden's sister, the west stair. Buyer, the Keeper. Hand, Thirty. Lookout, Seventeen." She reads every word, and learns which fort at the same moment he does.
- The parrot newel, the hollow eleventh step and her wide path round the stair all reread. She held the lamp at the foot of his sister's death.
- The last quire is stitched in green thread under the queen's sunbird wax. Devan says to leave it whole, because an unbroken seal is evidence. He sets the contract book down carefully, says "I need to walk", and goes into the rain without his cloak.

**Reader question out:** Can their marriage survive a crime neither of them chose?

**Techniques:** 17, 3, 12

## Chapter 17: The Broken Beak

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** Decide what to do without lying to her or to the court.
- **Value shift:** ease -> care without ease

**Beats:**
- In the Resin House training yard in rain, Devan runs the drill he uses to master his own hands.
- He remembers the killing point he struck at 24 and the blow he struck the bear last night, and knows he could strike again.
- He hears Asha's note in his head: say it anyway. The contract he wanted for the court names his wife as party to the murder.
- Neela comes out. She doesn't apologise for the guild. She asks what he needs. He says "I won't answer that" to whether he can look at her, then: "I'm not leaving. I need a day."
- At dusk he puts the pebble in his cheek, though she took no resin, and she sees it.
- He sleeps across the master's-room door with his back to the wall. At dawn he asks for the second contract, and the third. He wants all eleven before he decides anything.

**Reader question out:** What will he do once he knows all eleven?

**Techniques:** 15, 9, 25

## Chapter 18: Forty Names

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Keep the forty safe, fed and guarded.
- **Value shift:** a rogue -> a master in all but name

**Beats:**
- She tells Devan the remaining contracts through the morning, flatly. Then she argues her belief: she was 21, blindfolded, holding a lamp, and Chathu chose and Vellan struck, so it isn't hers. He says, "I know. She's still dead."
- She learns every member's name. Most know only their numbers, and their intake rolls, with their birth names, are fastened to the pages in her hands.
- She has reasons to wait on handing the pages back. Rogues are hunted. Rema sits a day's ride away. Chathu has hunters in the wood, and the groves need guarding. She decides to hold the pages "until it's safe" and to vouch for the forty as the House.
- They obey her at once. The groves are guarded, the kennels cleaned, Kirri washed. It is easy.
- Before riding back to the fort, Devan says gently that Chathu thought the ledger was mercy too. It lands.
- Thilak, 41, brings her the master's tea and calls her "Master" without irony. She doesn't correct him.

**Reader question out:** How long can she hold forty lives before she becomes Chathu?

**Techniques:** 14, 15, 13

## Chapter 19: Assize Weather

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** Keep the garrison loyal and the court satisfied while his wife holds the guild.
- **Value shift:** apart by choice -> riding to her before he's ready

**Beats:**
- Rema asks where the warden rides. Devan answers truly but not fully. She warns that the court won't accept "I won't answer that" for ever.
- Kandan's faction refuses a patrol order. Devan answers them in the yard, openly: "If I'm wrong, the court will say so. Until then, walk." He doesn't strike anyone, and he names the cost.
- Muthu ran the fort honestly in his absence, and Devan returns the post-key.
- Eravi asks leave to ride up the wood road for medicines from "a tapper I know". Devan grants it absently.
- At the assize table that night, a shallow welt opens across Devan's ribs. Neela has been hurt at the House.
- He rides at once, in the dark, alone.

**Reader question out:** What has happened to Neela?

**Techniques:** 7, 16, 14

## Chapter 20: Nellimala

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Defend the groves and answer Chathu's offer.
- **Value shift:** a number with no past -> a name and a village burned by agreement

**Beats:**
- In the grove at dawn a hunter's arrow grazes her ribs. Devan arrives mud-soaked and cleans the wound. Neither speaks of Asha.
- At the gate sits a tin of warm jaggery-sesame sweets. Under the sweets are her intake slip and Chathu's note: return the book and come home.
- The slip reads: "Indu, of Nellimala, coast side of the Monkey Pass. Village burned the second harvest. Sold at six years." The goat path and the lantana make sense: she was born below Kettamala.
- Using the cipher key from the ledger, she reads Ittan's tally book properly at last. There are two columns: his extra passes, and sunbird-marked "allowed" passes. "Nellimala, second harvest" stands in the allowed column, twenty-one years back.
- They understand together: the crown permits raids, and her village was burned by agreement.
- She asks Devan to say her birth name aloud in daylight, and he does. She says she will keep both names, and tips the sweets into the fire.

**Reader question out:** What can Devan prove now that the crown itself is in the tally book?

**Techniques:** 3, 9, 20

## Chapter 21: The Sunbird Column

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** Rebuild the court case around what he can prove.
- **Value shift:** a case built on grief -> a case built on evidence, his wife's name included

**Beats:**
- Devan works out the pattern. The queen allows raids. Ittan sold extras on top. Asha found an extra, so Ittan bought her death. When Devan asked for the seal registers, the queen bought his.
- He can prove one conspiracy in Rema's court: Ittan's tokens and Asha's murder. The treaty and the queen's writ need the high court in full session.
- He asks Neela to help him write the case in plain words. She asks him to help her guard the groves with Bhaskaran's men. Each asks for help where they are weak.
- He tells her his decision before he acts on it: the contract goes to the court whole, lookout line included. He will not strike out her number.
- She says she won't ask him to. She doesn't yet say she will stand beside it.
- She asks to ride back to Kettamala with him, because there is someone she owes first.

**Reader question out:** What does Neela owe, and to whom?

**Techniques:** 15, 10, 16

## Chapter 22: The Hollow Step

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Ready herself to tell Janu.
- **Value shift:** a gap in memory -> a memory she must speak

**Beats:**
- She brings the ledger to Kettamala in her saddlebag and keeps it on her. Janu overfeeds her, and she can't eat.
- Eravi dresses her graze. His satchel reeks of nightbark. He asks how many members the House holds, and on which day she will read their pages back. She answers neither and files the question.
- Alone on the west stair at dusk, without resin, she counts forty-four steps, and the eleventh rings. The memory comes: the blindfold lifted at the stair foot, a lamp in her hand, a parrot with a broken beak, a woman's voice above asking who was there.
- She remembers holding the lamp steady because she was told to. She remembers saying nothing.
- Devan finds her there. He says he'll stand where she can see him, and won't speak for her.
- Janu passes with a lamp and stops ten paces from the stair foot, as she always does. Neela calls her name.

**Reader question out:** What will Janu do when she hears it?

**Techniques:** 11, 2, 9

## Chapter 23: Ten Paces

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Confess to Janu without excuse.
- **Value shift:** "not mine to answer for" -> answering for it

**Beats:**
- At the stair foot Neela tells Janu she held the lamp the night Asha died. She doesn't say "the guild made me" until Janu asks why.
- Janu strikes the newel, not Neela. Then she confesses her own guilt: she was asleep at the kitchen fire.
- Janu asks one thing: was Asha afraid. Neela answers truthfully that Asha asked who was there, and that Neela, holding the light, did not answer.
- Janu does not forgive her. She says "Come and eat", because that is what Janu does.
- Neela tells Devan the drain grate under the eleventh step is new iron. Gopan says the old postern under it was kept locked.
- Gopan says, "Only the physician keeps that key."

**Reader question out:** Did the fort's own physician let Asha's killer in?

**Techniques:** 24, 20, 12

## Chapter 24: The Physician's Key

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** Catch the inside informant.
- **Value shift:** a fort with a hidden enemy -> the enemy named, and the House exposed

**Beats:**
- Devan waits at the drain postern at night. Eravi passes a salve jar through the grate to a runner from the pass village, who flees.
- Eravi confesses. For twenty years he has bought Resin House medicines for the fort and paid in news. Six years ago he unlocked this postern for "a guild man". He passed on the cart order he overheard outside the sickroom, the position of the sword, and drained the stair lamps.
- Tonight's jar told Chathu that Neela is at the fort, the House has no master in it, and the forty think she is just another one.
- Devan apologises to Muthu in front of Bhaskaran, and Muthu takes it.
- In the warden's chamber, second explicit scene. Devan says, "I choose you knowing." She says, "Don't forgive me yet." He says he won't. He'll choose. The terms change: forgiveness is no longer the price of staying.
- At first grey a rider comes off the wood road. The Resin House gate is shut against Bhaskaran's lads, and Chathu's hunters are on the walls.

**Reader question out:** What has Chathu done with the forty?

**Techniques:** 12, 14, 9

## Chapter 25: The Gate Shut

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Retake the House and the forty.
- **Value shift:** a master -> a master deserted

**Beats:**
- They ride the wood road. Devan leaves the assize table with Rema watching, and the court notes his absence.
- At the Resin House the forty stand on the walls beside Chathu's hunters. Thilak shouts down: "You kept the book. So did he. He says he'll burn ours."

## Chapter 26: Human at Dusk

- **POV:** Devan
- **Goal:** Keep Neela alive through the night.
- **Value shift:** a couple with a plan -> two people and forty pages against a hunting wood

**Beats:**
- On the slope above the House, Neela has no resin. Her flask is in the House stores with everyone's ration. She will be human at night against forty animals.
- She says it aloud: she has become Chathu. Devan doesn't argue. He says what she did next is still hers.
- She tears Vellan's page from the ledger and gives it to Devan. If Vellan comes tonight, the choice should belong to the man with most right to it, and she trusts him not to read it.
- At dusk the forced turnings begin in the yard. The cries carry, and Devan names it for the cruelty it is.
- He takes the lamp and the pages' weight onto his own pack so she can run.
- Forty animals pour out of the gate into the wood. Every bite on her will open on him.

## Chapter 27: Before Dawn

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Survive the night without caging anyone.
- **Value shift:** hunted by her own -> the hunt broken without a single caging

**Beats:**
- Forced-turned members hunt them through the wood. She holds forty pages and a lamp and reads none. Devan carries her when she falls, and both bleed from the same bites.
- When she calls members by their names, not their numbers, some break off.
- Near dawn Vellan, in bear shape, brings her down at the blackbark tree where Ammu died. Devan stands over them with Vellan's page open and the lamp lit. One sentence would cage his sister's killer.
- She keeps the bargain. She reaches up and takes the page from his hand, and he lets go.
- Instead she reads Vellan a line from the contract book, which is ordinary ink and no page: "Thirty. Release not to be granted. Sell to the crown when spent." The bear stops.
- At first light Vellan turns back in front of them, so Devan, against his will, becomes his witness. The members slink back to the House sheds to turn unseen.

## Chapter 28: Daylight Reading

- **POV:** Neela
- **Goal:** Make herself no one's master.
- **Value shift:** holder of the ledger -> holder of nothing but her own name

**Beats:**
- Muthu rides in with thirty of the garrison, against Rema's written order that no soldier aid a rogue, and burns his unsent report in the gate fire. Chathu's hunters lay down their poles, and Pachan opens the gate.
- In the yard by daylight, Neela first tells the forty the cost: every listener becomes their witness. She asks them to choose. Then she reads every page aloud in full, Vellan's too.
- Each member takes their own page. Some keep their intake rolls and hear their birth names for the first time. Thilak burns his, and his village with it.
- At kennel nine she reads Kirri's page and lays it in the straw by the anklet. Kirri stays a civet, and that stays lost.
- Devan holds no page now, only a witness's word. He gives Vellan his back and lets him go, and Vellan walks into the wood.
- Chathu, with an empty ledger, is bound for the Justice. Pachan stays to keep the kennels and the groves. The forty ride with one season's flasks. Neela keeps only the sealed green quire and the contract books for Devan's court.

## Chapter 32: Sold to the Crown

- **POV:** Neela

- Neela says "Indu" to herself, then "Neela". Devan says they will need a bigger hall. Thirty more pages are held by the one master they cannot reach.

## Reader-complaint pass (fixed)

- **"Why not hand the pages back at once?"** Neela gives concrete reasons in ch 18: rogues are hunted, Chathu is in the wood, the groves need guarding. Devan warns her, and the delay costs her the forty in ch 25. It is a flaw with a price, not a plot hold.
- **"How did anyone cross a closed pass?"** Rema crossed by the known goat path in daylight, with ropes the escort fixed by night. The night travel is the clue.
- **"Is Devan's withdrawal cruel?"** It is a day, stated honestly. His care continues (the pebble, the door), and he never punishes her.
- **"Is the informant twist fair?"** Eravi is planted in book 1 and in ch 3, 19 and 22, and the drain grate in ch 10. Muthu is the prediction-bait suspect and is cleared with an apology.
- **"Romance buried by plot."** Every chapter with both leads changes the relationship for a new reason. The three explicit scenes each rewrite their terms.
- **"Heroine only reveals, never changes."** Her stated belief is argued in ch 18, cracked in ch 23, and overturned by her choices in ch 28 and 29.
- **"Losses undone."** Kettamala is never returned. Kirri stays caged. Thilak's burned roll stays burned.
