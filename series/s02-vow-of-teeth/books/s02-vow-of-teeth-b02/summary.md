# Oath of Claws — summary

## Plot

**Act 1 (ch 1–5).** Nine days into the monsoon, Neela (27, a shapeshifting leopard odiyan) and Devan (32, warden of Kettamala) are married by contract and by vow. They agree to raid the Resin House together for the guild ledger. He needs Asha's murder contract as court evidence. She wants every page returned to its owner. Neela has two smears of resin left. Langurs go silent on the closed pass road (ch 1). Justice Rema arrives over the goat path with four numbered, sashed escort whose packs are dry (ch 2). She grants a three-day assize, then moves Ittan's execution to dawn (ch 4). Ittan's only request is for his wife's medicine, and he says "Ask your captain what he brought me." Devan objects formally but will not make his sixty soldiers traitors, and Ittan dies (ch 5). The First Guard sees Neela's split nails and says "It passes." Rema keeps a second sealed writ case.

**Act 2a (ch 6–15).** They agree on a first term: contract books go to the court, pages go to their owners. Neela steals fresh resin and finds the ledger in the well-house shaft (ch 7). Rema thanks Muthu for six years of letters. Muthu confesses in the pit that he wrote to the queen's household "to keep the warden safe." He denies ever writing to the guild, and Devan keeps him as captain (ch 8–9). Neela wins the barracks by learning every man's name, using Asha's method. She notices the hollow eleventh step and the newer drain grate (ch 10). The leads bargain in bed: whoever begins reading a page at night is stopped by the other (ch 12). In the raid, Neela fights Vellan's bear above the shaft and Devan takes the book. Chathu flees, and Devan hands the book to Neela before the forty (ch 15).

**Midpoint (ch 16).** Neela reads the contract aloud: "Kettamala, the warden's daughter, the west stair. Buyer, the Keeper. Hand, Thirty. Lookout, Seventeen." She held the lamp at Asha's death. Devan leaves the green-sealed quire whole and walks out into the rain.

**Act 2b (ch 17–24).** Devan stays but withdraws his ease, and asks for all eleven contracts. Neela holds the forty's pages "until it's safe" and accepts the title "Master" (ch 18). Chathu's note and her intake slip reveal her birth name, Indu of Nellimala. Ittan's tally book shows sunbird-marked "by leave" raids, including her village's burning, so the crown permits raids (ch 20–21). Neela remembers Asha calling "Who's there?" and confesses to Janu without excuse (ch 22–23). The physician Eravi had unlocked the postern for the guild and passed on news. Devan holds him and apologises to Muthu (ch 24).

**Act 3 (ch 25–32).** Chathu retakes the House because Neela delayed reading the pages (ch 25). On the hill, Neela is human without resin and gives Vellan's page to Devan. Forty forced-turned animals hunt them (ch 26–27). Beneath Ammu's tree, Devan begins to read Vellan's page, and Neela takes it from his hand and he lets go. She reads Vellan his plain-ink line instead: "Release not to be granted. Sell to the crown when spent." Nobody is caged (ch 27). Muthu brings thirty soldiers against Rema's order, and Pachan opens the gate. By daylight Neela reads every page aloud, and Vellan walks free (ch 28). At the assize, Chathu names the Keeper as buyer. Neela reads the lookout line unasked and swears she is an odiyan. Rema records Asha's name for the high court (ch 29). The queen's pre-dated writ strips Devan of Kettamala. Both declare for each other publicly, and Muthu takes the seal (ch 30). The forty ride into the hills. They break the queen's seal on the last quire, headed "Sold to the crown": thirty numbers (ch 32).

## Romance

Devan and Neela are married, committed, and now partners. Their intimate scenes are consensual, with check-ins. In ch 12 they swear a mutual veto: if one begins reading a page at night, the other takes it, and no reason is given. It holds under pressure in ch 27. In ch 24 Devan says "I choose you knowing" and refuses to forgive on demand. Neela says "Don't forgive me yet," so staying is not the price of forgiveness. In ch 31 they set new terms: no warden and no bride, equals, each at the other's back, and each tells the other before acting.

Disclosures: Neela has told him all eleven contracts, her birth name, and her lookout role. He has told her everything, including the withheld writ. Devan keeps the writ from the court. They did not discuss Eravi's questions to her until ch 24.

Promises: the veto bargain; "after the book," for the untold contracts, now fulfilled; both bound to the forty's vote (ch 32). The wound-share continues: her injuries welt on him. Devan keeps a pebble in his cheek as a brake. Each counts the other's breathing, and each has a hitch.

## Character states

- **Neela/Indu:** She keeps both names and is hunted by the crown and the guild. She has no resin of her own and has gone without it. She has the ache coming. She holds no pages, only the contract books, the stubbed back half of the ledger, and the green quire. She is the lookout, publicly confessed. Her belief has changed: she owes repair even for what she was made to do.
- **Devan:** Stripped of Kettamala and his office. He carries the quire, the chit, the sunbird writ and the gift seal. He has welts and a bitten thigh. He did not strike first. He witnessed Vellan's turning, so Vellan holds a night-shape claim over him.
- **Vellan:** Free, with his own page, lame, and "nobody's." He arrives at the shelter without resin and confirms the queen's night-guard are odiyans. Kamalakshi accepts his carved bird.
- **Chathu:** Bound for Rema, gagged at dusk. His ledger is emptied.
- **Muthu:** Holds the pass seal for the border, not for the household. He burned his report and is loyal.
- **Eravi:** Confessed and held at Kettamala. He wrote an account for Rema. He had passed on the sword position, the cart order, and drained the stair lamps.
- **Rema:** Carries the finding south. She warns that the queen remembers every road.
- **Pachan:** Keeps the groves and kennels.
- **The forty (Pavi, Thilak, Kuttu, Twenty-two/Kamalakshi, Madhavi):** Each holds their own page. Thilak burned his.
- **Janu:** Has not forgiven Neela but fed her.
- **Kandan:** Saluted Devan.

## World changes

- The Resin House is dissolved. The groves are leaderless and unrationed.
- The crown is shown to permit raids, and the queen sells odiyans.
- The Kettamala pass has a new captain.
- The high court holds Asha's conspiracy finding.
- Forced turning is named as cruelty.
- The queen's stripping writ shows she acts through law.

## Open threads

- **C20:** The First Guard's "It passes" and her pared seam. She is one of the queen's thirty night-guard, and the escort crossed the shut pass. Freeing them is book 2.
- **C21 (series):** Devan withholds the sunbird payment writ for the full high court. The court will not sit for a stripped warden, so it is evidence against the queen.
- **C22 (series):** The forty ride with one season's resin, nine knives and Pachan's grove map. The groves stay behind. Who supplies resin next season is unanswered.
- **Hinge:** Thirty pages sit in Kodiyur under the queen's hand. Neela will read them back by daylight but is no one's master.
- The folded-wing bird pressed into the green thread of the quire, and the same bird beside the thirty stubs. Someone other than the queen, or the same hand on another day, marked them.
- The watcher's log reaches the household by the new moon, and the shelter's trail follows.
- Eravi's unnamed "tapper" supplier and the pass-village runner are still unidentified.
- The queen's thirty include a paring surgeon and a palace chain.
- Parukutty's milled coin was never explicitly tied to the mint.
- Kirri stays a civet, a permanent loss.
- Who "the Keeper" answered to is unresolved, because Ittan is dead.
- Janu's forgiveness is unresolved.
