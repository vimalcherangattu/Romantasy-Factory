# Oath of Claws — summary

## Plot

**Act 1 (ch 1–5).** Rains close the Monkey Pass. Neela (27, leopard-shifter odiyan, ex-guild assassin) and Devan (32, warden of Kettamala) agree to raid the Resin House for the guild ledger. He needs Asha's contract as court evidence. She wants every page returned to its owner. Ch 2: Justice Rema arrives with four numbered "escort" who crossed the shut pass, and holds an assize. Ch 4: Ittan says "Ask your captain what he brought me." Ch 5: Rema executes Ittan early. The First Guard tells Neela "It passes." Rema sets a second sealed case on the table.

**Act 2a (ch 6–15).** They agree a first term: contract books to the court, pages to their owners. Ch 7: Neela taps nightbark, finds the book in the well house, and Pachan spares her. Ch 8–9: Rema thanks Muthu for six years of letters, and Muthu confesses to Devan in the pit. Ch 10: the west stair, with its new drain grate, unsettles Neela. Ch 12: they make the night-bargain: whoever holds the ledger and begins to read a page at night is stopped by the other. Ch 14–15: Neela fights the bear (Vellan) while Devan climbs the shaft and takes the book. Chathu flees, and Devan hands the book to Neela before the forty.

**Midpoint (ch 16).** Neela reads Asha's contract aloud: "Hand, Thirty. Lookout, Seventeen." She held the lamp. The "released" members are in kennel cages. A green-stitched quire under the queen's wax stays whole.

**Act 2b (ch 17–24).** Devan withdraws but stays, sleeping across her door. Ch 18: Neela holds the forty's pages "until it's safe" and accepts the title "Master." Ch 20: Chathu's slip gives her birth name, Indu of Nellimala. Ittan's tally shows her village burned "by leave" under a sunbird mark. Ch 21: Devan resolves to submit the lookout line whole. Ch 22–23: Neela remembers the lamp and confesses to Janu. The stair grate points to the physician's key. Ch 24: Eravi confesses and Devan apologises to Muthu. The couple reconcile. The Resin House gate is shut against them.

**Act 3 (ch 25–32).** Ch 25–27: Chathu holds the groves hostage and forces resin on the forty. Neela has no resin. She gives Devan Vellan's page. In the wood she keeps the bargain: she takes the page from his hand and reads Vellan his ledger line, so no one is caged. Ch 28: Muthu's thirty and Pachan open the gate. Neela reads every page aloud by daylight and Vellan walks free. Ch 29: At the assize Chathu names the Keeper as buyer. Neela reads her own lookout line and admits she is an odiyan. Ch 30: Rema opens the queen's writ and Devan is stripped of Kettamala. Ch 31: Janu finishes her leopard story. Ch 32: In the hills they open the green quire, "Sold to the crown," thirty numbers. Vellan arrives and reveals the queen's night-guard are odiyans.

## Romance

Neela and Devan end as partners. They are married by contract and by vow, and he has declared publicly at the cost of his office. Key moments:
- Ch 5: the sparring round.
- Ch 12: the first bed scene and the mutual-veto bargain.
- Ch 16–17: the midpoint rupture and his withdrawal.
- Ch 24: "I choose you knowing." She says "Don't forgive me yet" and he says he won't, he'll choose.
- Ch 27: she takes the page from his hand.
- Ch 30: the public declarations.
- Ch 31: new terms, with no warden and no bride, partners who lead together and stand at each other's back. He counts her breath and finds her hitch.

Disclosed: he knows all eleven contracts (the first told ch 12, the rest ch 17–18), the lookout role, and her names Indu and Neela. She knows about his killing at 24 and Muthu's letters. Janu has not forgiven her.

Binding promises:
- The night-bargain: if either begins to read a page at night, the other takes it.
- Wound-share: her wounds appear on him.
- No lies, but "I won't answer that" is allowed.
- At ch 32 she offers a vote of the forty, and if they vote her out he goes too.

## Character states

- **Neela/Indu/Seventeen:** She has no resin and gave up her flask, so the ache is coming. She is publicly a confessed odiyan, unvouched, and she holds no pages. She keeps the contract books, the emptied ledger back half and the green quire. Claw and arrow grazes and a calf bite are healing. She keeps both names and is no one's master.
- **Devan:** He is stripped of Kettamala and the seal, and his thigh bite is closing. He carries the chit, the sunbird payment writ and the cut seal. He has sworn never to strike first and is Vellan's witness against his will.
- **Muthu:** He now holds the pass seal "for the border, not the household," and burned his last letter.
- **Rema:** She has recorded the finding, will not write where they went, and warns the queen will remember.
- **Chathu:** He is bound for the Justice with an empty ledger and is gagged at night.
- **Vellan:** He has his own page, no master, and is free. Kamalakshi (Twenty-two) holds his carved bird.
- **Kirri:** Permanently a civet, with her page in the straw.
- **Thilak:** He burned his page.
- **Pachan:** He keeps the groves and kennels.
- **Pavi:** She is an otter-shifter among the forty.
- **Eravi:** He confessed and is held.
- **Janu:** She knows the truth and has not forgiven Neela.

## World changes

- The guild's ledger is dismantled, and its members hold their own pages.
- The Resin House has no master. Groves are tended by Pachan, with no rationing.
- Devan is no longer warden, and Muthu holds the seal.
- A crown record names Asha's murder and the pass sales as one conspiracy.
- The queen's raid-permission system ("by leave") is exposed in Ittan's tally book but not yet before the high court.
- The queen's night-guard are secretly odiyans. A palace surgeon pares their wrist scars and their throats are painted clean.

## Open threads

- **C20:** The First Guard's "It passes." She is one of the queen's thirty who has survived the ache. Her identity and stake are for book 2.
- **C21:** Devan withholds the sunbird payment writ for the full high court. It proves the queen paid for his death. No court now exists to hear it.
- **C22:** The forty ride with one season's resin and the groves stay behind with Pachan. Who supplies resin after the season is open.
- The queen holds thirty pages and witnessed each first turning. The stubs in the ledger match the "Sold to the crown" list.
- Vellan says the watcher's scent-post log reaches the queen's household, so the trail can be followed after the new moon.
- Rema's warning: the queen will remember where they go.
- Eravi's Resin House contact and the pass-village runner both know Neela is at large.
- Chathu's fate at the high court is unknown.
- The hinge: can they free the queen's own night-guard, and what will it cost Varanad?
