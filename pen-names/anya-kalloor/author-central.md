1. Sign in to author.amazon.com with the KDP account.
2. Claim the first live book: search the title, choose the book, confirm "Anya Kalloor" as the author.
   The author page appears once Amazon approves the claim (usually a few days).
3. Biography: paste `bio_long` below.
4. Author photo: upload `author-image.png` (a monogram; no invented face).
5. For each new book, claim it under this author so it joins the page.

## Biography (Author Central)

Anya Kalloor writes dark romantasy drawn from the folklore of Kerala, on India's southwest coast. Her first series, The Odiyan Contracts, begins with Vow of Teeth. It follows the odiyan, Malabar's shapeshifting assassins, who use a secret preparation to become animals by night and kill for a price.

Expect enemies to lovers built on opposing values, forced proximity, forbidden love, a morally grey hero and a "touch her and die" protector. The burn is slow and the first kiss is costly. The open-door scenes change the story as much as the relationship. The darkness has edges but is never grimdark. Losses are real and stay lost, the hero's danger points outward, and each book answers its central question while leaving one door open to the next.

The kingdoms are invented, built on real regional history and the stories Kerala tells about itself. The heroines are desi, capable and never decorative. The setting is a world with its own rules, not a backdrop.

These books are written with AI assistance and edited by the publisher.

If you want assassins, dangerous bargains and a love that costs something, start with Vow of Teeth, the first of The Odiyan Contracts. Oath of Claws and Crown of Night follow.
