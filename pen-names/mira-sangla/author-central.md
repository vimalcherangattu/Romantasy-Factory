# Author Central kit: Mira Sangla

Set up at author.amazon.com in the owner's browser (Claude in Chrome or the desktop app). Claude
fills the forms; the owner signs in. One Author Central account can manage several pen names.

## Steps

1. Sign in to author.amazon.com with the KDP account.
2. Claim the first live book: search the title, choose the book, confirm "Mira Sangla" as the author.
   The author page appears once Amazon approves the claim (usually a few days).
3. Biography: paste `bio_long` below.
4. Author photo: upload `author-image.png` (a monogram; no invented face).
5. For each new book, claim it under this author so it joins the page.
6. Later: add the website and newsletter link once they're live (docs/reader-platform.md).

## Biography (Author Central)

Mira Sangla writes desi romantasy for readers who want the danger up front and the slow burn earned. Her series, The Winter Passes, is set in invented mountain kingdoms built on the history and folklore of the Himalaya: villages ruled by gods who hold court through a human oracle who cannot lie, mountain spirits who bargain for what they want, and passes that close for winter and trap two people who should never share a roof.

Expect enemies to lovers rooted in clashing values, forced proximity, political marriage, forbidden love, and morally grey heroes with a touch-her-and-die streak, whose danger points at their enemies and never at the woman they love. First kisses cost something. The love scenes are open door, and each one changes the story. Every book gives its couple a happily ever after or a happy for now, while a larger question carries you into the next.

These books are written with AI assistance and edited by the publisher. Mira Sangla is a pen name, a brand built to deliver what the shelf promises: high stakes, sharp banter and heat that matters.

Ready to cross the pass? Start with Bound till the Thaw, book one of The Winter Passes.
