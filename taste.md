# Owner taste file

S1 to S3 must follow this file. Edit it before `factory new-series`.

## Heat level

Open door, moderate: 2 to 3 explicit scenes per book, each one changing the
relationship or the plot. All characters adults (see `canon/content-rules.md`).

## Darkness

Dark edges, not grimdark. Real losses that stay lost. No sexual violence on the
page. Danger from the love interest points outward or is disciplined.

## Tropes to use

- Enemies to lovers rooted in values, not rudeness
- Forced proximity
- Forbidden love
- Slow burn with a costed first kiss

## Tropes to avoid

- Love triangles
- Fake dating
- Miscommunication that one honest conversation would fix
- "Not like other girls"

## Engines preferred

Court (information and power gaps) or rule (one costly metaphysical constraint).

## Comps (describe qualities, never imitate prose)

Readers who want a lethal setting, a competent underestimated heroine, a
dangerous love interest whose tenderness is selective, and a series hinge at
each book's end.

## Ending

HEA or HFN for each book's couple. The book's central question resolves; only
the series hinge stays open.
