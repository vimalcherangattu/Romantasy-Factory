# Owner taste file

S1 to S3 follow this file. Updated 19 Oct 2026 after the market research (canon/desi-folklore.md).

## Direction

Desi romantasy for the global Kindle Unlimited reader: desi leads and real South Asian folklore
inside a romantasy package US readers already buy. Familiar promise, fresh specifics
(canon/first-look.md). amazon.in readers are the second market.

## Regions to draw from

Kerala (yakshi, odiyan, Theyyam, serpent groves, kalari, tharavad, spice ports, monsoon) and
Himachal (devta courts and oracles, mountain spirits, closed passes). Invented kingdoms built
on real regional history; never real religions or major deities as romance (respect lines in
canon/desi-folklore.md).

## Heat level

Open door, moderate: 2 to 3 explicit scenes per book, each changing the relationship or the
plot. All characters adults (canon/content-rules.md).

## Darkness

Dark edges, not grimdark. Real losses that stay lost. No sexual violence on the page. The love
interest's danger points outward or is disciplined.

## Tropes to use

- Enemies to lovers rooted in values, not rudeness
- Forced proximity; forced or political marriage
- Forbidden love
- Morally grey love interest, "touch her and die"
- Slow burn with a costed first kiss

## Tropes to avoid

- Love triangles, fake dating
- Miscommunication that one honest conversation would fix
- "Not like other girls"
- Generic exotic India: monsoon palaces, spice-and-silk princesses, vague mythic courts

## Must pass at first look

A US romantasy reader understands the title and one-line hook in five seconds: who falls for
whom, why it's dangerous, what's new. English titles.

## Ending

HEA or HFN for each book's couple. The book's central question resolves; only the series hinge
stays open.
