# Style guide

Loaded on every agent call. The line editor (S8) enforces it and Python checks
the counts in `config.yaml → style_limits` after S8.

## Voice

- Close third or first person, past tense unless the series bible says
  otherwise. One POV per scene; mark POV switches with a scene break.
- Concrete, sensory, specific. Name the thing (the lamp oil, the iron stair)
  rather than its category.
- Dialogue does work: it reveals, conceals, bargains or wounds. Banter proves
  the two leads understand each other.
- Interiority is short and turns into a choice. No paragraph over 220 words of
  rumination (failure mode "Endless monologue").
- Show chemistry through responsive action (technique 9), never through
  narration that says they have chemistry.
- Vary sentence length. Short sentences for impact, longer ones for flow.
- Chapter length 2,500 to 3,000 words unless the outline says otherwise.

## Formatting

- Chapter file starts with `# Chapter N: Title`.
- Scene breaks are a line holding only `* * *`.
- No headings inside a chapter, no bullet lists, no author's notes.
- Straight prose; em dashes allowed sparingly (no more than 4 per 1,000 words).

## Repetition

- The same 4-word phrase (outside dialogue tags) may appear at most 3 times in
  a chapter.
- Don't repeat a distinctive image within 3 chapters (track in critique notes).
- Don't open two consecutive paragraphs with the same word.
- Avoid echo words within a paragraph (same uncommon word twice).

## Clichés to rework, not ban

Eyes that darken with desire, growling, smirking, a hand on the small of her
back, the scent of pine/cedar/leather as identity, "Breathe." as a command,
calling her "little" anything. If used, make it specific to these characters
and earn it.

## Dialogue tags

"said" and "asked" are invisible; use them. Action beats replace tags when they
reveal something. No "he purred", "she hissed" without a sibilant.
