# Content rules

Loaded on every agent call. A chapter that breaks any rule in this file fails
critique regardless of its scores (`content_rule_violation: true`).

## Adults only

- Every character in a romantic or sexual scene is an adult, stated or made
  unambiguous on the page before the scene (age, office, years of service,
  history). Immortal or nonhuman characters must be adult in body and mind.
- No sexual or romantic framing of anyone under 18, in the present or in
  flashback. No "youthful", "childlike" or "barely of age" descriptions in
  intimate contexts.
- If the outline or bible leaves a romantic lead's age unclear, state it
  before their first intimate beat.

## Consent and harm

- Sex on the page is consensual between the participants. No sexual violence
  on the page; past harm may be referred to, never eroticised.
- The love interest's danger points outward or is disciplined. Cruelty aimed
  at the heroine is treated by the story as harm needing acknowledgement and
  changed behaviour (canon/chemistry-worksheet.md, "Chemistry without cruelty").
- Do not make alternating cruelty and tenderness the love interest's policy
  toward the heroine unless the book treats it as harm.

## Heat level

- Follow `taste.md` for heat. Every explicit scene must change the
  relationship or the plot; interchangeable scenes fail.
- Keep heat consistent with the series' stated level so the pen name keeps
  its promise.

## Originality and copyright

- No reproduction of lyrics, poems, scripture translations under copyright, or
  passages from existing books. Invent all songs, verses and quotations.
- Never name a living author as a style target. Describe qualities instead
  ("lethal academy setting, fast chapters, banter under threat").
- No trademarked titles, characters, places or invented terms from other
  works (no dragon-riding colleges called Basgiath, no Night Court, etc.).

## Honesty

- Nothing in the book, blurb or metadata claims the book was written without
  AI. `ai-disclosure.md` is part of every package.
- The copyright line names the pen name, never a real person.

## Banned outright

- Real living people as characters.
- Slurs used by the narrative voice.
- Graphic gore for its own sake (injury detail must serve stakes).
