# Chapter checklist

From Appendix A, Part 8. Every chapter must have:

1. **Hook on page one:** an action or a question in the first 150 words.
2. **At least one partial answer** to a question already open.
3. **At least one new question bigger than the one answered** (Answer-and-
   Escalate).
4. **An emotional turn:** a character feels differently at the end than at
   the start, and the reader can name why.
5. **An ending that interrupts an action or reveals something that makes the
   next chapter necessary,** caused by the story, not coincidence.

Plus:

- The techniques the outline tags for this chapter are on the page.
- The chapter stays inside the outline: new plot facts are listed in the
  draft's `new-facts.json` for the critic to judge.
- Length 2,500 to 3,000 words (±20% passes S4).
- If both leads appear, the romance moves.
- The book's main question resolves by the final chapter; only the series
  hinge (technique 26) stays open. A chapter-ending cliffhanger is fine; a
  book-ending one that leaves the book's own question open is not.
