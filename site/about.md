# About

I write romantasy for readers who like their worlds dangerous and their love stories earned:
heroines who change their minds about something that matters, men whose tenderness is rare
and costly, and magic that makes every promise, touch and secret mean something.

Every book is a complete story with its own ending, and every series has a bigger question
waiting at the end of each one.

My books are written with AI assistance and shaped, edited and approved by me before they
reach you. Each Kindle listing says so.
