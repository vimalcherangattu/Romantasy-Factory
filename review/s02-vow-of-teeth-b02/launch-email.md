Hello, reader,

The rains are still falling over Kettamala, and the ledger is waiting.

Oath of Claws, book 2 of The Odiyan Contracts, is out now.

Neela and Devan are married in truth. She is a hunted odiyan with almost no resin left, and he is a warden who has sworn never to say the word that could cage her. Now they must rob the guild that owns every odiyan, and the stakes are not only the danger outside. A ledger can free forty lives, or turn the one who holds it into a master.

If you need a reminder of where book 1 left off: Neela turned in front of the man she was paid to kill and gave him the word herself. That choice bound them, wound for wound, and made her a rogue with a price on her head. Book 2 starts nine days into the rains, with the passes shut and a crown justice at the gate.

This one has a heist, a bargain made in the dark, and a marriage tested by something neither of them chose. It has open-door heat and a hopeful ending, and it sets up the final book.
