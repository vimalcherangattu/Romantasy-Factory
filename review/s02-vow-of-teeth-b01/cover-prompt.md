## Art prompt
Close-cropped, intimate, dangerous book-cover illustration, portrait 1600 x 2560, no text, no lettering, no full face. A woman's hand wearing a plain gold wedding bangle lies flat on a man's bare chest, just below the vertical centre of the frame. Her fingertips are turning into leopard claws, curved and pale, catching warm gold light. The scene is lit from one side as if by a single oil lamp, with deep shadow falling away on the other side. The background is a black field with a faint leopard-rosette pattern, gloss black on matte black, barely visible. Skin tones are warm and natural, cropped so no face shows. Hushed, sensual, the moment before a word is spoken. Painterly photoreal style, shallow depth of field, fine grain. No real person's likeness, no logos, no trademarked imagery.

## Motif
A woman's hand in a plain gold wedding bangle on a man's bare chest, fingertips becoming leopard claws that catch the gold light. It sits just below centre, matching the placement used for every book in the series.

## Palette
