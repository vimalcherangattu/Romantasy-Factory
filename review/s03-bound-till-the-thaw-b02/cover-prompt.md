## Art prompt

A tight-focus, painterly-realistic cover illustration on a cold, wide mountain ground: a brass surveyor's compass with a plain, unmarked dial hangs from a single strand of madder-red hand-spun wool rope, suspended slightly below the centre of the frame over a sheet of old map paper etched in frost, its contour lines and tracery caught in white rime, with no letters, numbers or words anywhere on the map or dial. The red rope is the only warm colour in the image, and it loops up out of frame at the top. Behind the motif, a jagged blue-white ridge line runs across the lower third. The upper third is a pale, cold sky of snow white fading to glacier blue, left clear and empty for the title. The palette is snow white with warm-grey shadows, glacier blue, and deep slate ink, with wool red used only on the rope. Light is low and flat, with a faint glint on the brass. The mood is cold, intimate and high-stakes, like breath held in frozen air. No people, faces or figures, no hands, no religious symbols, prayer flags, monasteries or shrines, no logos, and no text of any kind. Portrait orientation, 1600 × 2560 px.

## Motif

A brass surveyor's compass hanging from the red wool rope over a map etched in frost. Series object rule: one object at the centre, in tight focus, always touched by the same red rope.

## Palette

- Snow white, with a warm-grey shadow
- Glacier blue
- Deep slate ink
- Wool red (madder red), used only on the rope and the title's single accent line

## Type spec

All type is added later, not in the art. Keep the same vertical spacing as every book in the series.

- **Series line:** "The Winter Passes · Book 2", clean geometric sans-serif, small size, in white, centred above the title.
- **Title:** "THE WINTER OATH", tall condensed serif capitals with sharp chiselled wedge serifs, widely letter-spaced, snow white with a faint frost-crack texture. Set in the top third over the pale sky, in two lines ("THE WINTER" / "OATH") so it stays large and readable at thumbnail size.
- **Accent:** a thin wool-red horizontal rule under the title.
- **Pen name:** "MIRA SANGLA", clean geometric sans-serif small capitals, very widely tracked, in white at the bottom edge, over the dark slate ground. It should stay legible at thumbnail size.
